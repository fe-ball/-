# MANDATORY CHEAT / CAPABILITY PREFLIGHT CANON — V8 GTCORE1

> **AIRPOWER5 / GTCORE1 highest capability-audit guard.** Read this before any propulsion, material, aviation-performance, high-speed or future-technology capability audit. This file does not grant a numerical bonus by itself; it prevents both historical-ceiling under-rating and double-counting of the reincarnator/computation advantage.

## 0. Why this guard exists

The v38-v46 rebaseline already rebuilt Asai capability using reincarnator foreknowledge, pre-paid industrial clocks, computation, test/QC infrastructure and organization-level convergence. In v46 this is exactly why the E6-M installed audit moved the old conservative jet anchors from roughly 500 kgf pure-J / 650-700 kgf JF to about 850-900 kgf pure-J / 950-1,000 kgf JF.

Therefore a later audit must **not** take those rebaselined values and then pull them back toward historical early-jet values merely because they look too advanced for the calendar. That is a second, invalid historical-conservatism correction. Conversely, do not apply an extra generic "cheat multiplier" to already-rebaselined values; any further increase must identify a specific previously-unpaid capability or accounting error.

## 1. Reincarnator rule — the idea gate is intentionally very strong

The governing upstream rule is not "history plus a modest head start". It is:

- the protagonist knows the answer and the route sufficiently to open the **idea gate** almost for free;
- a later historical invention may be proposed much earlier when its physical/industrial prerequisites can be assembled;
- methods from another field may be transferred early;
- dead-end search can be greatly reduced because the correct branch and the relevant failure modes are known in advance;
- long clocks can be started years or decades before demand appears;
- once test standards, computation, QC and failure feedback have accumulated, the **organization itself** may converge toward answers more accurately than the protagonist can specify in detail.

The exact coefficient, map, alloy heat, vibration mode, field durability or manufacturing yield can still require physical work. Knowing the class of solution does not create unmeasured coefficients, but it changes what is tested, how early the test clock starts, and how little time is spent on wrong branches.

## 2. Historical dates are anchors, never ceilings

Do not reject or down-rate a result because an analogous historical product appeared later. Historical examples are used to extract invariant physics, loading, losses, failure mechanisms and scale. Their calendar dates are not the Asai worldline's capability ceiling.

Phrases such as "too early", "too advanced for 1939", "miraculous", "magic", or "historically nobody had it yet" are **not engineering gates**. If a claim is to be rejected or reduced, name the actual unpaid gate: material/process, geometry/precision, measurement, test duration, flow map, rotordynamics, cooling, control, endurance, yield, mass/volume, supply, or installation.

## 3. Two gates and the conversion rule

Every capability question must separate:

1. **idea/design gate** — mostly opened by foreknowledge and later by organization-level analysis;
2. **physical/industrial gate** — what can actually be melted, machined, joined, measured, tested, supplied, maintained and reproduced at that date.

A constraint is not a single "ceiling". Decompose it and check whether Asai can convert it into another, more favorable problem. Examples already embedded in the worldline include cooling to reduce strategic-alloy demand, fabricated/hollow parts to bypass casting limits, VIGV/bleed/multi-spool approaches to buy compressor operability, modular overhaul to spend life for performance, and cross-domain NDT/QC transfer.

## 4. Pre-payment rule

When a later program needs a long clock, first ask whether Asai had reason to start that clock earlier. This applies to creep, oxidation, fatigue, corrosion, bearings, combustion, cooling passages, rotor dynamics, process capability, field-return statistics and specialist training.

Do not reset all accumulated infrastructure maturity when a new product appears. Reset only the genuinely new alloy/process/geometry/duty clocks; inherit furnaces, instrumentation, procedures, staff, databases and failure taxonomy where applicable.

## 6. E-generation rule

E1-E9 are **continuous technology-front coordinates**, not engine model numbers and not an invention sequence. A future architecture may be known and investigated in rig form before the generation in which it becomes an integrated, qualifiable product.

Therefore:

- E7's 1939-42 integrated-core schedule does not mean E8/E9-related ideas are absent before then;
- VSV, multi-spool, reheat, fan, advanced cooling and high-PR component work must each be audited on their own start/maturity clock;
- generation, core scale, backend and rating remain separate axes.

## 7. Life/TBO rule

Never use one word "life" for all of the following:

- material/component baseline life;
- representative rated core hot-section life (`L_core`);
- cumulative qualification/endurance hours (`H_qual`);
- product overhaul/inspection interval (`TBO_prod` / OH);
- starts/cycles and accumulated overtemperature/emergency minutes.

Historical W1/Jumo/Welland figures may calibrate meanings and mechanisms but do not automatically replace Asai's already-audited life coordinates.

## 8. Capability is not procurement

The cheat/capability rule applies to what can be researched, designed, tested, qualified and manufactured. It does **not** automatically create a military requirement, funding priority, production quantity, deployment, combat result or post-1943 branch outcome.

Technical availability and historical adoption remain separate gates.
