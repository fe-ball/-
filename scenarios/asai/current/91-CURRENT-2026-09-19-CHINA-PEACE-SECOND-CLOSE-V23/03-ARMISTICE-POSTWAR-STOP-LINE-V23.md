# V23 — GENERAL ARMISTICE / POSTWAR STOP LINE

## Status

**WORKING-CLOSED ARMISTICE STRUCTURE / LONG-RUN POSTWAR OPEN**

This file defines where the second China-peace route closes. It does not canonize a complete postwar history.

## 1. Why an armistice is possible

The 1946 settlement is not based on German victory over the USSR or on Soviet collapse.

It becomes possible because:

- Germany remains expensive to conquer and retains a functioning state/army/industry;
- the USSR has achieved major territorial/security gains but pays heavily for repeated high-quality mobile operations;
- Britain lacks a historical US-backed continental army and has growing concern about a complete central-European vacuum;
- the United States remains a nonbelligerent great power able to serve as guarantor/observer rather than occupation belligerent;
- Japan remains a strong armed-neutral power and can carry limited diplomatic messages to Moscow;
- all principal actors can describe an armistice as a defensive/security success rather than unconditional defeat.

## 2. German concession concept

Germany gives up the external imperial/occupation structure in order to preserve the German state.

Working armistice center:

- withdrawal from France, Belgium, Netherlands and other non-German occupied areas;
- withdrawal from Poland, Czechoslovakia, Hungary and other non-German eastern/southeastern occupation positions;
- end of military support for satellite regimes as a condition of continued occupation;
- Austria re-established/separated;
- Sudeten sovereignty returned to Czechoslovakia;
- East Prussia final sovereignty deferred with a demilitarized/inspection solution during the armistice period.

## 3. German state / armed-force continuity

The armistice does not require immediate comprehensive occupation of Germany.

The German central government remains the effective counterparty.

The Wehrmacht/national armed establishment survives in sharply reduced form.

Exact permanent numbers remain OPEN. The working logic is:

- rapid large-scale demobilization;
- heavy/offensive equipment concentrated and inspected;
- offensive massing near eastern demarcation zones restricted;
- strategic/offensive systems more tightly controlled than local defensive systems;
- final military ceiling negotiated in the peace process.

Do not turn this into either historical total German disarmament or unrestricted German rearmament.

## 4. Air / naval / long-range strike controls

- U-boat attacks cease; operational submarine fleets are concentrated/inspected.
- V-weapon/long-range offensive attacks cease.
- Luftwaffe air defense can survive in limited form while offensive strategic capabilities become subject to restriction.
- Jet technology does not disappear; its production/service scale becomes a later arms-control issue.

## 5. Reparations

No final amount is closed.

The center mechanism favors multi-year in-kind/industrial deliveries over immediate destruction of the German industrial base.

Possible categories include:

- rolling stock;
- machine tools;
- industrial/electrical equipment;
- vehicles;
- chemicals;
- coal/steel and manufactured goods.

This is both a reparations mechanism and a way to avoid creating a central-European economic vacuum.

## 6. Humanitarian / crimes issue

The armistice is not a blanket amnesty.

Immediate obligations include, in working principle:

- POW/detainee registration and return processes;
- forced-laborer release/return;
- neutral/humanitarian access to detention facilities;
- stopping deportation/mass-killing systems;
- preservation of records/evidence.

Final responsibility of senior political/military/SS leadership is not closed at the armistice line.

Do not auto-create a historical Nuremberg process without occupation and custody conditions; do not auto-immunize the German leadership either.

## 7. Political regime

Hitler remains the effective German head of government at the armistice stop line.

The armistice does not formally endorse the Nazi regime as a permanent legitimate settlement.

It also does not require a pre-armistice forced change of German government.

Post-armistice German political reconfiguration, including SS/Party/Wehrmacht/state-bureaucracy relations, is OPEN.

## 8. France

France is not treated as already unified by a historical Normandy/Paris liberation sequence.

Vichy-state institutions, Free French/de Gaulle legitimacy, resistance forces, military networks and constitutional continuity questions require a separate reintegration process.

Do not auto-import the historical 1944–45 French political settlement.

## 9. European security

The stop-line world is not NATO.

Germany and the USSR retain substantial military power.

Britain and the United States have strong incentives to prevent both renewed German expansion and a central-European security vacuum.

Permanent European military architecture is OPEN.

## 10. East Asia

The route stop line does not close:

- Soviet return to Manchuria;
- Japanese nuclear weapons;
- the Chinese Civil War;
- Japanese aid to the National Government;
- Soviet aid/intervention for the CCP;
- a later Japan–USSR or wider war.

These are possible later branches.

## 11. STOP

Do not continue postwar history automatically.

The active frontier now returns to 1939–40.
