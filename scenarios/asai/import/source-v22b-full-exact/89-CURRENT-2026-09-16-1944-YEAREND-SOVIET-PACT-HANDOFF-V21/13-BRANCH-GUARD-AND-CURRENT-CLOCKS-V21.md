## Fact / estimate guard

The package repeatedly distinguishes:
- FACT: objective current-world event/state;
- ESTIMATE: intelligence/analysis available to an actor;
- BELIEF: leader subjective interpretation;
- PLAN: intended action contingent on information and authority;
- PUBLIC-LINE: propaganda/diplomatic statement.

Do not let player/observer knowledge (e.g. exact impact of Far-East division retention on Winter Storm) become Tokyo/Moscow/Berlin knowledge without an intelligence path.
