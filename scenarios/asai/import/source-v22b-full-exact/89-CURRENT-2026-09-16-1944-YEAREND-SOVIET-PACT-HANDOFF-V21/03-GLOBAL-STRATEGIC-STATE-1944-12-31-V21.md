# GLOBAL STRATEGIC STATE — 1944-12-31

## Europe

The USSR is still the strategic offensive power but has not achieved the historical 1944 cascade of Axis political collapses and German military annihilation.

Germany has lost enormous territory and cannot restore the 1942 strategic position, but still has:
- a functioning eastern front;
- a non-terminal mobile reserve;
- Romania and Ploiesti still aligned;
- Italy still Axis;
- no Normandy ground front;
- no historical Italian front;
- no US daylight strategic-bombing system;
- no historical Big Week / Oil Campaign;
- materially healthier rail, aircrew and fuel conditions.

The decisive German weakness is increasingly manpower/cadre depth rather than the immediate absence of tanks or aircraft.

## USSR

The USSR retains overwhelming total manpower and material capacity relative to any single Axis state and continues to move west in the long run.

V21 working cumulative divergence through 1944 year-end:
- extra Soviet irreversible military loss vs historical branch: **~0.8-1.3 million**;
- extra military deaths within that: **~0.5-0.8 million**.

These are analysis bands, not an exact loss ledger.

The more important qualitative divergence is elevated cadre loss among experienced junior leaders, tank crews, artillery observers, engineers, signals and maintenance personnel. `SOV-CADRE-STRAIN = HIGH`; total manpower crisis is not yet reached.

## Western Europe

There is no Allied continental army comparable to historical 1944 Anglo-American forces. Britain cannot execute an Unthinkable-type anti-Soviet land war.

Instead, London studies `WESTERN-ENTRY`: reconstitute a legitimate French state, obtain invited access to French ports, rebuild French ground forces, and if Germany later collapses, move west-to-east far enough to prevent the Red Army from becoming the only occupation power in Germany.

The French problem remains unstable because Vichy contains pro-German factions and Germany still retains punishment capacity. A Lebrun constitutional bridge is a working concept, not an executed political settlement.

## Mediterranean

Britain controls Libya by early 1943 correction. Vichy Tunisia/Algeria/Morocco remain outside an Allied Torch occupation in this branch. No historical Case Anton / Toulon scuttling chain has been closed. Italy remains Axis through current clock.

## United States

The United States is a powerful nonbelligerent. It supplies Britain and the USSR, expands the navy under prewar programs, and runs an Atlantic shadow war but has not formally declared war on Germany.

The next US-Germany war trigger remains maritime escalation, not an automatic ideological decision.

No automatic Japanese belligerency follows a future US-German war; Tripartite interpretation remains a separate political gate.

## East Asia

Japan is the strongest major power not currently fighting a great-power war. Its central strategic problem is no longer short-run scarcity but how to preserve freedom of action as the European balance evolves.
