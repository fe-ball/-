# US / UK / FRANCE / ITALY / VICHY POLITICS — 1944 YEAR-END

## United States

The US remains formally outside the European war through current clock while heavily supporting Britain and the USSR.

`US-GER-WAR = OPEN / RISING`.

Most credible trigger is maritime escalation in the Atlantic. Berlin benefits from avoiding a declaration or repeated deliberate attacks on US naval units; Washington benefits domestically from aid without mass combat losses.

If US-Germany war begins from a European/Atlantic incident, Japanese entry is not automatic. US diplomacy is likely to emphasize that Japan need not treat a German-origin conflict as a Tripartite trigger.

US 1944 election outcome is intentionally **OPEN** in V21 and must be closed before relying on a 1945 inauguration path.

## Britain

Britain increasingly shifts from "can Germany be beaten?" to "who occupies the vacuum if Germany is beaten?"

`SOV-HORIZON = ACTIVE/HIGH`.

No Unthinkable-style anti-Soviet capability exists because there is no US army in Europe and no western continental bridgehead.

Instead Britain emphasizes:
- Greece;
- Turkey/Straits;
- Balkan influence bargaining;
- restoration of a viable French state;
- eventual western presence in western Germany if Germany collapses.

## France / Vichy / Free French

Working political actors:
- Philippe Petain: regime/state preservation and delay.
- Pierre Laval: collaborationist political survival.
- Francois Darlan: fleet/imperial military power broker; survives because no historical Algiers assassination chain.
- Charles de Gaulle: Free French legitimacy / French great-power restoration.
- Albert Lebrun: dormant constitutional continuity tool.

`WESTERN-ENTRY = PLANNING`.

Working Lebrun route:
- Lebrun reasserts republican constitutional continuity;
- Petain returns extraordinary power / retires;
- de Gaulle heads a provisional republican government;
- military integration precedes full political reconciliation;
- Darlan transfers fleet/forces and exits front-line politics;
- Laval and hard collaborationists are excluded.

This is not executed. German counteraction, Vichy pro-German factions, and the lack of a massive British ground army are major constraints.

`FRA-SWITCH-WINDOW`: narrow. Too early -> German punishment. Too late -> Soviet strategic depth / pro-German preemption / lost western-entry opportunity.

## Italy

Mussolini regime survives. No historical Sicily invasion / armistice / German occupation of Italy.

Working political leadership:
- Mussolini remains head of government and after Feb 1943 also holds foreign-affairs direction;
- King Victor Emmanuel III remains a potential constitutional exit actor;
- Vittorio Ambrosio heads the military command in the relevant branch;
- Ciano is removed from the foreign-minister role as historical Feb 1943-type reshuffle remains plausible even without the later July collapse.

Italy wants Germany to stabilize the east and avoid bringing the US into the war. Royal/military exit thinking exists but German punishment capacity remains high enough to deter an early jump.

## North Africa / Mediterranean

Correction closed in V21:
- Britain takes Libya by early 1943 on the Eighth-Army axis;
- no Torch means no western pincer and no historical Tunisia mass-surrender chain;
- Vichy Tunisia/Algeria/Morocco remain outside an Allied occupation unless a later political trigger fires;
