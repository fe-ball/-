## 5. Payload doctrine for rapid transport

Do not treat E5 as a bulk logistics aircraft.

Normal high-value rapid-transport payload band:
- **~200–400 kg**, depending fuel / range / field / weather.

Priority cargo:
- photo plates / developed imagery
- maps / classified orders / cipher material
- radio sets, tubes, precision electrical parts
- bombsight / camera / fire-control components
- engine accessories and high-value spares
- medical / technical specialists
- commander / staff party of roughly 1–3 additional persons where layout permits
- limited special ammunition / test articles

Bulk food, routine ammunition, and ordinary divisional tonnage remain surface-transport work.

## 6. Command-cycle effect

Where E5 command-recon is physically allocated and weather permits:

### Local theater loop
A typical same-day loop can be:
- early-morning takeoff
- target-area photography
- return by late morning
- development / interpretation
- headquarters decision during afternoon

Working analyst effect:
- selected operational reconnaissance information age at headquarters: **~40–60% of conventional comparable cycle**
- probability that a major fixed concentration / route change is still current when the afternoon decision is made: **+15–25% relative**
- no real-time ISR credit

### Inter-hub courier loop
For major prepared airfields:
- Wuhan ↔ Nanjing class movement: same-day practical
- Wuhan ↔ Shanghai class movement: same-day practical
- Wuhan ↔ Guangzhou class movement: same-day but weather / fuel / reserve sensitive

Working analyst effect:
- high-value information / specialist / precision-part transit that would otherwise take ~1–3 days can often be reduced to **same-day / next-half-day** movement.

Do **not** apply this to bulk supply throughput.

## 7. Military-system effect

E5 now provides two distinct military effects:

1. **Battlefield access effect**
   - recon -> strike -> BDA -> retask
   - already covered by the E5 fixed-target coefficients.

2. **Headquarters time-distance effect**
   - faster command reconnaissance
   - rapid movement of imagery, staff, specialists and high-value spares between major air hubs.

For replay, where E5 command / courier allocations exist:
- headquarters information / specialist latency penalty: **reduce by ~20–35%**
- operational decision timing improvement: normally **hours**, occasionally **up to ~half a day**
- no automatic increase to raw troop movement or bulk logistical tonnage.

## 9. 1938-12-31 closure

By year-end 1938, E5 command-recon and rapid transport are no longer occasional demonstrations.

They are:
- a recurring theater-headquarters reconnaissance service;
- a recurring high-value courier / emergency-transport service between prepared hubs;
- still numerically too small to become a general air-transport system.

Use these fixed ledgers when replaying 1939.
