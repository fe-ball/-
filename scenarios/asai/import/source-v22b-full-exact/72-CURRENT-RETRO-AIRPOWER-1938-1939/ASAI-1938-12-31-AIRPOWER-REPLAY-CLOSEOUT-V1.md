## Military effects Nov-Dec 1938

Central China:
- Yueyang is occupied on essentially the historical clock, with the worldline center at late 10 Nov / early 11 Nov.
- E5 repeated photography makes the degree of Chinese withdrawal disorder clearer and supports a somewhat harder pursuit through 13-14 Nov.
- No full Changsha operation is launched.
- Japanese patrol/recon pressure reaches the northern approaches to the Xinqiang River line, but no permanent deep bridgehead is credited.
- matched-history analyst delta for the extended pursuit zone:
  - Chinese additional killed/wounded/captured/scattered: roughly **+600–1,000**
  - scarce transport/heavy-equipment abandonment: **~+5–8%** in the specifically covered withdrawal corridor
  - Japanese additional ground casualties: roughly **+100–200**
- the Chinese still receive a breathing spell and reorganize; E5 interdiction reduces its quality, not its existence.

Air interdiction:
- regularly E5-covered sectors shift to smaller daytime depots, more night movement and dispersal.
- direct large-concentration vulnerability drops, while handling/throughput suffers.
- replay coefficient remains:
  - daylight large-concentration exposure ~-20%
  - dispersion handling/logistics efficiency ~-6%

South China:
- Navy E5 detachments support repeated reconnaissance and fixed-target attack in northern Guangdong and toward Guangxi approaches.
- effect is mainly shorter repair/re-attack cycles against roads, rail, airfields and supply concentrations.
- no deep Guangxi ground advance is credited in 1938.

Political:
- Japanese Nov 3 'New Order' policy and the post-Wuhan/Canton military situation create a stronger peace-pressure environment.
- Wang Jingwei leaves Chongqing on Dec 19 and publicly urges peace negotiations by year-end.
- the worldline military situation marginally improves the plausibility of his argument but does not create a mass elite defection in 1938.

## Year-end interpretation

By 1938-12-31:
- E5 Twin is a real low-rate military production aircraft and supports two persistent operational concentrations plus detachments.
- its strategic value is the recurring recon -> strike -> BDA -> retask cycle and common-airframe mission flexibility.
- Ki-42 pure-J is a proven specialist, while JF has now demonstrated enough improvement to justify an operational block.
- the 1939 replay must treat both as real order-of-battle items rather than scarce experimental exceptions.
