# 1939-08-21 CORRIDOR RACE / LINE-SHORTENING — V8 NOMONHAN-A3

## Historical anchor retained
Historical Soviet pressure on Aug21-22 bends the Japanese southern flank inward while mobile elements move onto likely eastward escape routes. The Japanese center remains capable of hard local resistance, but the historical command continues to think in terms of holding/counterattack too long. Worldline intelligence and the surviving mobile reserve alter the timing, not Soviet scale.

## 0000-0600: night recovery and rear-echelon evacuation
Workshop/recovery parties return several light-damage vehicles. First Tank Group immediate ready rises from ~45 at Aug20 24:00 to **~49** by dawn:
- 3rd Tank Regiment **25**;
- 4th Tank Regiment **24**.

Heavy wounded, vulnerable rear services and excess stores continue moving east before the road is closed.

## Soviet adaptation
The Soviet southern force now knows Japanese tanks and mobile 37 mm guns are present. Aug21 employment therefore becomes more deliberate: reconnaissance, artillery/air preparation, AT-gun deployment and bypasses rather than allowing another clean strike on a marching column.

The Japanese 4th Tank Regiment is therefore not ordered to repeat the Aug20 side attack.

## 4th Tank Regiment: outer screen
4th Regiment becomes an **outer screen**. Forward groups force Soviet columns to deploy, then withdraw before artillery/armor mass can fix them.

Center result:
- Soviet AFV disabled/KO observed/likely: **~5-8**;
- soft vehicles affected: **~8-15**;
- clear additional special-37-mm contribution: **~3-4 AFV-equivalent effects**;
- Japanese 4th Regiment: **3 irreversible +4 repairable**;
- immediate ready at 24:00: **~17**.

The Aug20 surprise geometry is not repeatable.

## 3rd Tank Regiment: inner corridor force
3rd Regiment stays inside the withdrawal geometry, covering road junctions, artillery rear and the truck-capable eastward corridor. Chi-Ha is used as local mobile fire support, not as a high-velocity tank destroyer.

Center result:
- 3rd Regiment: **2 irreversible +3 repairable**;
- immediate ready at 24:00: **~20**;
- Chi-Ha: **3 ready +1 repairable**, no Aug21 irreversible Chi-Ha loss in the center branch.

## Special AP
By Aug21 24:00 center working stocks:
- **TANK-37 ~52 usable**;
- **AT-37 ~320 usable theater-wide**.

The limiting factor is increasingly surviving guns/vehicles and firing geometry, not merely ammunition inventory.

## Command decision
By late morning/early afternoon, Ki40/ground reporting shows continued southern envelopment and northern pressure. The Aug24 counterattack concept is no longer operationally credible.

Around **1300**, 6th Army / 23rd Division elevates the practical decision to **line shortening / force preservation**. Political wording avoids an immediate declaration of total abandonment, but the operational purpose is to move the mobile main body east before the encirclement closes.

Withdrawal starts around **1800** in echelons:
1. wounded/rear services;
2. towable high-value artillery and transport;
3. infantry main bodies;
4. heavy weapons/rear guards;
5. tank/AT screens.

71st Regiment and 2/28th Infantry are placed on a common withdrawal clock; the historical problem of a neighboring unit disappearing without timely notice is explicitly rejected.

## 2/28th Infantry
Aug21 night center combat-effective strength remains roughly **45-50% of original battalion strength**. It is badly worn but still able to move as an organized battalion remnant rather than being trapped as a static pocket.

## End state
- First Tank Group immediate ready: **~37**;
- eastward vehicle corridor: **narrow but open**;
- Soviet envelopment: **not defeated, not yet closed**;
- Aug24 counterattack: **operationally abandoned**;
- 7th Division early battalion-group: approaching the eastern relief chain and expected to affect Aug22, not Aug21.

**War clock: 1939-08-21 24:00.**
