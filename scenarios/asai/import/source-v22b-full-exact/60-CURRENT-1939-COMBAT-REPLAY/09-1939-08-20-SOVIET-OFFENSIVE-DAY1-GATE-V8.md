# 1939-08-20 SOVIET GENERAL OFFENSIVE — DAY 1 — V8 NOMONHAN-A2

## Historical anchor retained
- Soviet general offensive begins 0630 with a large air attack, up to ~250 aircraft in the historical study.
- Historical Japanese center is fixed by air/artillery/infantry while 6th and 11th Tank Brigades and 8th Motorized Armored Brigade swing around the Japanese left/south to cut supply lines.
- Historical reconnaissance later reports more than 1,000 trucks and as many as 500 tanks/armored cars along the left flank.
- Soviet strategic mass and double-envelopment plan are unchanged by the worldline.

## 0500-0630: warning is operational, not prophetic
All front-line units stand-to before dawn. 64th Sentai/fighter reinforcements are part of the alert rotation. One Ki40 launches a planned dawn reconnaissance before the Soviet opening strike.

The Soviet air attack still lands. The key difference is that the first hour is not effectively unopposed: Japanese fighters intercept earlier, while troops/equipment are more dispersed. Working result: Soviet opening air-ground disruption roughly **10-15% below the historical-effect reference**, not canceled.

## 0645-0930: Ki40 flash report and release decision
The airborne Ki40 observes large vehicle movement on the southern/flank approaches and sends a coarse radio flash report before landing. Fine photo confirmation follows after development.

- ~0645-0700: flash warning = southern mechanized movement is large and not explained by the center attack.
- ~0715: 4th Tank Regiment receives release order to move toward a southern intercept/release line, without committing to a head-on tank battle.
- ~0900-0930: developed photography plus ground reports confirm that the center is a fixing attack and the southern mobile force is an envelopment axis.

This advances the operational decision by several hours compared with a late-afternoon realization, while retaining 1939 processing/communications limits.

## Outer-reserve battle, roughly 1000-1530
4th Tank Regiment begins with **34 HOT vehicles**. A mobile AT group of 4 Type94 37 mm guns accompanies it. A reinforced truck-borne infantry company and liaison parties provide limited local security/coordination; this is not a modern mechanized combined-arms battalion.

Employment:
1. force Soviet reconnaissance/armored-car/lead elements to deploy;
2. attack marching-order motorized infantry, AT-gun towing elements and soft transport where exposed;
3. use 16 TANK-37-equipped Ha-Go and mobile AT guns as refusal fire when BT/BA armor presses;
4. withdraw by bounds before Soviet artillery/air/armor mass fixes the regiment.

Center result of this mobile delaying action:
- Soviet armored vehicles temporarily or irreversibly knocked out/disabled: **~14-20** observed/likely, some later recoverable;
- of these, **~7-10** have clear special-AP / 37 mm attribution above ordinary-round expectation;
- Soviet soft vehicles: **~20-35** destroyed/abandoned/disabled working band;
- the affected southern march/deployment cycle is delayed **~5-7 hours** rather than stopped.

Japanese 4th Regiment result:
- **6 irreversible vehicle losses**;
- **7 additional repairable temporary losses**;
- ~21 immediately ready by evening;
- mobile AT group: 1 gun destroyed, 1 damaged/recovered, 2 operational.

The important effect is forcing a large mechanized column to deploy and reorganize, not the kill count.

## Inner reserve: 3rd Tank Regiment
3rd Regiment is not spent with the first counterstroke. During the afternoon it moves to protect road junctions, artillery rear and the eastern corridor.

Only a limited group enters direct contact. Chi-Ha uses 57 mm primarily against infantry/gun positions and retains file52 mobility rules; it does not duel BTs as a high-velocity AT tank.

Center result:
- 3rd begins with29 HOT;
- 2 irreversible losses (non-Chi-Ha center);
- 3 repairable temporary losses, including **1 Chi-Ha running-gear/fragment damage**;
- 24 immediately ready at 24:00;
- Chi-Ha: **3 immediately ready +1 repairable/recovered**, no irreversible Chi-Ha loss on Aug20 center.

## Special AP expenditure
TANK-37:
- 192 usable at dawn;
- ~82 fired during mobile action;
- ~14 lost/unrecoverable with knocked-out vehicles/ammunition stowage center;
- **~96 usable remain** at 24:00.

AT-37:
- 576 usable at dawn;
- mobile group ~66 fired;
- forward 8 guns ~74 fired;
- ~12 lost with destroyed position/ammunition handling;
- **~424 usable remain theater-wide** at 24:00.

These values are ammunition-flow closures, not a claim that every shot struck armor.

## Center and northern front
The Soviet fixing attack remains powerful. Registered fires, warning and more robust communications reduce initial personnel/equipment disruption, but prolonged artillery still breaks lines and positions.

The Japanese center generally holds through the day. Northern pressure continues and some local positions bend. No claim is made that the Soviet offensive is repulsed.

## Operational result at 24:00
The Soviet southern envelopment has **not been stopped**. The outer Japanese reserve has been badly worn and cannot repeat the same action at full strength on Aug21.

However, compared with the historical geometry:
- 71st Regiment/southern Japanese positions are pressured and partially shortened, but their rear is not yet cleanly sealed;
- at least one truck-capable eastward corridor plus foot routes remain usable, under artillery/armor threat;
- the Soviet southern closure clock is displaced toward Aug21-22;
- 3rd Tank Regiment remains an intact inner reserve rather than having been consumed with the first response.

## Command interpretation
The worldline does **not** convert the 4th Regiment's local success into 'the Soviet attack is beaten.' Ki40 and ground reporting show continuing large columns.

At night 6th Army therefore:
- cancels any immediate grand counterattack based on center holding;
- starts eastward movement of heavy wounded, excess supply and vulnerable rear echelons;
- keeps 3rd Tank Regiment for corridor protection / second-day crisis;
- orders 23rd Division to hold only while communications/corridor remain viable;
- waits for Aug21 situation before a full withdrawal/line-shortening decision.

The 7th Division battalion-group is still outside the battlefield but approaching the Hailar/eastern relief chain; it cannot affect Aug20 combat directly.
