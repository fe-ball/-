# NEXT GATE — E15K Shiun full reconciliation

## Why this is next

The CRP common technology has now been separated from the E15K-specific design. The next task is to determine which E15K special features remain worth their mass, drag, schedule and maintenance burden in this worldline.

## Do not assume

Do not assume that:

- CRP must fail because it was difficult historically;
- retractable outrigger floats must be abandoned;
- the speed requirement must be relaxed;
- more engine power alone solves all drag;
- turboprop should replace piston power immediately;
- later E7 availability can be backdated into the original 1939/1940 prototype.

Worldline improvements may either simplify the aircraft **or** make an ambitious feature practical. The audit must price both.

## Mandatory propulsion cases

### P1 — Kasei 14 + CRP
Historical-style early baseline, improved by worldline gearbox/QC/test methods.

### P2 — high-output Kasei + CRP
Use the worldline's earlier stable high-power piston practice; examine cooling drag, nose area, prop loading and schedule.

### P3 — Homare-class piston + CRP
Smaller frontal area and high power may be attractive, but schedule, navalization, cooling and real series maturity must be respected.

### P4 — E7 lower-medium turboprop + CRP
**Later Shiun-modification candidate**, not initial prototype. Evaluate ~1,750–1,850 shp normal / ~1,900–2,000 shp short-time class only under the established E7 schedule and TBO/fuel/maintenance gates.

## Mandatory float/mechanism cases

Cross each viable propulsion family against:

1. retractable outrigger floats + jettisonable main float;
2. retractable outriggers + fixed/non-jettison main float;
3. low-drag fixed outriggers + jettisonable main float;
4. low-drag fixed outriggers + fixed main float.

## Outputs required

For each serious combination calculate/estimate:

- takeoff power and water-run handling;
- torque/yaw burden;
- takeoff distance / rough-water sensitivity;
- clean/max speed with main float attached;
- emergency speed after main-float jettison where applicable;
- cruise speed/range/endurance;
- drag breakdown: fuselage/wing/main float/outriggers/cooling/prop;
- mass and CG;
- maintenance man-hours and special tooling;
- salt-water sealing/corrosion burden;
- reliability and mission-abort modes;
- development/qualification schedule;
- compatibility with Oyodo handling/catapult/storage requirement.

## Performance guard

Do not use simple cube-law power scaling as a final answer. It is only a sanity check. Propeller efficiency, cooling drag, float drag, frontal area, weight and water takeoff all change simultaneously.

## Working target bands to test, not yet canon

Previous discussion suggested approximate candidates around:

- high-output Kasei + CRP + fixed low-drag outrigger: ~490–500 km/h;
- high-output Kasei + CRP + successful retractable outrigger: ~500–510 km/h;
- Homare-class piston + CRP: ~500–515 km/h candidate;
- E7 lower-medium TP + optimized CRP: ~510–525 km/h later candidate;
- emergency main-float-jettison state: ~525–540 km/h or higher remains open.

These are **input workbands only** and must be recalculated before promotion.
