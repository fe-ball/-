# Historical anchor notes used during V17 discussion

These notes are provenance aids, not independent authority over the worldline files.

## CRP / propellers

- NACA dual-rotation full-scale propeller work (1940–41): modest efficiency gain, stronger high-loading/power-absorption case.
- Smithsonian N1K1 Kyofu history: first prototype CRP intended to cancel reaction torque; gearbox reliability drove abandonment.
- EngineHistory Hamilton Standard / Sumitomo material: contra-rotating pitch-control and translation-bearing lubrication were real high-load problems; Japanese Pe-7 experimentation existed.
- Yamaha corporate history: Nippon Gakki entered Hamilton-type propeller production preparations for Army requirements in 1938.

## E15K

- 14-Shi high-speed reconnaissance floatplane project begins in 1939.
- E15K is the first major Japanese aircraft project designed around CRP; N1K follows later.
- Historical E15K combined CRP, retractable outrigger floats, jettisonable main float and high-speed requirement; production remained very small.

## Auxiliary fleet

- Akashi completed 1939 as a dedicated repair ship with extensive machine-shop/electrical/foundry/forging/welding capability.
- Mizuho used 11-go-family diesel machinery and suffered historical reliability/speed restrictions.
- Late-1941 Pearl Harbor preparation required hurried tanker modification and limited underway-replenishment practice, illustrating the split between hardware and crew doctrine.
