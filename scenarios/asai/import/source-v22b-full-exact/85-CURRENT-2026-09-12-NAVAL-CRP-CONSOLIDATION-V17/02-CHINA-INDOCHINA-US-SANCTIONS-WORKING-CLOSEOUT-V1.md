# China / Indochina / US sanctions working closeout

## China operational interpretation

Japan's local combat efficiency, intelligence, communications and unit preservation are better than historical baseline, but China is not strategically broken. Yichang is a real Japanese forward air base; Hankou remains the deeper maintenance hub. China increasingly answers superior Japanese reconnaissance/fighter conditions with force preservation, dispersion and night/logistics movement rather than unfavorable daytime concentration.

### Hundred Regiments

The first and second phase were discussed analytically, but **absolute casualties are not closed in V17**. Central qualitative result only:

- physical rail/infrastructure destruction can remain near historical scale;
- Japanese reaction and command recovery are faster;
- Communist forces pay higher costs in prolonged positional assaults;
- Japanese relief/counterattack/withdrawal handling is better;
- do not promote provisional casualty numbers to canon.

## Northern French Indochina

Occupation value is higher in this worldline because Hanoi/Gia Lam/Haiphong become forward aviation, reconnaissance and logistics nodes. The marginal effect on already-pressured Chinese supply routes may be smaller than in a naive model, but the total military value is greater because E6-family reconnaissance and conventional bomber support can exploit the geography.

Working planning only:

- normal piston operations can begin quickly from existing fields;
- E6-specific FOD/filtration/electrical/O2/photo support requires additional preparation;
- E6 Twin long-range reconnaissance can reach Kunming-class distances with external fuel;
- E6 Single is marginal for sustained long escort at that distance; A6M/conventional fighters remain relevant.

## Chinese dispersion response

Central interpretation: warning succeeds by **dispersal**, not by massing fighters for RAF-style interception.

Working bands retained as planning aids, not measured historical facts:

- sortie-generation tax roughly 15–25%;
- irreversible fighter losses vs aggressive interception potentially 20–35% lower;
- local mission-capable rate penalty roughly 3–8 points;
- Kunming logistics processing friction from dispersion roughly 8–12% normal and 12–18% in heavy raid weeks.

## US sanctions gate

The decisive question is not simply how violent Japan is. US readiness, domestic politics, Europe-first strategy, deterrence logic, southward threat perception and Allied coordination remain primary gates.

Working conclusion:

- stronger Japanese pressure / northern Indochina / Kunming bombing can accelerate non-oil sanctions and aid to China;
- this does **not** automatically move the full oil cutoff into autumn 1940;
- oil remains a high-risk coercive step because US planners understand it could provoke a southward move or war;
- southern Indochina-level direct threat remains a much stronger trigger.
