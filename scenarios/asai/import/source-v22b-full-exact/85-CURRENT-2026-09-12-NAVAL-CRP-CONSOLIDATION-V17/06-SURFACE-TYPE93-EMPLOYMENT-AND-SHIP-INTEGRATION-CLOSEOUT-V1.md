# Surface Type 93 employment / ship-integration closeout

## Common rule

Type 93 body performance is already exceptional and remains essentially historical. Worldline gain comes from the full employment system:

**torpedo + launcher + rapid reload + fire control + night optics/comms + formation synchronization.**

Japanese night-fighting doctrine itself is historical; Asai does not invent it.

## R/FCS separation

R1 means data transmission/wiring/synchronization integration. It is not radar and not automatically a new fire-control computer. FCS generation remains a separate axis.

## Long-range uncertainty

At ~50 kt, 15 km requires roughly 9.7 minutes and 20 km roughly 13 minutes. Target-course/speed errors therefore dominate. Worldline integration helps keep ships on a common solution and lets destroyers deliberately offset tube/ship aim points across the uncertainty band rather than all aiming at one ideal point.

## Reload

Japanese at-sea rapid reload remains a historical strength. Asai-worldline effects mainly reduce alignment/interlock/reset failure and the delay tail. Do not halve reload time by fiat.

## Class interpretation

- Mutsuki: old generation, near-historical.
- Fubuki/Akatsuki: 3×3 61 cm architecture; post-Tomozuru/Fourth Fleet reconstruction constraints remain. Do not count all as 9+9 after rebuild without ship-specific verification.
- Hatsuharu: post-Tomozuru reduction to 2×3 remains.
- Shiratsuyu: 2×4, corrected design.
- Asashio: 2×4 and first strong mass R1 integration generation; early maneuver/turbine synchronization issues still exist but converge faster.
- Kagero: 2×4; cleaner R1 integration.
- Yugumo: same basic headline torpedo architecture, cleaner mature integration; not yet 1940 OOB.

### Cruisers

- Tenryu/Kuma/Nagara/Sendai old systems remain old-hull constrained.
- Kitakami/Oi 1941 torpedo-cruiser conversion remains historical 10×4 / 40-tube concept; no magical 44-tube or Kiso completion.
- Furutaka/Kako after modernization: 2×4; one-side first salvo 4, not 8.
- Aoba/Kinugasa modernization completion state must be checked at any exact 1940 date.
- Myoko class modernization is staggered; Myoko herself remains in refit through the 1940-07-31 current clock while three sisters are further along.
- Takao/Atago receive large refit; Maya/Chokai do not automatically share it because yard constraints remain real.
- Mogami/Mikuma structural history remains historical; Suzuya/Kumano enter service with cleaner corrections.
- Tone/Chikuma are strong reconnaissance/information nodes; Chikuma benefits from later calibration/wiring maturity.

Do not paste 1941 completed configurations into the 1940-07-31 OOB.
