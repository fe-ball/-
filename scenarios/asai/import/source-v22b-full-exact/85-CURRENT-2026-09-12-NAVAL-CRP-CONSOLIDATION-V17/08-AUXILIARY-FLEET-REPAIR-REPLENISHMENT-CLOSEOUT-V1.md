# Auxiliary fleet: repair / aviation support / replenishment closeout

## Akashi

Treat Akashi as a mobile industrial/QC node rather than a combat-stat bonus.

Worldline effects:

- common drawing/part-number/tolerance practice;
- gauge and instrument calibration;
- shaft/bearing/electrical/pump diagnosis;
- before/after vibration, temperature and insulation measurement;
- failure-history and rework reduction.

Working planning bands for repairs substantially internal to Akashi's capabilities:

- diagnosis→repair→retest cycle roughly 15–25% shorter;
- rework/repeat-failure tail roughly 20–35% lower;
- effective labor throughput roughly 10–20% higher.

These are planning bands, not universal multipliers. Drydock-heavy hull, armor, major shaft-removal and giant-gun work remain outside the simple band. No automatic second Akashi.

## Mizuho

Mizuho's 11-go-family machinery benefits directly from the already-closed Taigei/11-go convergence. Central branch:

- initial commissioning defects and tuning remain possible;
- chronic historical-type 17–18 kt restriction is not central;
- practical 20–21 kt-class use should settle earlier;
- plan 22 kt remains achievable in good/test condition;
- historical-scale prolonged 1940 machinery rework is shortened substantially or absorbed into a normal maintenance window.

Do not merge Mizuho engine subtype with Taigei mechanically; the conclusion comes from common family maturity/QC, not identity.

## Underway replenishment

Separate technical integration from doctrine/experience.

Technical gains:

- standardized pump/valve/hose/coupling packages;
- pressure/flow instrumentation;
- better seals and leak test;
- emergency disconnect logic;
- faster repeat installation across tankers.

But station-keeping, line handling and heavy-weather coordination require actual crew training. If the Navy waits until late 1941 to demand full-scale fleet replenishment practice, crews remain inexperienced even with better hardware.

## Nisshin

Type 13-family machinery is more technically mature and acceptance should be cleaner. However Nisshin completion is also gated by role/outfitting changes, including midget-submarine support. Therefore:

- machinery-risk reduction = CLOSE;
- exact commissioning advance = OPEN;
- 1941 commission is not pre-recorded.

## Chitose / Chiyoda / seaplane support

Improved aviation-support tooling, radio/nav/electrical maintenance, fuel/bomb handling and photographic support increase the value of seaplane tenders as mobile aviation bases. This makes premature carrier conversion less obviously attractive, not more.
