# P2 SMALL TURBOPROP STOL PRODUCT CLOSURE — V8 AIRPOWER9

> Current technical/product authority for the P2 line at **1939-04-20**. This restores the old useful design coordinate only after reconnecting it to the current E5-S-T installed-power authority. It does not restore old customer/procurement history automatically.

## 1. Role

P2 is the purpose-designed short-field evolution of the P1 practical turboprop product. Its mission is utility/liaison/survey/training/medical/rough-field and short-deck work. It deliberately spends available turbine power on static thrust, low wing loading, high lift and field operability rather than maximum speed.

It is not Material No.1 and is not a combat-platform reward.

## 2. Current powerplant

Use mature E5-S-T:

- non-Ni quantity/continuous line: ~**952 hp**;
- common headline/normal product rating: **1,000 hp**;
- selected Ni/high-end installed capability: ~**1,050-1,060 hp** where needed;
- engine + free-power turbine + reducer + accessories: ~390-460 kg;
- full installed powerplant including prop/mount/duct: ~510-640 kg, center ~575 kg.

P2 does not require an E6 upgrade simply because E6 exists.

## 4. Low-speed sanity

At 2.80 t and 27 m2:

- CLmax 2.2 gives Vs ~99 km/h;
- CLmax 2.4 gives Vs ~95 km/h;
- CLmax 2.6 gives Vs ~91 km/h.

Thus a mature Fowler/slat configuration around CLmax ~2.4-2.6 naturally puts approach-speed planning in roughly the **118-123 km/h** range at maximum weight and lower at operational landing weights.

Do not convert those analytical numbers into measured field performance. Actual takeoff/landing distance remains an operator/airframe test result.
