# AERIAL PHOTO RECON / DEEP-FIRE INTEGRATION — CANON — V8 NOMONHAN-A2

## Purpose
Promote the E4-era photo-recon and heavy-tractor lessons into a 1939 operational chain. This is not a radar/real-time ISR system and does not create extra gun range or ammunition.

## Lineage
E4 Twin combat/service use in China closes the practical question that a high-speed, low-vibration aircraft can take militarily useful large-format photographs. Domestic optical firms develop the camera/optics; Asai contributes aircraft, mount, vibration, power, optical-window, shutter linkage, test/calibration, metadata, and processing workflow.

By 1939 the mature chain is:
`wide-area strip -> develop -> frame metadata/index -> stereo/mosaic -> change detection -> target coordinate -> weapon/range suitability -> fire mission -> re-photo battle-damage / relocation check`.

## Camera capability
Retain V7 camera physics and domestic-industry division of labor.
Practical packages:
- AREA: **18-25 cm** focal length class for roads, vehicle parks, bridges, airfields, broad artillery search;
- DETAIL: **50-70 cm** long focus for gun positions, bridge details, vehicle concentration, emplacement change.

Representative good-condition long-focus detail:
- ~0.7-1.0 m ground detail at 5 km;
- ~0.8-1.1 m at 6 km;
conditions, haze, film, shutter, focus and interpretation remain gates.

## Ki40 / E5 Twin reconnaissance closure
E5 Twin physical population at 1939-04-20 remains **10 completed airframes**. Army Ki40 evaluation branch = **3 aircraft**, all JF in the current allocation closure.

Nomonhan operational evaluation:
- **Ki40 JF x2** forward to Hailar-area support in July 1939;
- **Ki40 JF x1** retained rear/training/comparison reserve;
- this is a special service-evaluation detachment, not an operational squadron adoption.

Working mission envelope:
- normal precision photo altitude **5.0-6.5 km**;
- 6.5-7 km evaluation use is possible but not routine due oxygen/cold/window/film/haze gates;
- precision photo run **340-380 km/h**;
- high-threat strip **390-420 km/h** with reduced loiter/coverage margin;
- escape **500+ km/h**;
- no claim that 520-540 km/h maximum equals usable photo-run speed.

Radio flash reports may transmit gross observations before landing. Fine identification, coordinate closure and change detection require film development/interpretation and therefore normally remain hours-scale.

## Deep-fire feedback
E4 photo reconnaissance plus improved heavy-vehicle/artillery-tractor readiness drives an earlier concept of **mobile long-range artillery as a system**, not merely a heavier gun.

By July 1939 the following doctrine is substantially present in selected units:
- long-range 10-15 cm-class guns prioritized for counterbattery and deep targets;
- short-range guns prioritized for east-bank/direct support instead of wasting ammunition on unreachable targets;
- photo coordinates, survey, meteorology, muzzle-velocity/ammunition-lot data and communications integrated before firing;
- heavy tractors/recovery permit night or half-day-scale displacement of selected heavy batteries;
- ammunition columns and recovery are recognized as part of the weapon system.

Not closed / not granted:
- no extra gun range;
- no universal hit-rate multiplier;
- no modern shoot-and-scoot in minutes;
- no mass 24 cm super-heavy artillery solution;
- no unlimited ammunition or truck fleet.

## July 23-25 Nomonhan result
The worldline gets a better opening counterbattery/target allocation from roughly the same historical-scale gun park and ammunition consumption. Enemy batteries can be temporarily silenced/displaced more efficiently and one crossing/approach can be disrupted for hours, but Soviet range, ammunition, redundancy and mobility still defeat the Japanese offensive. **Attack remains a failure and is stopped on 25 July.**

The main lesson becomes: finding and ranging the target is no longer the only problem; enough long-range guns, shells, transport and replacement positions are required.
