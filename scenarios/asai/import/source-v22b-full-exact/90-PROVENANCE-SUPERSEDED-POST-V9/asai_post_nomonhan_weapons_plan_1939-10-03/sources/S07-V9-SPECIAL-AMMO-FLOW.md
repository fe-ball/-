After Jul3 the retained test lots imply 24 AT-37 and 14 TANK-37 remaining. V9 later closes the Aug19 usable theater issue basis at **AT-37 912 / TANK-37 288**.

Therefore the minimum additional Jul4-Aug19 forward replenishment needed to reach the V9 state is:

- AT-37: 912 - 24 = **888 additional rounds**;
- TANK-37: 288 - 14 = **274 additional rounds**.

Minimum cumulative accepted-and-forwarded complete rounds by Aug19 are consequently:

- AT-37: first 48 + later 888 = **at least 936**;
- TANK-37: first 48 + later 274 = **at least 322**.

These are **lower bounds**, not factory production totals. Factory gross production and inspection acceptance must be at least this high, but may be higher because rejects, proof expenditure, depot reserve, transit loss, training expenditure and unforwarded accepted stock are not enumerated.

## Industrial interpretation
Past dense-material audits already show that raw dense material is not the immediate constraint at this scale. The operative chain is:

`core/material processing -> machining -> cartridge assembly -> QA/proof -> inspection acceptance -> military release -> forwarding -> theater issue`.

The exact factory-by-factory monthly ledger remains unknowable from the retained records and is not required for the closed Nomonhan campaign. It should only be reopened if a later procurement decision requires national production capacity, reject rates or competing caliber demand.
