### Q1 — E5 Twin ceases to be an event-only prototype

- prototype

- low-rate military production

It is now a real common military airframe, not a scarce special demonstrator.

### Q2 — mission role stops being welded to airframe identity
The decisive operational lesson is not “reconnaissance wins.”
It is that the same E5 common airframe can be allocated among:
- photo / command reconnaissance
- fixed-target attack
- BDA
- same-type high cover / escort
- rapid liaison / command transport
- extended-fuel missions

Mission kits, not permanent airframe taxonomy, increasingly define daily employment.

### Q3 — recon -> strike -> BDA -> retask becomes a recurring loop
This is the main combat-system change.

The gain is not a magic increase in bomb lethality.
It is:
- fresher target information
- less repeated attack against already-disabled targets
- faster retasking
- better assessment of repairs / resumed movement
- increasing enemy pressure to disperse and move at night

### Q4 — same-type “high cover” becomes an operational tactic
The E5 formation can protect its own mission aircraft without requiring a completely different escort type.
Enemy fighters climbing to intercept an E5 mission package can be attacked by lighter / higher E5 aircraft or by Ki-42.

This is not air superiority by itself, but it changes interception geometry.

### Q5 — Ki-42 becomes a specialist, not a failed standard fighter
The 1938 operational conclusion is:
- ordinary turning fighter: unsuitable
- high-speed / high-cover / bomber interception: valuable

Doctrine is now explicit:
- obtain altitude first
- preserve energy
- do not enter sustained low-speed turning combat

The JF comparison is sufficiently promising to justify an operational block.

### Q6 — headquarters time-distance is materially shortened
E5 command reconnaissance and rapid courier work are now recurring services.

This creates a second military effect distinct from bombing:
- same-day command-recon cycles over selected 350–450 km radii
- same-day movement of high-value documents, imagery, staff, specialists and precision parts between major hubs
- no bulk-logistics credit

This effect has already been booked and does not need a new additional multiplier in 1939.
