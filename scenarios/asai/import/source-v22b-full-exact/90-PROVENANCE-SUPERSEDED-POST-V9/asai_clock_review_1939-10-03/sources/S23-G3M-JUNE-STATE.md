## Ruling
For China replay through 1939-06-20, G3M2 units used in the Chongqing campaign receive **EX0-equivalent headline-performance credit** for combat calculations. Existing Asai-chain production/QC, engine matching, vibration/cooling and maintenance advantages remain valid.

This is deliberately conservative. It does **not** assert that every participating G3M2 physically retained the historical collector exhaust. V8 technical authority allows EX2 on 1939-40 new-build or major-overhaul G3M2 aircraft, with selective fleet retrofit. The package, however, contains no named Chongqing unit/lot ledger proving how many participating airframes had completed that change by 1939-06-20.

Therefore:

- do not import the Ki-27 Nomonhan EX2 fraction into G3M units;
- do not invent a G3M unit percentage;
- do not credit the +4 to +6 km/h-class EX2 full-shadow increment in the Jun1939 China replay unless a later named-airframe/unit record is introduced;
- continue to credit already-closed reliability/serviceability/QC effects;
- a later explicit unit/lot record may locally promote individual aircraft or a named unit to EX2 without reopening the class technology question.

## Why this closes the issue
The open question affected the current campaign only if an exact EX2 fraction were needed to change bombing reach, timing or survivability. No such exact fraction is supported by the retained records. The correct current-state action is therefore to close the replay input at the conservative state rather than leave a permanent global OPEN.
