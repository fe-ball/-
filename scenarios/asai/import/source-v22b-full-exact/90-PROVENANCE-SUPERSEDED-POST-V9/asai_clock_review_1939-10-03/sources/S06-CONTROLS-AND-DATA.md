# CONTROLS / COMPUTATION / DATA ORGANIZATION — 1939-04-20 — V8 AIRPOWER12

> The purpose is to distinguish **capability** from a later formal organization name. Do not backdate the H2 bureaucracy; do not erase the already-existing function.

## 1. Computation is already a business

Since the 1928 organization, Asai separates:

- **Computation & Office Machine Division** — products, sales/lease, installation, maintenance, card/workflow design and service-bureau business;
- **Research Institute Central Technical Calculation Room** — secret/internal scientific calculation for GT, materials, aviation and other R&D.

By 1935-37 major engine customers can operate secured customer-side Asai-supported calculation rooms rather than sending proprietary design data back to Asai.

## 2. Control engineering at Apr20

The company already designs and qualifies:

- mechanical fuel controls and schedules;
- centrifugal governor functions;
- pressure/aneroid compensation where needed;
- starting sequences;
- acceleration limiting / surge-avoidance interfaces;
- overspeed / overtemperature protection;
- turbocharger wastegate/boost control;
- free-power-turbine governors;
- generator speed/frequency interfaces;
- propeller/governor interface requirements.

Ownership is distributed:

- Powerplant Division owns product/system integration;
- Research Institute owns analysis, transient rigs and advanced methods;
- T-AUX builds/packages/tests much of the accessory hardware and interface equipment.

## 3. Formal Controls & Actuation Group — NOT YET

The Sep1939 audit calls for a cross-company **Controls & Actuation Group during 1939H2** because the number of simultaneous control problems is becoming too large for informal/project distribution.

Therefore at Apr20:

**FUNCTION = CLOSED / ACTIVE.**

**FORMAL CROSS-COMPANY GROUP = NOT YET FORMED.**

Do not treat absence of the future title as absence of control technology.

## 4. Data/reliability at Apr20

Existing streams include:

- independent Inspection Division production/QC records;
- serial and lot traceability;
- engine/test cards;
- operating-hour/start/rating logs where products require them;
- overhaul teardown and returned-part records;
- customer service/commissioning fault records;
- computation-based statistical/failure classification.

These streams are not yet one enterprise-wide standardized field-return bureaucracy.

## 5. Field Reliability / Data Return Bureau — NOT YET

The formal bureau in the Sep1939 audit is an H2 organizational response, strongly sharpened by later field experience. It is not an Apr20 institution.

Its future job is to unify/standardize already-existing information streams, not to introduce empirical feedback for the first time.

## 6. Organizational guard

Do not create a single fictional Apr20 `Controls/Data Department`. The actual Apr20 advantage is a **network of mature functions plus common cards/gauges/computation**, with formal cross-company consolidation still ahead.
