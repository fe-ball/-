# NOMONHAN AIR / EXHAUST / SPECIAL-AMMUNITION CORRECTION

This file supersedes any interpretation that Japanese normal aviation was essentially unable to operate after Soviet air superiority, and any interpretation that Ki-27 EX2 remained a small minority by Aug1939 merely because some earlier sentai state rows were OPEN.

Machine-readable companion for air values: `03A-NOMONHAN-AIR-SPECIAL-AMMO-WORKING-V9.tsv`. The special-ammunition flow has now been formally closed by `12-NOMONHAN-SPECIAL-AMMO-PRODUCTION-FLOW-CLOSEOUT-V9.md` and `12A-NOMONHAN-SPECIAL-AMMO-FLOW-LEDGER-V9.tsv`; those files supersede the former "pre-formal production ledger" status while retaining the V9 912/288 Aug19 basis.

## Air control interpretation
Soviet air superiority is real and remains operationally important. It does NOT equal complete aerial suppression of Japanese aviation.

Historical and worldline Japanese fighters/bombers still fly on Aug20-22. The V8 branch itself already allowed fighter/bomber support where possible; the missing element was a continuous stock-flow/sortie/loss ledger, not a physical prohibition on flying.

Ki40 is a scarce high-value reconnaissance platform. It is NOT invulnerable, real-time ISR or FAC.

## EX2 adoption correction
Relevant V8 adoption facts:
- late-1938 Ki-27 new production already trends to EX2 standard
- existing aircraft can convert EX0→EX2 at major overhaul
- 24th Sentai May1939: EX2 15 / EX0 4 (~79%)

Therefore an OPEN 11th/64th Sentai mix cannot be frozen unchanged for months. EX2 is effectively an absorbing state unless the aircraft is lost.

Working Aug19 Ki-27 EX2 mix used in replay:
- 24th Sentai: ~19 aircraft; EX2 center 18 (17-19)
- 11th Sentai: ~32 by Aug21; EX2 center ~26 (24-28)
- 64th Sentai: ~36; EX2 center ~32 (30-34)
- 1st Sentai: ~20; EX2 center ~17 (15-18)
Overall frontline Ki-27 EX2 fraction: ~80%+.

Performance consequence remains modest: roughly +5-8 km/h-class speed benefit and FCS/quality gains, NOT a magic kill multiplier.

## Special 37mm ammunition correction
Old Aug19 forward stocks AT-37 576 / TANK-37 192 are not well supported by the V8 production→qualification→release timeline.
Relevant timeline:
- AT-37 combat-evaluation release May29
- TANK-37 limited qualification Jun17
- partial PASS Jun30
- both combat-validated Jul3
- dense-core material availability is not the immediate bottleneck; processing, QA, cartridge assembly, inspection, military release and forwarding are

Working corrected Aug19 issue basis:
- AT-37: **912 center** (working 900-1000)
- TANK-37: **288 center** (working 240-320)
Example allocation logic only: AT front/mobile/army reserve; TANK selected Ha-Go carriers. Do not infer every 37mm gun has special ammunition.

Approximate residual under already-replayed consumption:
- Aug22: TANK ~116, AT ~550
- Aug25: TANK ~100, AT ~470
These residues are now tied to the formal minimum-flow reconstruction in file 12/12A. Exact factory gross output remains intentionally unasserted.

Strategic lesson: the bottleneck is not "uranium raw material ran out." It is machining/assembly/qualification/inspection/release/forwarding. Army reaction is likely to demand more completed special AP and future larger-caliber variants while Asai argues to preserve W/WHA alternatives and strategic natural-U stock.
