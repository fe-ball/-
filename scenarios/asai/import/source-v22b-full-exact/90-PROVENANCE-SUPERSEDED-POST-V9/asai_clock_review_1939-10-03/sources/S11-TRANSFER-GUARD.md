# CHEAT DISTANCE / KNOWLEDGE-TRANSFER GUARD — V9 TECHCLOSE2

## Status

**CURRENT-HIGH cross-organizational technical guard.**

This file refines the mandatory cheat/capability preflight when a technology leaves Asai personally or the Asai corporate core. It does not reduce transferred machines or methods merely because Asai is absent, and it does not clone the reincarnator's foreknowledge into every customer organization.

## 1. Distance is epistemic and organizational, not geographic

Use `CD0-CD4` only to answer **where the answer-selection advantage comes from**. It is not a performance multiplier.

- **CD0 — ASAI PERSONAL:** Asai is explicitly participating in the design/review. The strong idea/search-order gate applies: he may identify the right branch and known failure classes early. Unknown measured coefficients, actual defects, endurance and industrial execution remain physical gates.
- **CD1 — ASAI CORE ORGANIZATION:** Asai internal divisions/research rooms inherit accumulated standards, calculation forms, test infrastructure, failure taxonomy, QC, and previously institutionalized architectures. They do **not** receive a new packet of personal foreknowledge for every problem. Their advantage is organizational convergence.
- **CD2 — SUPPORTED CUSTOMER / SECURE CUSTOMER ROOM:** the customer possesses Asai machines, generic calculation forms, training, maintenance, interfaces and contractually transferred methods. Customer-secret input data may remain entirely inside the customer security compartment. Asai's unpublished latest internal methods and personal hidden answer are not automatically transferred.
- **CD3 — AUTONOMOUS INSTITUTION:** a bureau, arsenal, shipyard, manufacturer or service technical office has absorbed the tools and workflow sufficiently to operate and improve them independently. Its domain experts choose problem-specific models, cryptanalytic attacks, fire-control doctrine, ship arrangements, etc. No Asai personal cheat is present unless an explicit event reopens CD0/CD2 consultation.
- **CD4 — FIELD / OPERATIONAL USER:** ships, units, stations and maintenance organizations use issued hardware, doctrine, tables and training. Design-room optimization and hidden knowledge do not appear at the field edge. Field results remain constrained by readiness, operator practice, communications, environment and enemy action.

`CD` and the older customer-computation maturity/U-level are different axes. A mature U4 customer room can be CD2 or CD3 depending on whether the question concerns vendor-supported infrastructure or autonomous domain work.

## 2. Non-decay / ownership rule

Knowledge that has already been transferred through a real channel does not vanish when Asai leaves the room.

A customer may keep and independently exploit:

- purchased or leased calculation/office machines;
- installed secure calculation rooms;
- generic calculation forms and verification examples;
- training, maintenance practice and card/data formats;
- accepted QC, calibration and test procedures;
- licensed or delivered electromechanical modules;
- its own locally developed methods and improvements.

Therefore **do not take the tool away from the Naval Technical Department, gunnery/ordnance offices, code/cipher organizations, shipyards or other customers merely to preserve narrative distance**. Distance limits the *unpaid foreknowledge*, not legitimately acquired industrial capital.

## 3. No remote reincarnator cloning

The following shortcuts are forbidden unless an explicit CD0 intervention is recorded:

- Asai somehow knows a customer's secret design problem and supplies the exact optimum without being briefed;
- a Navy cryptanalytic office gains the correct attack/key/crib merely because Asai computation hardware exists;
- a ship class receives the best later fire-control architecture because Asai knows future naval electronics;
- every customer inherits Asai's latest internal computation form on the purchase date;
- an operational unit improvises a design-office solution because it is inside the wider Asai supply network.

For subjects outside Asai's principal expertise, especially operational cryptanalysis and Navy-specific doctrine, **CD0 does not manufacture specialist insight out of nothing**. His useful contribution is normally problem selection, instrumentation, machine engineering, reliability, computation workflow or a broad later-known concept; the service's own specialists must do the domain work.

## 4. Transfer channels that are allowed

A technical effect at CD2/CD3 must have at least one paid channel:

`PRODUCT / MACHINE / DOCUMENTED METHOD / TRAINING / EMBEDDED SUPPORT / JOINT TEST / LICENSE / LOCAL REDEVELOPMENT / FIELD FEEDBACK`

The transferred result may then compound locally. This is important for the 1930s: calculation rooms, maintenance networks, common cards, gunnery calculation work and cipher-machine electromechanics have existed long enough that selected Navy organizations can be autonomous users by 1939.

## 5. Default 1939 placements for the present closeout

- Asai central technical calculation and core R&D: **CD1** unless Asai personal intervention is explicitly recorded, then CD0 for that decision only.
- Major external engineering customers with secure Asai-supported calculation rooms: **CD2**, with mature problem classes capable of becoming CD3 locally.
- Naval design / shipbuilding calculation practice after the 1934-35 accident-driven expansion: **CD3** for routine weight, stability, structure, shafting and redesign work.
- Naval gunnery/ordnance/fire-control technical offices by 1936-39: **CD3** for their own FCS work, using Asai-origin machinery/method support through CD2 procurement channels.
- Naval cipher/cryptanalytic central technical offices by the late 1930s: **CD3** for cryptologic work; Asai is CD2 hardware/workflow supplier, not the holder of service keys or automatic solver of enemy systems.
- Fleet ships, intercept stations and combat units: **CD4**.

These placements are baselines, not universal personnel assignments. A named event can record a closer or farther relationship.

## 6. Causal accounting

When a result differs from history, record both:

1. the **technical causal channel** (`O0/O1/O2/O3`, test/QC, electromechanics, etc. where applicable); and
2. the **distance/transfer channel** (`CD0-CD4` plus the actual transfer mechanism).

Do not use `CD1 is +x%`, `CD2 is half the cheat`, or any other scalar decay rule.

## 7. Domain consequences adopted in TECHCLOSE2

### Computation

The Navy and other mature customers retain their calculation rooms. By 1939 routine calculation culture in selected central bureaus is institutional and no longer requires Asai's presence. New, unusually difficult problem classes may still require local experiments or explicit consultation.

### Cryptography

The Navy owns the cryptologic problem. Asai may supply reliable electromechanical hardware, card/relay machinery, maintenance and generic workflow. Naval cryptanalysts supply the target-specific insight, language work, traffic analysis and operational exploitation. This closes the institutional baseline without granting automatic decrypt success.

### Naval FCS

The Navy owns the fire-control architecture and ship integration. Asai-origin computation/electromechanical practice can support design, calibration, transmission, servo/switchboard and testing. Class-level retrofit depth is therefore allowed to differ from history through paid refit/new-build channels, but no class jumps directly to a future fully integrated system solely from Asai foreknowledge.

## 8. Precedence

This guard supersedes any reading of older computation/naval material that treats the protagonist's personal answer-selection advantage as automatically replicated in external organizations. It does **not** supersede already closed physical hardware, procurement, ship-refit or combat-state ledgers.
