## 8. The main Q1 conclusion

By 30 March 1940, Asai is no longer primarily constrained by “can a difficult engine be built at all?”

The growth bottleneck has shifted toward:
- accessories and final package acceptance,
- field service / training,
- hot-test and metrology replication,
- customer installation capacity,
- strategic material allocation,
- skilled supervisory staff.

Demand is not exhausted.

T-2 is now producing enough to matter. T-3 has reached the qualification threshold. T-4 and T-5 overlap behind it. Consequently **Q2–Q4 1940 can grow faster in absolute output than 1939 did**, provided T-AUX, hot-section material, test capacity and customer absorption continue to expand.

At the same time, 1940 is the first year in which post-Nomonhan weapons programs begin to generate physical evaluation hardware. Their main combat effect is still future; their industrial and doctrinal effect is already present.
