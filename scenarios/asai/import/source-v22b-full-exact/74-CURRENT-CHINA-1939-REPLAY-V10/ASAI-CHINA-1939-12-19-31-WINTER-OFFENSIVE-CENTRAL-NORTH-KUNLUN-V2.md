- conventional aviation remains

### What E5 can observe
14–16 Dec:
- abnormal road / motor traffic
- artillery / supply movement

Chinese night / hill movement prevents exact order-of-battle identification.

### Revised interpretation

By **16 Dec evening**:
- “large Chinese counteroffensive against Kunlun/Nanning route probable” = high-confidence
- exact 5th-Corps strength / tank count = unknown
- attack within roughly **24–72 h** = probable

This is enough to overturn the historical-style dismissal of the threat.

It is not enough to know the exact dawn-H-hour.

# 8. Japanese pre-18-Dec Kunlun actions

Instead:
1. freeze nonessential outward movement from the Nanning–Kunlun system;

3. move one additional battalion-class infantry element toward the pass / rear blocking line;
4. reinforce artillery / mortar ammunition;
5. mark alternate headquarters / withdrawal points;
6. put E5 / conventional air onto approach-route and BDA priority.

Not all reinforcement reaches the forward crest before the Chinese assault.

Working opening difference:

- actual immediate crest/strongpoint increment is smaller.
