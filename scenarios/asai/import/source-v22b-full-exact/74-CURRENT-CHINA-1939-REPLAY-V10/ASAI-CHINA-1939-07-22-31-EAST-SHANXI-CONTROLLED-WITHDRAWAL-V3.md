## 1. Historical anchor

Matched-history southern-front sequence:
- 21 Jul: 14th Army / 93rd Army attack southwest of Yangcheng.
- 25 Jul: Japanese 39th Brigade crosses the Qinshui while being pursued.
- 25 Jul: 93rd Army recovers Yangcheng.

- 27 Jul: 39th Brigade reaches Jincheng.
- 35th Division, after handing over Jincheng to the 20th Division, begins withdrawal on 24 Jul.

The key historical feature is not merely “China retakes two towns.”
It is that the 39th Brigade disengages under pressure after being extended around Dongfeng / Yangcheng.

## 2. Revised starting condition — 22 Jul

Compared with matched history, the 39th Brigade enters this phase:
- less deeply extended west of Yangcheng;
- with more cohesive battalion spacing;
- with a local reserve / route-security layer closer to Yangcheng / Qinshui;
- with fresher artillery target information;
- with fewer vehicles / support elements pushed into the exposed forward area.

E5 / ground intelligence assessment:
- the 14th Army and 93rd Army remain capable of continued pressure;
- Chinese movement is a counter-concentration, not a general retreat;
- the Japanese operational objective is now Jincheng consolidation / force link-up, not permanent occupation of every swept county town.

This changes the rational decision.

## 3. 22–23 Jul — do not turn Yangcheng into a prestige hold

Chinese attacks continue around the Yangcheng / Dongfeng approaches.

Revised Japanese command does NOT:
- launch a deep westward counterattack;
- hold Yangcheng at all costs;
- read temporary daylight gaps as Chinese collapse.

Instead:
- forward battalion positions fall back by planned bounds;
- mountain / field artillery fires on registered approach routes;
- vehicles, wounded, communications and nonessential stores are moved first;
- rear guards remain long enough to prevent the withdrawal becoming a rout.

### Working clock
- withdrawal preparation: begins **22 Jul afternoon/evening**
- first deliberate shortening: **23 Jul**

This is ~1–2 days earlier than the historical pressure-driven crossing sequence.

## 4. Yangcheng — local nonlinear map difference

Because the Japanese choose force preservation over symbolic retention:

**Yangcheng is vacated / recovered by Chinese forces on 24 Jul**, approximately one day earlier than matched history.

This is NOT a Chinese operational breakthrough.

Chinese forces enter because:
- the Japanese 39th Brigade has deliberately shortened;
- the town is outside the preferred Jincheng-centered final geometry;
- holding it would expose a brigade to converging Chinese pressure.

### Consequences
Japanese:
- fewer casualties in the final town / route-defense fighting;
- more transport and support equipment is withdrawn.

Chinese:
- gain the town sooner;
- pursuit formations are exposed on known roads / crossings and pay somewhat more to artillery / air attack.

This is a genuine qualitative difference:
**better reconnaissance produces an earlier territorial concession, not an extra Japanese occupation.**

The Japanese do not attempt to preserve Qinshui as an isolated forward garrison after the 39th Brigade begins its controlled eastward movement.
