## 4. What the Japanese can see

E5 / conventional layers together improve:
- road-flow trend;
- river-line occupation;
- artillery / transport relocation;
- whether a position remains occupied after bombing;
- daytime withdrawal routes.

They do NOT reliably reveal:
- night marches;
- wooded ravines;
- every temporary headquarters;
- final Chinese reserve positions;
- whether an empty daylight road will fill after dark.

Japanese staff therefore increasingly labels sectors:
- CONFIRMED ACTIVE
- PROBABLY VACATED
- STALE / UNOBSERVED

This is carried into the 23 Sep assault plan.

- 106th Division has already begun operations from 15 Sep.

### Result

- stale-information sectors;
- river/road condition;
- artillery/support reach.

### Physical delta, 17–19 Sep
