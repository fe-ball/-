# SOURCE LEADS FROM THE 1937 REPLAY

These are research leads used/referenced during the session. They are not a substitute for source criticism and are not all equal authority.

High-value recurring source families:
- Japanese Monograph 178 (North China operations): ibiblio HyperWar Japanese Monographs.
- Japanese Monograph 70 / 166 / 179 (Central China / naval air / Nanking-pursuit chronology): ibiblio HyperWar.
- FRUS 1937–38 State Department reports for Shanghai, Nanking and Panay.
- US Navy / NHHC / USNI for Panay details.
- Chinese local archives/memorial/military-history pages for detailed daily defense chronologies, used cautiously and cross-checked.
- Askew, “The Nanjing Incident: Recent Research and Trends” / chinajapan.org PDF for Nanking troop-strength/reconstruction problems.
- IBM historical punched-card material and period aviation-camera museum records for photo metadata plausibility.

Important source-treatment note:
The user values physical consistency over false precision. When casualty/weapon totals conflict, use them as bounded constraints rather than fabricate a single exact number.
