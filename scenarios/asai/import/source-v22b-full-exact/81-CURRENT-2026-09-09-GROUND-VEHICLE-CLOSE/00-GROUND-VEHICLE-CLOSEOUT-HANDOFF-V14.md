# GROUND VEHICLE CLOSEOUT HANDOFF — V14

Status: **CURRENT-HIGHEST for ground-vehicle technical/development/procurement state through 1941.** Combat clocks do not advance.

## 1. Scope and actor boundary
Asai is **not** a general complete-vehicle manufacturer and does not control Army vehicle policy. Its strongest ground contribution remains:
- materials/QC: special steel, bearings, gears/shafts, track pins, springs, friction materials, heat treatment, gauges;
- subsystems: turbocharging, intake/exhaust, filtration, cooling, electrical/generator, winch/mobile power;
- co-engineering: gearing, cooling, drivetrain life, CG/weight bookkeeping, turret loads, optics/QC;
- direct Asai power-unit work only in narrow GT/APU/research branches.

Complete vehicle design, procurement and doctrine remain with Army arsenals, primes and service organizations.

## 2. 1941 central picture
- Type 95 Ha-Go remains the mass light-tank line, ~120 hp NA; turbo is a selected hot/high flat-rating branch, not blanket fit.
- Type 97 Chi-Ha family retains a **~500 new-hull 1941 budget**. Derivatives are counted inside this hull budget, not added on top.
- New-turret 47 mm Chi-Ha is the first serious 1941 series fielding branch.
- Chi-He is an advanced development line, not ordinary 1941 OOB.
- 75 mm fixed-casemate SPG is pilot/troop-test, not normal tank OOB.
- So-Da and Te-Re are useful service vehicles; Ho-Ha remains prototype/troop-test in 1941.
- Heavy 6x4 trucks and artillery tractors are where Asai turbo/QC has the largest immediate military effect.
- Amphibious Chi-Ha and deep-wading Chi-Ha remain **zero**; WET1 is wet-weather/post-ford reliability only.

## 3. 1941 new-hull budget

Existing-hull 47 mm conversions are a separate flow: **48 in 1941**, because they do not consume a new hull.

## 4. Post-opening development branches
The prewar technical state is sufficient to start a small **20–22 t next-medium technical test-vehicle program** before/through 1941 without making it a service tank. Two research vehicles are used to test 24–25 t ballast load, ~1.70 m turret ring, 75 mm complete-round integration and 300 hp-class drivetrain margin. OOB = 0.

After war opening, two branches are distinct:
1. **18–19 t emergency 75 mm Chi-He-family tank**: existing 240 hp/50 mm-class infrastructure used to get a usable 75 mm turret tank quickly. Formal name/year designation remains a naming gate.
2. **23–25 t next-medium line**: 27 L V12 ~300 hp continuous / 320 hp short, 1.70–1.75 m ring, three-man turret, 75 mm 720–750 m/s development path with 770–800 m/s threat-driven growth.

The 25–27 t / 810–830 m/s / 330–350 hp envelope is a later growth envelope, not a 1941 service tank. A 30 t+ heavy-tank program is not automatically opened merely because a KV-class threat exists.

## 5. Counting guard
Factory accepted != service released != assigned != operationally ready != combat presence. Development, troop-test and support vehicles never backflow into ordinary tank OOB.
