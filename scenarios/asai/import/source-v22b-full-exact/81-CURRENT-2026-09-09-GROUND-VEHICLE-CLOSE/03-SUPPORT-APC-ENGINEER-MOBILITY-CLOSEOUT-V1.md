# SUPPORT / APC / ENGINEER / MOBILITY CLOSEOUT — V14

## Heavy road and artillery mobility
The largest practical Asai ground effect is not a new tank model. It is improved heavy-vehicle readiness through selected turbo flat-rating, filtration, cooling, transmission/QC, bearings, winches and mobile power.

- Type 94-class 6x4 heavy-truck family remains broadly historical in vehicle identity; ~70 hp diesel baseline, selected 80–90 hp turbo priority lots.
- Artillery-tractor families remain the normal prime movers. Turbo/flat-rating improves loaded pull and hot/high performance rather than top speed.
- 1941 Asai heavy-vehicle turbo component allocation: 1,200–1,450 packages, center ~1,350. **These are not extra vehicles.**

## So-Da / Te-Re
- Type 98 So-Da: standard engine remains near historical ~65 hp; Asai contribution is QC, transmission/filtration and support integration.
- Type 100 Te-Re: electrical/generator/wiring/vibration/optics/QC are the strong Asai ripple; it remains an Army observation/command vehicle rather than an Asai design.

## Ho-Ha
Half-track technology itself is not the main missing principle: Type 98 Ko-Hi-class prime-mover experience provides a school. Ho-Ha therefore becomes a **1941 prototype/troop-test branch**, not an impossible technology and not ordinary OOB. V14 closes eight physical articles as a working development total; 1942 low-rate procurement remains a service-priority gate.

## Engineer and recovery
Do not invent a wholly new Asai armored engineer family when Japanese existing vehicle schools suffice.
- armored engineer work should preferentially grow from existing SS-Ki/Soko Sagyo Ki-type schools;
- Chi-Ha-family armored recovery is treated as a Se-Ri-family/related low-rate support solution rather than an unrelated new vehicle identity;
- mechanized engineer packages are tractors + trailers + girders/pontoons + winches + pumps + generators, not an early mass tank-bridgelayer fleet.

## Asai role guard
Vehicle primes, Army arsenals and doctrine owners remain in control. Asai may improve components, production reproducibility and test methods; it does not create additional nationwide motorization by declaration. Fuel, tires, drivers, roads, bridges and maintenance remain separate constraints.
