# GROUND OOB COUNTING BOUNDARY — V14

Use five separate states:
1. **physical article exists**;
2. **factory/arsenal accepted**;
3. **service released**;
4. **assigned to a unit**;
5. **operationally ready / combat present**.

Never collapse them.

### Ordinary OOB-eligible by 1941
Ha-Go, standard Chi-Ha 57 mm, accepted new-turret Chi-Ha 47 mm, So-Da (limited), Te-Re, normal heavy trucks/tractors and engineer/workshop support packages.

### Support-limited
Chi-Ha-family armored recovery / Se-Ri-family low-rate vehicles.

### No normal 1941 OOB
75 mm SPG, Chi-He development vehicles, Chi-Ho test vehicles, 20–22 t next-medium research vehicles, Ho-Ha troop-test vehicles, amphibious cargo trial vehicles, heavy-Daihatsu pilot craft as standard flotilla, Ka-Mi prototypes, direct-GT traction demonstrator.

Exact theater/unit allocations, repair state, attrition and combat presence remain replay/HISTORY outputs.
