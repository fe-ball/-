# LANDING CRAFT / AMPHIBIOUS NAVAL INTEGRATION — V14

Normal landing craft remain naturally aspirated diesel products. Asai contribution is reliability/QC, gearing/shafts, sealing/pumps, electrical waterproofing and calculation of ramps/loading/turnaround—not blanket GT conversion.

### Heavy Daihatsu branch
The 20–22 t payload-class growth craft is retained as low-rate engineering/pilot inventory through 1941. It matters because it may cross the vehicle-weight threshold needed for heavier armored/engineer systems; it is not yet a standard landing flotilla.

### Amphibious vehicles
Ka-Mi remains a Navy amphibious-tank program on a near-historical development clock. Existing F-B/soft-ground vehicle experience is a school for cargo transport. Do not resurrect an amphibious Chi-Ha or give an early cargo carrier the later Ka-Tsu name.

### Strategic guard
Improved craft do not erase beach slope, surf, tides, hydrography, mother-ship capacity, unloading congestion or shore logistics. They create options; operational success remains a system/replay problem.
