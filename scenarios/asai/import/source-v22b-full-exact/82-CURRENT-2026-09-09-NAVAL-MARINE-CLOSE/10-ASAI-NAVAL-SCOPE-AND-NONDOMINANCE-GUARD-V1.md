# ASAI NAVAL SCOPE / NON-DOMINANCE GUARD — V14

Asai is not a sovereign planner, Navy dictator, shipyard monopolist or universal prime.

### Direct / strong-domain contributions
- gas turbines, turbochargers and related core/gear/test work;
- materials/process/QC;
- measurement, computation, instrumentation and failure taxonomy;
- selected electrical/generator/control integration;
- joint propulsion and machinery experiments.

### Ripple-domain contributions
Naval architecture, fleet doctrine, radar/ASW, mine warfare, carrier conversion, submarine mission design, landing doctrine and ship procurement remain Navy/yard/service decisions. Asai methods may improve calculation, reliability, sealing, EMI, production repeatability and test speed only where an actual connection exists.

### Prohibited backflow
Do not infer that because Asai knows a future successful technology:
- Navy wants it early;
- the ship can fit it;
- the yard can build it at scale;
- it has enough endurance history;
- it appears in fleet OOB;
- doctrine changes automatically.

### Cheat rule
Future-informed advantage may choose better questions, start long clocks earlier, reject dead-end architectures and instrument the right failure modes. It **cannot skip real corrosion time, fatigue cycles, reducer pitting, actual sea-state behavior, manufacturing yield or fleet maintenance experience.**
