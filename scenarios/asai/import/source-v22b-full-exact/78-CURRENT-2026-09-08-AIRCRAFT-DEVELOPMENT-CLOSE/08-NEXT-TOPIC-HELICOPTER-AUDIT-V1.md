# NEXT TOPIC HANDOFF — HELICOPTER / ROTORCRAFT AUDIT

V11 deliberately does not close the helicopter branch. It only preserves the following starting points for the next audit:

- a separate small piston-helicopter school exists, with two development airframes by 1939 in current/support history;
- a small turboshaft helicopter candidate around E5-S-S ~330 shp normal / ~360 short and ~1.35 t appears in support material as a 1940-41 flight-test / mission-test possibility;
- medium turboshaft rotorcraft stay 1942+ unless a new audit proves otherwise;
- helicopter nomenclature, manufacturer, rotor system, transmission, payload, service role, first flight and production are all to be re-opened from development history, not imported from old branch conclusions;
- rotorcraft transmission demand must be checked against the same precision gear/reducer bottleneck identified in the aircraft/marine industrial audit.

Recommended next sequence:
1. locate all current and superseded rotorcraft source files;
2. separate piston demonstrators from turboshaft demonstrators;
3. reconstruct Army/Navy/civil customer pull and likely manufacturer/arsenal assignment;
4. re-run rotor diameter, disk loading, power loading, transmission mass and hover/forward-flight H->A;
5. close 1937-41 chronology before discussing wartime production.
