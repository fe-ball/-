# V11 AIRCRAFT DEVELOPMENT HANDOFF — 2026-09-08

## Scope
This closeout consolidates the aircraft-development and industrial-feasibility audit conducted after V10. It does not advance combat time.

The audit was triggered by rediscovery of old aircraft branches whose design concepts may still be useful while their names, numbers and performance tables can conflict with current E-family authority.

## Governing method
1. **Development history first.** Restore a research question only if the technology, customer pull and institutional path make sense at that date.
2. **Finished aircraft second.** A concept does not become a prototype merely because an old branch once contained one.
3. **Performance is re-propagated.** Current E5/E6/E7 power/thrust, weight, propeller/reducer and high-lift authority override old performance tables.
4. **Designation is a separate gate.** Identity guards may reserve A7M=Gaifu, A8N=Sakufu, J2N=Hekireki, but those codes are not back-dated into earlier paper/X research.
5. **Production/service is another gate.** Prototype existence is not automatic OOB or combat release.

## Development-school model
Five schools explain most 1937-41 derivatives:
- short-field/STOL: P1 -> P2 -> military STOL and carrier high-lift work;
- pure-J high-speed: E5 single/future-form/HS -> E6 Single and reheat research;
- JF practical high-speed: E5 Twin J->JF -> common E6 Twin and carrier-jet / heavy-interceptor studies;
- shaft/TP: P1/P2/E5-S-T -> X-TP fighter, high-altitude recon and later carrier-attack TP work;
- high-altitude/photo: early photo-recon + low-vibration TP experience -> pressurized TP strategic recon.

## Key closeouts
### E6-B fast-heavy twin
The requirement is current and natural: E5 attack success generates a 1938H2 request for more fuel/payload than the common twin. Army sponsorship is strongest; Navy may study it but already splits toward G4 high-speed torpedo work plus G3M/G4M long range. E6-B stays a separate ~6.7 t, 3-crew, 1.0-1.2 t internal-bomb airframe, not a swollen common E6 Twin.

### Military STOL
P1/P2 are the technical school. A mass military liaison/observation aircraft should normally be cheaper and can be handed to an outside manufacturer; it does not need a 1,000-hp turbine merely because P2 used one.

### Gaifu
The early E5 1,000-shp mass carrier-fighter concept remains rejected. X-aircraft testing survives and is valuable. A serious carrier-fighter requirement re-emerges with E6-S-T. 1941 prototype/service evaluation is plausible; mass service is not pre-recorded.

### Sakufu
Carrier-jet development is reconstructed as a merger of pre-existing high-lift/STOL work and E5/E6 JF experience, not as a reaction to a later foreign aircraft. A dedicated 1941 prototype is plausible. Carrier qualification naturally follows in 1942+.

### Hekireki
Dedicated heavy twin-jet research is plausible after E5 success, but common E6 Single/Twin cover too much of the mission to force a 1941 aircraft. Formal J2N proceeds only if heavy firepower, night/all-weather, radar or other dedicated requirements survive comparison.

### TP high-altitude reconnaissance
The branch is strongly supported by low vibration, kerosene, pressurization and E6-S-N-T altitude performance. Late-1940 first flight and 1941 service test are plausible. The code `Ki-40` remains subject to nomenclature control.

## 1941 endpoint
By 1941-12-31 the package may contain:
- mature/serial E6 Single and common E6 Twin J/JF;
- E6-B service-development/possible adoption depending 1940-41 test gates;
- military STOL in small service if its separate airframe program passes;
- Gaifu in prototype/carrier/service evaluation;
- Sakufu in dedicated land-based prototype / launch-recovery test state, not yet automatic carrier service;
- TP recon in service-test or limited service;
- Hekireki as requirement/preliminary design unless a specific dedicated need is approved;
- only limited E7 retrofit flight articles; no mass E7-dedicated aircraft before the 1942 gate.

## No-backflow
Nothing in this closeout changes the 1939 combat replay or creates 1940/41 combat results. This is technical/development planning authority only.
