## 2. Japan's current strategic gates

- **HOLD / armed neutrality: ACTIVE central branch.** Japan continues to benefit from Soviet-German mutual attrition while retaining freedom of action.
- **N-OPP / northward opportunity: RED.** German success around the Caucasus/Stalingrad creates a real opportunity argument, but Soviet Far Eastern forces are stronger than historical because Japan itself kept them there.
- **N-PREV / preventive northward war: LATENT-YELLOW.** If Soviet victory begins to look likely while the bulk of the Red Army is still tied to Germany, the argument “strike before the victorious Red Army returns east” can become powerful.
- **N-ACC / northern accommodation: LATENT.** If Soviet victory becomes too clear for a profitable Japanese war, Tokyo can instead harden Manchuria and buy a durable northern settlement through neutrality, Sakhalin/fisheries/trade and border arrangements.
- **S-COM / southern commerce: ACTIVE.** Buy oil, rice, rubber, tin and other goods rather than occupying their sources.
- **S-POL / southern political-economic penetration: LIGHT ACTIVE.** Long-term contracts, finance, refining, shipping, port/communications access and influence are acceptable so long as military seizure is not required.
- **S-CRISIS / southern resource crisis: CLOSED.** No comprehensive US/UK/NL strangulation currently exists.
- **S-WAR / southern war: CLOSED.** It can reopen if northern action triggers embargo, or if external policy independently produces financial/oil strangulation and negotiation fails.

## 3. Japan's 1942 strategic paradox

Tokyo can no longer treat “German victory” and “Japanese interest” as synonyms.

Recognized possible outcomes include:
1. prolonged German-Soviet mutual attrition — broadly favorable to Japan;
2. Germany wins in European Russia but an intact Soviet state is compressed eastward into the Urals/Siberia — potentially very bad for Japan;
3. Soviet state collapse while Japan secures Far Eastern faits accomplis — the Army's preferred opportunity outcome;
4. Soviet collapse with German political power reaching deep into Eurasia before Japan secures its own sphere — also dangerous;
5. negotiated German-Soviet peace — dangerous because both major armies become freer;
6. Soviet strategic recovery while Germany still ties down the Red Army — the most dangerous preventive-war decision window;
7. decisive Soviet victory — bad for the Army, but possibly manageable by accommodation and partial US/UK convergence against excessive Soviet expansion.

The next political discussion should not ask only “who wins the war?” It should ask which of these end-states each government recognizes, welcomes, fears, or tries to pre-empt.

## 6. China and economy

The Sino-Japanese War remains officially a non-declared “incident” that ended through the branch peace; absent renewed war, its historical label is not retroactively converted into a formally declared war.

China-proper withdrawal reaches its ~15-month center in August 1942. The peace dividend is real but uneven:
- combat fuel burn falls first;
- shipping returns to trade and domestic carriage;
- oil imports and commercial access continue;
- foreign exchange stabilizes;
- fiscal and labor recovery lag because withdrawal, veterans, the enlarged Kwantung Army and naval/air construction remain expensive.

Japan is not a peacetime economy. It is a heavily armed semi-war economy recovering from the China Incident while sustaining Kantokuen.

## 9. Global war state / Africa

Japan's non-entry prevents the historical 1941-42 global-war merger.

- Singapore, Malaya, Hong Kong, Philippines and Netherlands East Indies remain outside Japanese wartime occupation.
- Prince of Wales and Repulse survive because Force Z is not attacked by Japan.
- The United States remains formally nonbelligerent; Germany does not receive the historical 11 Dec 1941 trigger for a US declaration of war.

- No historical Japanese Indian Ocean raid. A 1942 British Madagascar invasion loses its main urgency and is not an automatic event.
- A historical-size Operation Torch is not available while the United States remains formally outside the war.

These Africa/Med branches are WORKING-REPLAY and remain open to dedicated theater audit if they become a primary topic.
