## Replay guards

### No historical-event autopilot

Do not auto-import:
- South Indochina occupation;
- July 1941 full asset freeze/oil embargo;
- Pearl Harbor/Malaya/Philippines/Hong Kong/DEI war;
- German 11 Dec US declaration;
- Tojo Cabinet in Oct 1941;
- Midway/Coral Sea carrier losses;
- Madagascar invasion;
- Torch;
- Vichy-France/Toulon consequences tied specifically to Torch;
- wartime Unryu emergency-mass-production logic.

These require their own worldline triggers.

### Soviet transfer guard

Never say “the Siberian divisions stay/go” as a single block. Use specific unit/date/sector transfer decisions.

### Japanese OOB guard

`ordered`, `moving`, `arrived`, `integrated`, and `ready` are distinct states. The two divisions ordered north on 24 Aug 1942 are not automatically counted in the 31 Aug ready OOB.

### Technology guard

`physical`, `accepted`, `service-released`, `assigned`, `ready`, `combat-present` remain distinct.

- E7 ground success is not a fleet E7 engine.
- A7M/A8N prototypes are not service squadrons.
- H2 helicopter military trial is not a helicopter unit.
- combined-cycle workbands are not sea-qualified plants.
- ideal E7 leader study is not procurement.

### Naval hydrodynamics guard

“No magic” bars uncaused bonuses; it does not force a worldline ship to have exactly historical underwater lines/propeller/efficiency where worldline design/testing could causally improve them.

### Political-perception guard

Next politics layer must keep objective events separate from:
- intelligence estimates;
- leader beliefs;
- bureaucratic planning assumptions;
- public/diplomatic claims.

Do not convert a leader's optimistic prediction into world truth.

### US/UK trigger guard

Tokyo does not know a secret exact US/UK trigger table. An ambiguous Roosevelt/Churchill statement can be interpreted, misinterpreted or cherry-picked by factions; it is not automatic proof of nonintervention or intervention.

### Economy guard

Peace dividend is real but not instant peacetime. Kantokuen, fleet/air building, withdrawal costs, rationing/price controls and accumulated inflation remain.
