## Core V7 changes
- E4/E5 aviation chronology is rebaselined earlier and more mature than old V6.
- E4 Twin No.2 (Army) is actually used in North China in 1937; E4 Twin No.3 (Navy) is actually used from Shanghai onward.
- Aircraft radio/EMI improvement begins from the 1932–33 E4 control experiment and spreads through 1934–37.
- Air-ground cooperation and photo reconnaissance are therefore materially better by 1937, but not modern FAC/CAS.
- E4 Twin combat success causes E4 additional trial production and earlier/more serious E5 Twin evaluation procurement.
- Independent GT production and T-1 chronology are moved earlier; T-1A/B are already operating by 1937, T-1C is the expansion under construction.
- 1937 China operations are replayed using old history as baseline plus causal deltas, with enemy feedback and no “ghost” units.
- Chinese retreat equipment accounting is changed from repeated percentage penalties to a stock-flow model.
- Japanese equipment advantage is separated into inventory, readiness, recovery, manpower/operator availability, production and unit performance.
- Panay remains sunk, but improved airborne radio likely shortens follow-on attack; diplomatic core remains.
- 1937 major capture dates still remain almost entirely SAME-DATE, despite many hour-scale operational changes.
