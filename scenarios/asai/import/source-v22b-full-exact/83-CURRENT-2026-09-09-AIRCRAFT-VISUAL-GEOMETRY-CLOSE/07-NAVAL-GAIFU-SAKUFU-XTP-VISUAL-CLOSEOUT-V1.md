# X-TP / A7M GAIFU / A8N SAKUFU VISUAL CLOSEOUT V1

## X-TP research family
A single `X-TP reference test configuration` is established so CG work can depict the 1938–40 research school without retroactively naming it A7M. It is 9.35 m long, 11.40 m span, 23.0 m², tailwheel, four-blade E5-S-T prop, conservative low tapered carrier wing and optional small outer-tip fold on carrier-test airframes. It remains a research-family visual reference, not a production type.

## A7M Gaifu
Locked dimensions: **9.65 m length / 12.00 m span / 25.0 m²**. The wing has 2.90 m root and 1.267 m tip chord, 3° quarter-chord sweep, 5.5° dihedral and -1.5° washout. Outer 0.55 m tips fold upward at y=5.45 m, giving a folded horizontal width of about 10.90 m. Automatic slats occupy the outer lifting portion, with inboard Fowler/slotted flaps.

The E6-S-T installation is a slim turboprop nose, not a radial-engine cowling: 3.35 m four-blade propeller, ~0.72 m spinner, compact chin inlet and side exhaust slots. The gear is inward-retracting main gear plus tailwheel; track 3.55 m. Carrier hook is permanent prototype hardware. CG service-center armament shows two wing 20 mm-class cannon and two smaller nose guns; the later four-20 mm growth fit is a variant, not the 1941 default.

## A8N Sakufu
Locked dimensions: **11.40 m length / 13.20 m span / 31.0 m²**. The wing is explicitly two-segment rather than a generic trapezoid: root chord 3.45 m, chord ~2.32 m at the fold, tip chord 1.15 m; major fold axis y=3.50 m. Outer panels fold almost vertical, producing an approximately **7.0 m folded width**.

The two E6-M-JF nacelles sit at y≈±2.25 m. Baseline nacelle OD is ~0.70 m. The aft body visibly separates core exhaust from the lower cold-bypass cascade. The cascade has 0/20/45° mechanical states and redirects only bypass air toward/over the Fowler flap. A CG showing the hot core nozzle pivoting is wrong.

A8N uses tricycle carrier gear, main track 4.65 m, a large arresting hook, leading-edge slats interrupted at the nacelles, large inboard Fowler flaps and drooping ailerons in the folding outer panels. The service-target gun nose has four 20 mm-class apertures. No hover/VL hardware is permitted.
