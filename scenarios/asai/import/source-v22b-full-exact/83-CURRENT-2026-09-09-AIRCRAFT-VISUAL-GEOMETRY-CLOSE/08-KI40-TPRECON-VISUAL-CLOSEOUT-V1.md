# KI-40 TP HIGH-ALTITUDE RECON VISUAL CLOSEOUT V1

The visual reference is a **10.50 m long / 15.00 m span / 28.0 m²** Mitsubishi two-seat pressurized reconnaissance aircraft. It is deliberately slender: maximum fuselage width 0.98 m, low high-aspect-ratio wing, conventional tailwheel gear and a clean E6-S-N-T turboprop nose.

The wing uses 2.55 m root / 1.183 m tip chord, 1° quarter-chord sweep, 5° dihedral and -1.4° washout. The four-blade propeller is 3.55 m diameter with a ~0.70 m spinner. A small chin inlet and lateral exhausts keep the nose smooth rather than giving it a radial-style annular cowling.

The pressure cabin is tandem and compact, with heavier framing/seals than a normal openable fighter canopy. The canonical airframe is unarmed. The rear lower fuselage carries one vertical and paired oblique optical windows. Gun ports, dorsal positions and fighter-style nose glazing are prohibited in the default OML.
