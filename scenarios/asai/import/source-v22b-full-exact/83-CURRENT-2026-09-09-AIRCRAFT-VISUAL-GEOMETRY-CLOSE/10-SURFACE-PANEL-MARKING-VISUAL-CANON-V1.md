# SURFACE / PANEL / MARKING VISUAL CANON V1

## Purpose
Lock a common 1935–41 visual language without pretending that every serial-specific rivet or paint batch has historical authority.

## Metal aircraft
- stressed-skin duralumin uses visible panel joints but restrained rivet relief; CG rivets should not be exaggerated into modern raised bolts;
- high-speed E5/E6 and Mitsubishi reconnaissance/fighter surfaces favor flush/countersunk treatment on leading/high-q zones, ordinary flush/low-profile riveting elsewhere;
- control surfaces may retain fabric on early/research blocks where stated; A8N and the fastest E6 blocks should render major controls as metal-skinned unless a serial-specific reference says otherwise;
- removable engine/module panels have regular fastener lines and clear split planes matching the gas-generator / aft-module architecture.

## Plant-fiber / bonded sandwich research
Material No.1/2 should have visibly broader smooth panels, fewer longitudinal rivet rows, bonded seams, metal hard-point inserts and conspicuous inspection/coupon repair patches. Do not make them carbon-fiber weave shells.

## Glazing
All glazing is flat or singly-curved period manufacture where possible. Avoid modern frameless bubble canopies unless the airframe entry specifically calls for a raised/bubble-like canopy; even then retain structural frames.

## Finish defaults for CG
These are **render defaults, not service-history authority**:
- Asai company/research airframes: dull natural metal or light neutral protective finish, black test stencils and red/orange datum/measurement marks as needed;
- Army/Navy prototype/evaluation renders: use a period-plausible prototype high-visibility or service finish appropriate to the depicted date, but do not encode an exact tactical unit unless HISTORY/OOB authority supplies it;
- Material test articles: light neutral finish favored for crack/repair inspection with large research numbers/measurement grids;
- rotorcraft research articles: light neutral/aluminum fabric/metal mix with high-visibility rotor-tip tracking marks.

## Prohibited visual anachronisms
No area-ruled coke-bottle fuselages, all-moving tails, swept wings justified by postwar transonic theory, modern NACA flush inlets, stealth shaping, digital sensor fairings, composite weave textures, zero-frame panoramic helicopter glazing, or modern low-profile landing gear doors unless separately qualified.
