# P1 / P2 / MATERIAL AIRFRAME VISUAL CLOSEOUT V1

## P1
P1 is the 1935–37 practical-product airframe, not a retroactive P2. The visual reference is a 9.15 m long, 11.80 m span, 23.5 m² high-wing utility turboprop with fixed tailwheel gear, one lift strut per side, simple slotted flaps and no full-span slat. The nose carries the E5-S-T gearbox/propeller installation and a 3.10 m four-blade propeller. The cabin is narrower/shorter than P2 and is visually a practical 4–5-place machine rather than an extreme STOL aircraft.

## P2
P2 is a new 10.00 m x 13.00 m / 27.0 m² STOL OML. It uses a fixed full-span leading-edge slat, large inboard Fowler/slotted flaps, mechanically drooping ailerons, long-stroke fixed gear with 3.85 m track and a 3.25 m four-blade propeller. The cabin is broad enough for side-by-side front crew and 5–7 occupants. Normal production OML has no arresting hook; the 1940 deck demonstrator's hook is a removable research conversion.

## Material No.1
Material No.1 is visually **the same P2 aerodynamic OML** by design: an A/B structural test requires the shape to stay the same. The CG discriminator is construction, not silhouette: large smooth bonded sandwich panels, fewer exposed rivet rows, research coupon/inspection patches and obvious removable metal fire-wall/engine-mount/gear hard points.

## Material No.2
Material No.2 is the opposite experiment: 8.60 m long, 7.60 m span, 9.5 m² straight mid-wing, a deliberately full 1.10 m fuselage diameter, rear E5-J and paired cheek inlets. It should look stubby and structurally over-bodied, not like a service fighter. Its thin high-q wing, tricycle gear, instrument nose and lack of weapons are locked.

## Material No.3 / AT-3
AT-3 is a design-freeze, not a flown 1941 aircraft. The CG reference is 35.5 m long, 48.0 m span, 240 m² high wing, non-pressurized 4.10 m outside-width cargo fuselage, four E6-M-T turboprops with 3.80 m props, fuselage-side multiwheel main gear and a rear lower ramp/upper door. It is visually a cargo machine first; no bomber glazing or gun positions are allowed by default.
