# E5 / E6 JET FAMILY VISUAL CLOSEOUT V1

## Architecture progression
The single-engine line deliberately shows development continuity without making every aircraft the same shell:

`E5 future-form` -> `Ki-42 service reference` + `HS-2 high-q research` -> `E6 Single`.

The shared visual language is forward cockpit + free nose + paired cheek/side inlets + central/rear engine + conventional fin with the horizontal tail placed high enough to remain clear of the hot core exhaust. Each step receives its own wing and fuselage OML.

### E5 future-form
8.75 m x 9.80 m / 15.5 m². Clean unarmed company testbed, tricycle gear, low-mid wing, narrow nose, side inlet lips and removable instrumentation panels. It is the CG reference for the late-1937 rear-installation/CG/thermal school.

### Ki-42
8.55 m x 10.25 m / 15.8 m². This is a **new visual-reference closure**, not a claim that a historical production drawing was recovered. It is a slightly more serviceable, broader-winged derivative of the same architecture. The default canonical CG is clean-nosed; fighter gun-port and recon-camera noses are detachable service-local variants inside the same forward volume.

### HS-2
8.55 m x 8.80 m / 13.0 m². Thin mid-wing, instrument nose/pitot boom, tricycle gear, E6-S-J rear jet. It must look like a dedicated compressibility research aircraft: sparse fittings, no weapon ports, visibly smaller wing than the service E6 Single.

### E6 Single
9.20 m x 10.25 m / 17.5 m². The old 14.2 m² visual proxy is superseded. The fuselage retains a free solid nose and forward cockpit, with large paired side inlets feeding the E6-M-J behind the cockpit. Fighter-CG default uses four visible generic blast apertures (two cannon-class, two smaller gun-class) without asserting a particular Army/Navy weapon mark; recon-CG uses a clean camera nose.

## Twin line

### E5 Twin J/JF
10.15 m x 12.00 m / 22.8 m². Two-seat tandem, low wing, tricycle gear. J and JF versions share fuselage/wing hard points; the JF has a visibly longer and fuller aft nacelle. A short central internal mission-bay door set is part of the OML.

### E6 Twin J/JF
9.95 m x 10.90 m / 19.0 m². This is a smaller, thinner, higher-wing-loading redesign rather than a stretched E5 Twin. Two-seat tandem canopy, six-port common forward firing envelope, tricycle gear with mains into nacelle/wing-root bays, and a ~0.40 t central mission bay are locked. JF nacelles use the 0.70 m-class aft-fan body; J nacelles are shorter/narrower.

### E6-B
E6-B does not share the common E6 Twin fuselage. Kawasaki's OML is 11.60 m x 13.50 m / 28.0 m², with three crew, 5.10 m main-track tricycle gear, 2.6 m internal bomb bay and only limited low-forward glazing. The rear station may mount a retractable/socket defensive weapon, but a large dorsal or ventral turret is prohibited by the visual canon.
