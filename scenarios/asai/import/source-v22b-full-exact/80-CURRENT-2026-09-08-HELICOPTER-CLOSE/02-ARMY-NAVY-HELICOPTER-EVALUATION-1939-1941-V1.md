# ARMY / NAVY HELICOPTER EVALUATION — 1939-1941

The military response is closed as **formal evaluation and next-generation sponsorship**, not immediate service adoption.

## 1. Army path

### 1939H1-H2
The Army aviation technical side and artillery/communications users move from observation to formal flight demonstration and mission evaluation.

H1 test questions:
- command liaison over broken terrain;
- radio relay;
- observer insertion/recovery;
- short local reconnaissance/observation;
- river/mountain obstacle bypass;
- autorotation and field-maintenance practicality.

The Army does **not** treat H1 as an economical replacement for fixed-wing liaison aircraft or autogyros. Its unique value is vertical placement/recovery of people where even a short strip is unavailable.

### Resulting Army requirement
H1 exposes the real military deficiency: two people + radio with hot/high margin, a third person when needed, and litter evacuation. That requirement directly supports H2.

## 2. Navy path

The Navy begins with technical observation, then shipboard-feasibility work rather than immediate shipboard deployment.

Evaluation priorities:
- base/ship liaison;
- aircrew rescue;
- harbor/anchorage utility;
- deck wind and rotor clearance;
- tie-down and rotor start/stop safety;
- corrosion/salt exposure;
- simple rescue hoist and emergency flotation;
- large-deck/support-ship operation before any small-combatant claim.

H1 is not assigned to arbitrary destroyers or cruisers. Rotor diameter, ship motion, superstructure clearance, deck personnel safety and maintenance remain binding.

## 3. Closed chronology

| period | Army | Navy |
|---|---|---|
| through 1938 | observe / no production order | observe / no production order |
| 1939Q2-Q3 | formal H1 demonstration and mission study | technical attendance/observation |
| 1939Q3-Q4 | mission-evaluation funding; next-machine requirements | shipboard-feasibility research funding |
| 1940H1 | H1 two-person/radio/observer trials | simulated-deck, wind, tie-down and clearance work |
| 1940H2 | sponsors H2 Army mission article | sponsors H2 Navy rescue/deck article |
| 1941Q1-Q2 | H2 free-flight / Army mission tests | H2 land-based naval-kit tests |
| 1941Q3-Q4 | liaison, third-person/litter, terrain trials | large-deck/rescue/liaison demonstration |
| 1941YE | promising development; no ordinary service OOB | promising development; no ordinary service OOB |

## 4. Funding/procurement boundary

- H1: military buys **test access, instrumentation and mission kits**, not a production batch.
- H2: Army and Navy each sponsor one mission-specific development airframe within the four-airframe H2 program.
- No 1941 standard squadron/unit establishment is created.
- Any real-world mission use during trials remains operational evaluation, not routine combat/OOB service.
