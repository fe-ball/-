# KAYABA INDEPENDENT ROTORCRAFT INTERACTION GUARD — V13

## Current rule
Kayaba is not an Asai subordinate, mandatory prime contractor or fixed helicopter partner.

Kayaba may continue its own autogyro/rotorcraft research according to its own technical priorities. Asai may continue its own helicopter line. The Army/Navy may convene research meetings, technical comparison trials and exchange non-proprietary safety/test data.

Reasonable shared discussion topics include:
- rotor airfoil/test methods;
- flapping and lead/lag behavior;
- ground-resonance incidents and damping practice;
- tracking and balance;
- bearing/blade fatigue inspection;
- prerotation/jump-takeoff experience;
- landing-gear damping and rotorcraft ground handling;
- instrumentation and vibration-measurement practice.

The following are **not implied**:
- a fixed `airframe/rotor = Kayaba, engine = Asai` division of labor;
- automatic transfer of proprietary hub/transmission/control drawings;
- automatic Kayaba production of Asai helicopters;
- automatic Asai propulsion installation in every Kayaba rotorcraft;
- early mass Kayaba autogyro inventory derived from old support files.

## Superseded support claims
Where `asai-works-rotorcraft-v46.md` or older support asserts mandatory Kayaba prime status, fixed Asai/Kayaba division, or early worldline mass service as a precondition for the Asai helicopter line, V13 supersedes those statements.

V13 does not set a new numerical Kayaba 1938-41 inventory. That independent branch requires its own evidence if exact procurement/OOB numbers become relevant.
