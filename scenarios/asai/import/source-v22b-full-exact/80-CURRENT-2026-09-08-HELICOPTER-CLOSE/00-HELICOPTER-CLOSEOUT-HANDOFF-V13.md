# HELICOPTER / ROTORCRAFT CLOSEOUT HANDOFF — V13

V13 closes the **Asai prewar helicopter line through 1941 planning/development state**. It does not advance any combat clock and does not create wartime helicopter combat results.

## Highest-authority conclusions

1. The paid helicopter history remains continuous: preliminary work late 1932, formal Asai in-house research in 1933, rotor/hub/transmission rigs in 1934, tethered lift and hops in 1935, free flight in 1936, Asai 180-200 hp gasoline-engine installation and maturation in 1937-39.
2. The early main architecture is single main rotor + tail rotor. Foreknowledge reduces configuration search, not durability/control/reliability work.
3. On 1939-04-20 exactly **two Asai piston-helicopter development airframes** are retained. They are research aircraft, not a customer/service fleet.
4. V13 closes the Apr1939 mature piston-machine working geometry/performance as the H1 audit configuration. `H1` is an audit label only, not a military designation.
5. Army and Navy interest becomes formal mission/feasibility evaluation during 1939H2. Neither service receives an H1 production fleet.
6. The next machine is the small E5-S-S-derived turboshaft H2: 330 shp normal / 360 shp short, ~1.35 t design gross, 11.2 m three-blade rotor. Free-flight and mission-test work occurs in 1941.
7. V13 closes **four H2 development airframes by 1941 year-end**: Asai flight/endurance, Army mission-evaluation, Navy rescue/deck-evaluation, and instrumented/backup/transmission article. No ordinary service OOB.
8. H3 medium growth is a ~3.2 t / 15 m / 900-1,000 shp-class **1941 design/rig outlook only**. No H3 flight airframe exists by 1941 year-end.
9. Kayaba remains an **independent rotorcraft/autogyro research actor**. Research meetings, military comparison trials and data exchange are allowed; forced Asai/Kayaba joint design or a fixed `airframe=Kayaba / engine=Asai` architecture is superseded.
10. Old support claims of early mass Kayaba autogyro deployment, forced Kayaba prime status, or medium helicopter service before the closed gates are provenance/comparator only unless separately re-closed.
