# NAVAL FCS / COMPUTATION CLASS CLOSEOUT — V9 TECHCLOSE2

## Status

**1939 CLASS-LEVEL INTEGRATION BASELINES: CLOSED.**

**INDIVIDUAL SHIP EXCEPTIONS, DAMAGE/REFIT EVENTS, NEW RADAR FITS AND LATER WARTIME MODIFICATIONS: EVENT-GATED.**

This closeout translates earlier naval working decisions into the current `FCS0-FCS4` + `R0-R3` framework and applies `CD0-CD4`. It does not make Asai a permanent member of the Naval Technical Department.

## 1. Institutional basis

By the late 1920s the Navy/shipbuilding side is already a customer for card and scientific calculation. By 1930-33 naval design and gunnery use is established; Tomozuru and the Fourth Fleet expand the scope and budget rather than creating the capability from zero. By 1936-39 routine naval calculation, gunnery calculation and integration work is treated as **CD3 autonomous institutional practice**.

Asai remains a CD2 supplier/support interface for machines, generic calculation forms, electromechanical workmanship, calibration/QC and selected joint tests. The Navy's own designers and gunnery specialists determine ship arrangements, director logic and doctrine.

## 2. Meaning of the class baselines

`R0-R3` is an **integration depth**, not a claim that every subsystem is advanced.

- `R1 general / R2 selected` means the ship has broad wiring/transmission/synchronization discipline plus one or more director/AA/switchboard chains integrated more deeply.
- `R3 infrastructure` on a new-build/major testbed means foundations, routing, power/space and interface reserves are designed with the system in mind. It does not assert a later radar or future FCS computer is fitted.

Function-specific `FCS` maturity remains controlled by V8 51/51A and historical hardware anchors.

## 3. Carrier baselines at 1939

- **Kaga:** `R1 general; R2 selected AA`. Its 1934-35 modernization absorbs calculation/calibration and electrical-transmission improvements, but the AA branch remains anchored to the earlier fitted high-angle system. No generic FCS3 or radar.
- **Akagi:** `R1 general; R2 AA/target-allocation selected`. The 1935-38 modernization occurs late enough to absorb the more mature transmission/synchronization/switchboard practice. This is deeper integration than Kaga, not a future radar/computer fit.
- **Soryu:** `R1 general; R2 fitted-AA chain`. New-build integration avoids some retrofit compromises, but its equipment maturity remains type/date anchored.
- **Hiryu:** `R1 general; R2 selected, more mature coordination than Soryu`. It benefits from the later design/fitting cycle; no automatic FCS3/radar.
- **Hosho:** `R0 general; selected R1`. Space/topweight and old architecture make broad R2 modernization unattractive.
- **Ryujo:** `R1 selected`. Wiring/calibration/transmission improvements are possible, but topweight/space constraints block a blanket R2 claim.
- **Shokaku/Zuikaku class:** during 1939 design/build, `R3 infrastructure` is CLOSED as a design/fitting property. The class may reserve foundations, routes, power and interface capacity for contemporary FCS/electrical equipment. This is not an operational 1939 ship, not radar adoption, and not automatic FCS3.

## 4. Battleship baselines

- **Hiei:** 1937-40 modernization/testbed is `R3 infrastructure` for FCS/electrical/power/DC/communications integration. Actual main-battery chain remains within the current FCS1/FCS2 interpretation; AA may use selected FCS2-class integration. No radar inference.
- **Kongo/Haruna/Kirishima:** `R1 general; R2 selected` after their modernization sequence. Do not copy Hiei's R3 testbed status fleet-wide.
- **Nagato:** `R1 general; R2 selected fire-control/test chain` where the modernization/test role pays for it.
- **Mutsu:** `R1 general`, with no blanket assumption that all Nagato test features are mirrored.
- **Yamato/A-140:** `R3 infrastructure at design/build stage` for power, routing, spaces, foundations and contemporary director/FCS interfaces. This does not create operational 1939 equipment, radar, or a future integrated combat system.

## 5. Cruiser/destroyer baselines

- **Mogami/Mikuma:** `R1 general` after correction/refit work. Their structural/welding history is not erased by computation.
- **Suzuya/Kumano:** `R1 general`, with cleaner calculation/integration practice than the earlier pair; selected R2 subsystems require an actual director/equipment fit.
- **Tone/Chikuma:** `R1 general`; Chikuma may have more mature calibration/wiring/synchronization execution within R1 because of the later fitting cycle. Do not invent a new director solely to make it R2.
- **Asashio/Kagero-era new destroyers:** `R1 general` for contemporary data transmission/wiring/torpedo-FC integration. `R2` remains selected-equipment/event specific.
- **older destroyers:** `R0/R1` according to refit depth; no fleet-wide modernization jump.

## 6. Fire-control effect accounting

For capital-ship main battery, historical mechanical fire control is already strong. The worldline difference should usually appear in calibration, reset delay, stable reference, transmission, switchboard/target designation, maintainability and reduced integration error rather than a generic accuracy or range multiplier.

For medium AA, the same institutional machinery can matter more because continuous tracking, angle transmission, fuze setting, follow-up and reset delay form a more integration-sensitive chain. Gun mount, fuze, feed, training rate and director hardware still limit results.

For torpedo fire control, computation/transmission may reduce target-motion/gyro-setting/reset error; torpedo hardware is unchanged unless separately closed.

## 7. Radar stop line

None of the class baselines in this file installs radar. Later radar requires its own type/date/quantity/power/antenna/director/training closure. R3 infrastructure may make later installation easier without backdating the equipment.

## 8. Provenance and precedence

This file promotes only the durable class-level decisions from prior naval working material, especially the 2026-08-29 handoff notes, and expresses them under current V9 guards. Where it conflicts with a later explicit ship/refit/combat event, the later local event wins.
