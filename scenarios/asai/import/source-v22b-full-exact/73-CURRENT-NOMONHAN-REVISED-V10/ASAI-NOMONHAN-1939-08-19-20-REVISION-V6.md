## 4. Soviet masking and the night 19–20 August

The Soviet side is not passive.

It already understands persistent Japanese aerial reconnaissance and therefore:
- moves major elements at night / in poor visibility;
- uses dispersion / concealment;
- uses deception / dummy activity;
- final crossings / jump-off movement exploit darkness.

Japanese reconnaissance detects the *scale and direction* of buildup better, but does not see the full final deployment.

The Soviet attack timetable is not changed.

## 5. Japanese actions on the evening of 19 August

No preemptive general attack is ordered.

Actions:
1. all front-line units to full stand-to before dawn;
2. disperse aircraft, vehicles, vulnerable headquarters and artillery-support vehicles;
3. move nonessential rear / wounded / excess stores eastward during the night;
4. keep essential forward ammunition and guns in place;
5. pre-position 4th Tank Regiment in concealed southern outer-reserve release geometry;
6. retain 3rd Tank Regiment as inner reserve;
7. place the 7th Division reinforced battalion-group as an eastern/southern second-line / corridor shoulder, not in the front trench line;
8. allocate its 4 Type94 37 mm AT guns **72 AT-37 rounds total (18/gun)** from existing theater stock before the battle;
9. establish conditional line-shortening triggers if either flank crosses specified phase lines.

This is readiness, not an ordered retreat.
