## 10. Soviet understanding / adaptation

- E5 is a high-speed recon / attack family;
- same-type high cover exists;
- Japanese air reconnaissance is persistent and BDA is used.

Countermeasures:
- higher / earlier CAP around key routes / airfields;
- more dummy vehicle parks / artillery points;
- movement concentrated at night / poor weather;
- more frequent artillery displacement;
- fuel / maintenance nodes dispersed.

These work.

Cost:
- additional aircraft-hours / pilot fatigue in CAP;
- local handling / concentration penalties;
- more night movement / tractor mileage.

The Japanese readiness buildup is itself partly observable.
Soviet response:
- more attention to likely tank-release / reserve areas;
- planned artillery / air suppression against Japanese depth positions receives somewhat greater emphasis.

Thus early Japanese warning is not a free one-way advantage.
