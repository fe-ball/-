# ASAI 1937 Manchuria Site Audit V2

Cutoff: 1937-07-27 worldline current date.

## Main correction
Dairen is operationally part of the Greater Manchurian Asai/SMR network but legally/economically belongs to the Kwantung Leased Territory, not Manchukuo proper. Therefore direct Dairen value added must not be added to the Manchukuo national-income denominator. The earlier +0.44% central Manchukuo uplift is retained only as an approximate audit result; territorial correction suggests ~+0.42% as a cleaner center, with a broad +0.2% to +0.7% band.

## Deployment topology
Greater Manchurian operating area central allocation: 34 computation FSE, 100 exhaust-turbo annual flow, ~10 independent GT customer deliveries. Manchukuo proper after subtracting Dairen direct deployments: ~28 FSE, ~88 turbo units/year, ~8 independent GT cores/year.

Dairen: primary port/SMR/warehouse/service hub. Best Asai channels are punched-card logistics, port/rail inventory reconciliation, customs/shipping statistics, workshop tooling and some industrial/emergency power. Direct Dairen output is excluded from Manchukuo NI.

Mukden/Fengtian: primary inland Asai hub. The Tiehsi machinery/metals district, commercial concentration, rail distribution and power administration make it the most natural combined computation + tooling + turbo service center in Manchukuo proper.

Fushun: highest turbo/tooling intensity. Historical 1937 coal output was 9.8 Mt. The meaningful worldline effects are lower equipment downtime, better spare-part identification, workshop throughput, pump/generator reliability and mine-haulage engine performance, not a magical increase in reserves or mine face capacity.

Anshan: highest QC/process-engineering intensity. Showa Steel was already operating three blast furnaces and about 1,350 t/day pig iron by late 1936 after process improvements. Asai effects should focus on gauges, tool steels/carbides, heat treatment, machine-shop consistency, maintenance records and limited technical calculation rather than large immediate furnace-capacity additions.

Hsinking: low machinery intensity but high administrative network value. The capital's five-year construction program and central agencies create demand for card systems, standardized forms/codes, project tracking, budgets, stores and communications records. Its productivity effect is mostly realized through other sectors/projects.

Harbin: northern rail/logistics/communications hub. Best fit for refurbished leased calculation equipment, stores/railway card systems, electrical maintenance, diesel/turbo service and cold-weather auxiliary power. Do not treat it as a Fushun/Anshan-scale heavy-industry concentration.

## Computation interpretation
FSE means standard full-system-equivalent annual punched-card/office processing capacity. A site with 5 FSE may have many separate punches, verifiers, sorters, tabulators and printers plus service-bureau work. High-end technical relay calculation rooms are far rarer: Greater Manchurian central estimate ~3 sites/rooms, likely Dairen + Anshan + one of Mukden/Hsinking.

## North-China transfer ceiling
By 1937-07-27, a conservative supply-side ceiling is that Manchurian/Kwantung formations and support organizations could release roughly 20-40 turbo-equipped heavy trucks/tractors/support vehicles without serious local stripping, perhaps 30-60 under urgent draw. This is a WORKING ceiling, not an assertion of exact deployed serials. Current event-gate outcomes stay unchanged: rail is still the strategic bottleneck; fuel, tires, drivers, roads, horse transport and late orders remain constraints; the 26 trucks at Guang'anmen are not automatically Asai-equipped.
