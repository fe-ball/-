# 1937-07-17 evening → 1937-07-20 21:00 — local compliance / renewed fighting gate

## Closure

**CLOSED-SAME in event sequence.** By July 1937, Asai-derived computation, survey reduction, firing-table work and QC have made Japanese artillery practice somewhat more mature than the historical baseline. That difference is real, but the 20 July exchange is strongly coercive/punitive rather than a sustained fire-for-effect battle, so it does not produce a radically larger result. Negotiations, the July 18 rail-reconnaissance air incident, the July 19 implementation agreement, and the renewed July 20 fighting remain unchanged in sequence.

## Historical / branch sequence

### 18 July — Soong Che-yuan at Tientsin
- 16 July talks had already produced a provisional understanding between Wachi and Chang Tzu-chung.
- At about 13:00 on 18 July, Soong Che-yuan visited Lt. Gen. Katsuki under the form of a courtesy call and expressed regret over the incident. Japanese sources treated this as the requested apology; Chinese presentation treated it as a courtesy visit with an expression of regret.
- This semantic difference is preserved. The visit and partial compliance occur in the current branch.

### 18 July — air reconnaissance / troop-train firing on the Peiping–Hankow line
- Japanese military aircraft reconnoitering northbound military trains around the Changteh/Xingtai sector were fired on from a train and returned fire; casualties among soldiers/civilians were publicized and anti-Japanese feeling worsened.
- Some later accounts describe two Japanese aircraft, but the exact aircraft type/unit is not closed here. In particular, do **not** automatically assign Ki-15 / Type 97 Command Reconnaissance Aircraft.
- Asai-method effects on piston-engine reliability do not remove a historically completed reconnaissance sortie, and there is no established engine/airframe failure gate. Event is retained.

### 19 July — Soong returns to Peiping; implementation details signed
- About 05:00, firing near Ichimonjiyama wounded one Japanese soldier.
- Soong returned to Peiping at about 09:40 and consulted Ch'in Te-ch'un, Feng Chih-an and others.
- At about 20:00, Matsui warned the Hopei-Chahar side that renewed unlawful firing after noon on 20 July would release the Japanese side to take "free action."
- At about 23:00, Hashimoto and the 29th Army representatives (Chang Tzu-chung / Chang Yun-jung) concluded detailed implementation terms for the third article of the 11 July agreement: suppression of Communist / anti-Japanese organizations, removal of officials judged incompatible with cooperation, suppression of anti-Japanese propaganda/education, etc. Withdrawal of the 37th Division from Peiping was to be carried out voluntarily by the Chinese side and notified separately.
- Same in the current branch; no Asai causal path enters the bargaining content.

### 19–20 July — both sides continue military preparation
- Chinese central / allied reinforcements continue moving north through Shihchiachuang and along the Peiping–Hankow axis.
- Soong is simultaneously attempting to buy time, comply where possible, and prepare the 29th Army for a possible war.
- Japanese reinforcement remains visible. Better Asai vehicle readiness slightly increases usable motor-vehicle fraction after arrival, but not enough to change Soong's strategic inference: the decisive facts are the existence of reinforcement and Nanking's support, not a few percent of truck readiness.

### 20 July morning — another apparent path toward settlement
- Soong ordered 37th Division elements around Peiping to concentrate at Hsiyuan by 21 July in preparation for movement.
- In afternoon talks he proposed moving the 37th Division toward Paoting and replacing it in Peiping with the 132nd Division.
- By evening he informed the Japanese side that a responsible battalion commander had been dismissed, the 19 July detailed terms would be implemented, and front-line withdrawal would begin by noon on 21 July.
- This apparent renewed de-escalation is retained.

### 20 July afternoon/evening — renewed Marco Polo Bridge fighting
- Japan had already given notice that renewed firing after noon would result in "free action."
- Around 14:30, firing from the Chinese side in the Marco Polo Bridge sector was followed by Japanese return fire; Japanese artillery initiated the artillery response.
- After 15:00, dozens of rounds were fired from the west bank toward Ichimonjiyama; Japanese casualties were reported.
- Around 17:00 artillery fire temporarily stopped.
- Around 19:30, Changhsintien-side fire concentrated on the Japanese combat headquarters at Wulitian.
- The Japanese side conducted punitive artillery fire against Wanping, damaging/destroying the southeast watchtower and gate. Chinese records report wounded troops including regimental commander Chi Hsing-wen plus security-force and civilian casualties; exact casualty totals are treated as report-dependent rather than hard branch constants.

## Branch consequence

The important consequence is political rather than a material-performance delta. The Japanese punitive shelling of Wanping occurs at the same time that Soong is visibly implementing parts of the settlement. This reinforces the contradiction noted in the historical record: top-level de-escalation and front-line coercion operate simultaneously. Since the shelling itself is not removed in the current branch, its effect on Soong's willingness to continue partial compromise is also retained.

## End state — 20 July about 21:00

- Local settlement implementation is still nominally alive.
- The 37th Division is being prepared for relocation.
- Both sides continue reinforcing/preparing.
- A serious artillery clash has just occurred around Marco Polo Bridge / Wanping.
- Artillery practice is modestly more mature because computation/survey/QC have improved, but the limited coercive intent of this exchange prevents that technical advantage from becoming a large damage/casualty divergence.
- No clear Asai-caused event divergence has yet occurred.
- Next gate: 21–22 July — renewed withdrawal, Tianjin Army opposition to additional home divisions, residual 37th Division noncompliance, railway repair and transport.
