# Q-052 — 1937-10-05 to 1937-10-10 — Zhengding / Shijiazhuang / Wenzaobin Gate

## Status
CLOSED-SAME-CALENDAR / CLOSED-MINOR-HOUR-SHIFT / CLOSED-NO-SHANGHAI-BREAKTHROUGH

## Clock
Start: 1937-10-04 18:00
End: 1937-10-10 20:00

## North China
- 6 Oct: Japanese 20th, 14th and 6th Divisions advance toward the Huto River / Chengting-Shihchiachuang sector.
- 8 Oct: 6th Division deploys siege guns and heavy field artillery; total support about 100 guns.
- By dawn 9 Oct: organized resistance in Chengting is substantially cleared.
- Chinese withdrawal south of the Huto River induces the 14th Division to advance its crossing schedule.
- Japanese spearhead reaches Shihchiachuang on the afternoon of 9 Oct in the Japanese operational record; local Chinese chronology commonly treats 10 Oct as the occupation/formal entry date.

### Asai delta
- High-value artillery tractors / heavy vehicles: ready fraction +3 to +6 percentage points for Asai-exposed subset.
- Artillery preparation / repeat fire cycle: about 5–10% shorter in well-prepared batteries.
- Repair and spares turnaround: small positive delta.
- Hard limits unchanged: river crossing, demolished bridges, walls/fortifications, infantry mopping-up, command timing.

### Decision
- Chengting clearance may move earlier by roughly 1–3 hours.
- Shihchiachuang spearhead entry may move earlier by roughly 1–3 hours within 9 Oct.
- Formal/secure occupation date remains 10 Oct.
- No one-day calendar shift is closed.

## Shanghai
- 5 Oct onward: intensified fighting around the Wusong/Yunzaobin river line and approaches to Dachang.
- Japanese artillery, aircraft and naval gunfire provide major firepower advantages, but Chinese riverbank defenses and terrain create severe attrition.
- The Dachang phase remains a positional battle; operational breakthrough is not achieved by 10 Oct.

### Asai delta
- Naval known-target fire-mission cycle: -5 to -10% where Asai-influenced procedures/equipment apply.
- Army artillery preparation/re-engagement: -5 to -10% in favorable conditions.
- Aircraft mission-capable rate: +3 to +5 percentage points locally.
- Heavy/repair vehicle readiness: +3 to +6 percentage points on exposed subset.
- Japanese personnel losses in this sub-period: sensitivity roughly -5 to -8% versus historical-equivalent local fighting.
- Chinese personnel losses: roughly 0 to +3% sensitivity.

### Decision
- No operational calendar shift in Shanghai by 10 Oct.
- No early Dachang fall.
- Asai effect remains a sustainment/fire-support margin, not a terrain or manpower substitute.

## Strategic board at close
- North China: Shihchiachuang is entering Japanese control; pursuit toward south and west is imminent.
- Shanghai: Japanese manpower/firepower has increased, but Wenzaobin/Dachang remains an attritional positional front.
- The next meaningful gates are: (1) pursuit from Shihchiachuang toward Hsingtai/Hantan, (2) Chinghsing–Niangtzukuan / Shanxi linkage, and (3) the mid-October Dachang battle and Chinese reinforcement/counterattack.
