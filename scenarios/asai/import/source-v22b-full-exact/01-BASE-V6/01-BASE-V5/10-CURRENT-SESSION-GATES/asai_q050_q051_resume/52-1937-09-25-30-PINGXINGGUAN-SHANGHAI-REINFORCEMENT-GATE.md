Status: CLOSED-SAME-STRATEGIC / MINOR-ASAI-SUSTAINMENT

- 28 Sep: 1st Army orders drive toward Shijiazhuang, with main-force movement beginning 1 Oct and concentration planned before the main attack.
- 27 Sep: Japanese 9th Division lands in Shanghai, joining 3rd, 11th and 101st Divisions and naval landing forces.
