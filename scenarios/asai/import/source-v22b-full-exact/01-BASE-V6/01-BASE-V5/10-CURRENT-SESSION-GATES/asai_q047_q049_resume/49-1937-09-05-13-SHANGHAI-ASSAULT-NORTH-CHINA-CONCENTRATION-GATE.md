# Q-047 — 1937-09-05 to 1937-09-13 — Shanghai Assault / North China Concentration Gate

Decision: CLOSED-SAME-CALENDAR / CLOSED-SAME-SHANGHAI-ASSAULT-OPENING / CLOSED-MINOR-ASAI-READINESS-FIRECONTROL / OPEN-14SEP-NORTH-CHINA-OFFENSIVE

Clock at close: 1937-09-13 approximately 23:00 local operational time.

## Shanghai

On 11 September Japanese forces opened a heavy attack along the Lotien-Woosung front under an artillery barrage. Contemporary reporting also records continuing Japanese landings of troops, artillery and supplies. Naval gunfire against Pootung was heavy, but it did not automatically suppress Chinese batteries and snipers.

The Asai delta remains bounded. On influenced ships, repeat naval fire missions against known or semi-fixed targets have somewhat shorter calibration/correction cycles. Army artillery and vehicle workshops carry somewhat less maintenance debt, while Japanese air units retain a small serviceability advantage. These effects improve readiness at the instant of attack but do not remove waterways, prepared positions, Chinese manpower superiority, target-identification constraints or the physical limits of unloading heavy artillery.

No calendar shift is granted to the 11 September attack.

## North China

The 14th Division's advance echelons had landed earlier, but concentration of its main strength was not completed until 12 September. The 1st Army order issued on 11 September set the main offensive for 14 September.

Asai-related unloading, repair and heavy-vehicle readiness can improve the fraction of equipment immediately usable after concentration by a few percentage points, but ship arrival, rail movement, weather and command timing dominate. The division is therefore not credited as fully concentrated before the historical-equivalent date.

## Casualty sensitivity

For Shanghai deliberate attacks in this interval, Asai effects are modeled as approximately -4 to -7 percent Japanese combat casualties relative to historical-equivalent local actions and 0 to +3 percent Chinese casualties, with very wide uncertainty. This is not applied as a universal campaign multiplier.

## Next gate

14 September opens the Chohsien-Chingyuan operation in North China while the Shanghai attack forces the first organized Chinese line adjustment.
