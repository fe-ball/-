## Rebuild doctrine
Kwantung Army is more likely to exploit the preserved skeleton than simply "save" every replacement.
- 23rd Division: rebuild around surviving officers/NCOs/specialists; do not rush to overfill with raw infantry solely to regain historical paper strength.
- target late Sep: practical fighting body still ~10-11k-class plus recovering personnel
- target Oct-Nov: ~12-13k-class effective manning is sufficient before further qualitative strengthening
- reconnaissance element is a prime candidate for experimental motorization/vehicle radios/light armor
- AT strengthening: 37mm guns + towing + special AP + trained crews
- artillery: use saved replacement burden to pursue towing, observation, survey, communications and army-level long-range guns rather than simply replace every lost light gun
- First Tank Group: retain as army/Kwantung mobile reserve; rebuild readiness toward 50-60-class practical combat group as production/allocation permits; do not attach everything permanently to 23rd Division
