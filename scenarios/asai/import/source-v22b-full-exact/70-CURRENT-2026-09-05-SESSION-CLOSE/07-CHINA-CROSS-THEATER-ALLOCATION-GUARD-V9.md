This is mandatory for continuing China 1939.

## Core accounting equation
WORLDLINE_CHINA_STRENGTH = HISTORICAL_CHINA_BASELINE (already containing historical Nomonhan/Manchuria drains) + prior worldline deltas - worldline-only additional transfers actually sourced from China.

Never apply an unitemized "Nomonhan drain percentage" on top of a historical China baseline.

## Historical drains that remain baseline events
- 5th Division: North China → Kwantung Army Jul1939. Keep absent from China unless a later actual return occurs.
- 64th Sentai: China → Nomonhan/Manchuria late Jul/early Aug; historical drain.
- 59th/31st and other aviation moves: handle by actual movement timetable and the 1939 aviation reorganization; do not automatically return them early.

## Aviation reorganization warning
The 1939 Army aviation buildup in Manchuria predated/overlapped Nomonhan and planned a substantial shift of China-based air units north. Therefore earlier session speculation that 27/98/45/77 would all simply stay in China after an early Nomonhan end is superseded.

Treat each sentai separately:
- permanent/reorganization transfer?
- emergency combat employment?
- date movement began?
- temporarily retained under China command despite formal reassignment?
- actual Nomonhan losses?
- return/redeployment date?

Immediate Sep1 China bonus from "cancelled northward aviation transfer" starts at **0** until unit-specific audit says otherwise.

## Ground forces / equipment
Most special worldline Nomonhan assets discussed (1/27 early move, First Tank Group, Kwantung artillery, workshop/recovery support) are Manchurian/Kwantung assets, not China draws. China delta 0 unless provenance proves otherwise.

Special 37mm ammunition sent to Nomonhan is not subtracted from China as if China possessed the same forward special-ammo allocation. The main bottleneck is special-core machining/qualification/release; normal China HE/AP supply is not automatically reduced.

## Post-campaign saved pool
Saved Nomonhan manpower/equipment first becomes Home/Manchuria/central retained capital. It is not a China bonus until physically reassigned.

Therefore for **1939-05-25 through 1939-08-31**:
- aggregate extra Nomonhan penalty on China: **0**
- aggregate post-Nomonhan rescue bonus to China: **0**
- historical transfers occur as historical baseline
- prior China worldline advantages remain intact unless consumed in China

From Sep onward, actual returned aircraft/replacements/logistics may create China-side deltas; calculate them on the day they move.
