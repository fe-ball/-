## Character strategy
The protagonist's "kamatoto" strategy is institutional, not translation-based:
1. find a contemporaneously legitimate use,
2. demonstrate real utility,
3. connect it to an institution's desire/failure/status,
4. let the institution demand expansion,
5. privately value future cross-domain uses.

Examples:
- special AP success -> Army funds high-density raw-material acquisition/metallurgy
- turbo battlefield success -> broader high-speed rotating machinery/turbomachinery ecosystem
- photo-recon target detection but insufficient gun reach -> Army demands long-range artillery/fire-integration
