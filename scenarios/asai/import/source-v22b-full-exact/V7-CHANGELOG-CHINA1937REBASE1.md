# V7 CHANGELOG — CHINA1937REBASE1

## Why V7 exists
V6 had already rebased later aviation, but replaying that logic backward showed that 1932–37 aviation/radio/photo capabilities should already affect the China war. The branch was therefore handed back to the Nanyuan preparation stage and replayed through the 1937 year-end.

## Major changes from V6
1. Earlier/more mature E4/E5 chronology and realistic flight-hour histories.
2. E4 No.2 Army operational deployment in North China; E4 No.3 Navy operational deployment at Shanghai and later operations.
3. 1932–33 radio-noise control experiment becomes the explicit origin of earlier Japanese aviation EMI standardization.
4. Air-ground cooperation improves through procedures and institutions, not modern FAC magic.
5. Photo-recon physics corrected; low vibration does not erase forward motion blur.
6. Photo metadata/index concept added: optical frame data + punch-card searchable index; Asai is integrator, not photography monopolist.
7. E4 combat success generates E4 extra4 and E5/Ki40/Ki41/J1As/R1As/Ki42 evaluation procurement earlier than V6 treatment.
8. GT production rebaselined to ~180 new independent cores in1937, with flight-grade capacity separately constrained.
9. T1A/B moved earlier and already operating; T1C is expansion, T2 begins early.
10. 1937 battles replayed with hour-scale operational differences and enemy feedback while major capture dates remain mostly unchanged.
11. Chinese retreat-equipment treatment corrected from repeated % penalties to stock-flow.
12. Japanese equipment difference decomposed into production quantity, readiness, recovery, trained operators and performance.
13. Nanking extra survivor calculation refined upward to +900–1,800 actual escape vs old branch, while quality/organization remains poor.
14. Panay still sinks, but follow-on attack likely shorter.
15. Nanking occupation narrative is intentionally not further “optimized”; technical differences do not cure structural command/discipline/POW problems.
16. V6 downstream 1938–39 is preserved but put on replay hold.
17. New HARD OPEN: historical and V7 effectiveness of air interdiction against the huge late-1937 withdrawals/evacuations.
