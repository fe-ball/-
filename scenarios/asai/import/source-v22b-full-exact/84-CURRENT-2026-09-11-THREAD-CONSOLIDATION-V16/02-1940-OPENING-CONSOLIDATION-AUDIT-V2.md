# 1940 OPENING CONSOLIDATION AUDIT — V16

## Audit philosophy

This worldline is not historical Japan plus isolated bonuses. By 1940 it contains roughly two decades of accumulated supplier, metrology, machine-tool, fuel, maintenance, training and service-network divergence. The audit therefore checks only for physical contradiction, double counting and backflow of future equipment—not for deviation from history by itself.

Checks:

1. A physical output is not counted twice.
2. One scarce capacity is not silently used by two branches at once.
3. Technology readiness is not procurement and procurement is not OOB.
4. Local performance improvements are not turned into one national scalar.
5. Genuine shortages are paid as delay, readiness, yield or local tradeoff.

## Aviation-industrial reading

1941 E6 accepted planning remains 228 Twin + 195 Single = 423 main-series aircraft, plus a larger trainer establishment. The practical aircraft-production divergence is therefore roughly +10–11% of historical national aircraft-production order of magnitude, not +10–11% national GDP.

Historical-type aircraft remain unless explicitly substituted through 1941. From 1942 onward, no unlimited pure-addition rule: new aircraft growth must point to new capacity, reallocation or substitution.

## Materials / shops

No blanket new aluminum crisis is inserted. Local bottlenecks remain:

- sheet rolling / extrusion / forging / heat treatment
- primary structure fabrication
- landing gear / brakes / tyres
- instruments / radios / electrical equipment
- armament

Strategic Ni/Cr/Mo/W are managed through product-specific use. Main E5/E6 life ratings deliberately lean on non-Ni architecture where practical; selected-Ni high-temperature material remains premium.

## Ground / shipping

No national motorization multiplier. Truck counts stay historical-order; high-value readiness improves. Merchant tonnage gains remain small, with larger effective-throughput gains from uptime and turnaround. Central-China combined logistics uses one end-to-end system delta, not a sum of truck + ship + Nanjing + river percentages.
