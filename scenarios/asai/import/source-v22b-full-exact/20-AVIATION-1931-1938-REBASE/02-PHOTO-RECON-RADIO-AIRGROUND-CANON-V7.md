# PHOTO RECON / RADIO / AIR-GROUND CANON — V7

## Photo physics correction
Low vibration does **not** eliminate forward-motion blur.

Useful relations:
- `b_forward = f V t / H`
- `b_vib = f ω t`

E4's real advantage is that low vibration permits faster shutters, longer focal lengths, large formats and high repeatability.

Mature sweet spot later:
- altitude ~5,000–6,000 m;
- photo run ~330–360 km/h;
- lens ~50–70 cm;
- shutter ~1/300–1/400;
- practical ground detail ~0.7–1.0 m at5 km, ~0.8–1.1 m at6 km (approximate, conditions dependent).

Large format geometry examples:
- 23×23 cm with70cm at5km ≈1.64×1.64 km footprint;
- 23×46 cm ≈1.64×3.26 km.

Overlap enables stereo strips/mosaics.

Initial 1932–33 demo can use purchasable Fairchild K-3/K-3A-class equipment; domestic cameras follow via military mediation with optical/photo companies. Asai handles mounting/vibration/power/test integration.

By 1937 the question “can a high-speed aircraft take useful photos?” is CLOSED. Remaining questions are coverage, focal length, processing time, interpretation and distribution.

Two practical branches by 1935–37:
- wide-area mapping ~18–25 cm;
- long-focus military ~50–70 cm.

## Radio/EMI origin
Gas turbine aircraft have no continuous repetitive spark ignition after start, while piston engines produce repeated high-voltage magneto/distributor/lead/plug impulses.

The phenomenon was historically known internationally by 1929–30. Asai does not discover RF interference; E4 provides a dramatic control experiment that makes the Japanese military standardize mitigation earlier.

Timeline:
- 1932 Q4 E4 Twin crews notice unusually quiet radio;
- 1933 H1 same radio/antenna compared E4 vs piston recon; engine on/off, magnetos, rpm, generator load isolate sources;
- 1933 H2 Asai proposes aircraft-side radio-noise acceptance test;
- 1934 Army/Navy trials ignition shielding, bonding, generator suppression, power filtering;
- 1935 important aircraft begin acceptance requirements;
- 1936–37 spread especially to recon/bomber/long-range/naval aircraft.

Tech:
shield magneto/HT leads/plugs, bonding straps, filters, condensers/chokes, power filtering, waterproof connectors. Adds weight/maintenance and must survive oil/water/vibration.

Effect is not a magic fixed range percentage. Weak-signal intelligibility/reliability improves; the same radio becomes more usable in recon, direction finding, formation and air-ground communication.

## Air-ground cooperation
Radio alone does not fix doctrine. Early successful communication creates institutionalization from 1933 onward:
- 1933–34 division/artillery HQ experiments;
- 1934–35 artillery observation with improved piston aircraft;
- 1935–36 semi-permanent divisional air liaison teams;
- 1937 China war exposes procedure/organization problems rather than “radio simply does not work.”

Combined cycle:
fly → spot → radio flash report → photograph → return/develop → confirm/detail.

By 1939 expected character:
- photo intelligence advanced;
- artillery air observation good;
- division-air liaison substantially improved;
- planned CAS useful;
- dynamic minute-by-minute FAC/CAS still weak due ground portable-radio density, marking, command chain, etc.

Army internal cooperation improves; Navy internal communication improves; Army–Navy strategic integration remains poor.

## Photo metadata / indexing
Use a unique frame ID shared by negative/print and punched-card index.

Film margin can optically expose time/altitude/frame/camera information using a data chamber. Ground staff then punch/search metadata cards. Machine sorting retrieves the right photos; humans interpret them.

Do not portray Asai as making the lenses, film, chemistry and all photo equipment. See the dedicated plausibility note.
