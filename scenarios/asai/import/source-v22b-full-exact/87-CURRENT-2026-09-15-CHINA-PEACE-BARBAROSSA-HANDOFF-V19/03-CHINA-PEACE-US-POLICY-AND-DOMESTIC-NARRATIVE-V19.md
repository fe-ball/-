# CHINA PEACE / US POLICY / DOMESTIC NARRATIVE — V19

## 1. Why the peace is politically saleable in Japan

The central domestic story is a classic “punitive expedition achieved its stated correction and returned with a treaty” narrative.

It is not identical to objective cost-benefit truth, but it is not fabricated from nothing:
- Chongqing actually stops the war and signs a state-to-state settlement.
- Japanese field armies are not routed at the peace date.
- Japan does not pay state reparations.
- Manchukuo remains in being de facto; Chongqing does not receive immediate Manchurian restoration.
- Japanese civilians/property receive guarantees.
- commercial normalization becomes possible.

Therefore official language can emphasize “incident objectives achieved,” “anti-Japanese policy corrected,” “East Asian peace restored,” and “normal relations resumed.”

The unsolved question is whether the military/political/economic cost since 1937 was worth this. V19 deliberately does **not** answer yes. Peace only creates a chance to stop the loss from compounding.

## 2. Two opposite meanings of “do not lose the gains”

Likely post-peace political slogan logic:

Strong-war interpretation:
- forces are free;
- therefore use them north or south while opportunity exists.

Peace-preservation interpretation:
- four years finally produced a treaty and reopened trade;
- do not destroy that result by opening another unnecessary war.

Both can claim to defend the “gains” of the China war. This tension becomes decisive after 22 June 1941.

## 3. US policy dilemma

US policy now receives the ball back.

If Washington continues to tighten after visible Japanese withdrawal:
- Tokyo’s cooperation line receives no material return for its concessions;
- “the US seeks Japanese submission, not peaceful behavior” becomes much easier to argue domestically;
- the resource-deadline logic that historically pushed south can be recreated even after China peace.

If Washington simply restores unrestricted strategic supply:
- China-returning Japanese formations, aircraft and vehicles can be refit with US/Dutch oil;
- the oil can become combat power aimed north or south.

Therefore the central V19 US branch is **conditional and reversible normalization**.

## 4. Suggested policy architecture for replay

Do not treat this as closed US behavior; it is a strong working branch to test.

If Japan:
- continues China withdrawal;
- does not occupy southern Indochina;
- does not coerce the Dutch East Indies militarily;
- does not use the Tripartite Pact as automatic anti-US war commitment;

then the US can:
- avoid a total Japanese-asset freeze;
- stabilize ordinary crude/fuel-oil/middle-distillate licensing;
- avoid pressuring the Dutch to cancel normal commercial oil contracts;
- negotiate broader trade normalization in stages.

Still restricted/tightly licensed:
- high-octane aviation gasoline;
- tetraethyl lead;
- selected premium military goods/technologies;
- extraordinary stock-building quantities that look like immediate war preparation.

## 5. Dutch East Indies

The Indies should not be treated as a passive US-controlled valve.

Central logic:
- normal oil contracts are economically useful to the Dutch and can reduce Japanese incentive for coercion;
- demands for very large guaranteed strategic quantities remain unacceptable;
- US asset-freeze/payment controls can indirectly kill Indies trade even without a formal “Dutch embargo,” so the decision not to freeze assets matters greatly.

Worldline guard:
- if Japan stays out of southern Indochina and treats Indies oil as commerce, the historical July 1941 freeze/embargo chain does not fire automatically.
- if Japan again seeks resources by force, the chain can rapidly reappear.

## 6. Why oil delivery becomes domestic proof

A Japanese leader cannot simply say “save me or the extremists win” without sounding coercive or self-serving.

The more credible message through diplomats is institutional:
- China withdrawal was a difficult policy reversal;
- if it produces no improvement in US economic treatment, the conclusion inside government/armed services will be that peaceful concessions have no payoff.

Visible commercial reality is more powerful than rhetoric:
- tankers arriving under stable licenses = cooperation produced a result;
- tankers disappearing despite peace/withdrawal = hardliners gain evidence for their case.

## 7. China-side consequences

Peace does not instantly reunify China.

KMT advantages vs history:
- avoids several 1941 Japanese campaigns;
- preserves more regular forces;
- can restore administration in evacuated areas;
- receives continued US economic/defensive support rather than having to choose between peace and America.

CCP advantages vs history:
- avoids the historical Wannan destruction;
- retains cadres/forces and base-area organization.

CCP disadvantages vs history:
- loses the political dividend of Wannan;
- faces more intact KMT forces in some regions;
- Japanese withdrawal makes administrative competition with KMT immediate.

Thus the first postwar competition is often a race for county government, police, taxation, rail security and local armed affiliation rather than an immediate nationwide civil war.
