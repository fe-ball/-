# HUNDRED REGIMENTS PRE-GATE — LOGISTICS / NANJING / HELICOPTER

## Political meaning

The major Communist offensive remains politically important in this worldline. The CCP still has strong incentives to demonstrate that the Eighth Route Army is not merely preserving itself or expanding behind the main Nationalist front.

Therefore V16 does **not** cancel the offensive because Japanese rear-area systems are better.

## Physical sabotage versus system effect

Expected first-phase rule:

- physical sabotage success can remain near historical scale; broad railway/security networks cannot be fully guarded at night.
- bulk rail transport suffers serious instantaneous disruption.
- command continuity / priority cargo / repair dispatch suffer less than historical-type because alternate communications, trucks, repair teams and light high-value air transport exist.
- small track cuts therefore lose strategic persistence faster.
- bridges, tunnels, yards, mines, power, locomotive depots and repair teams become more valuable targets.

## Japanese adaptation

Japanese response should appear as:

- faster repair-team dispatch
- better radio-equipped local response
- railhead-to-railhead truck bypass for priority loads
- specialist / crypto / precision-part / staff movement by road and occasionally E6 Twin
- more armed repair/security columns
- quicker identification of which sabotage nodes deserve major counterattack

Do **not** turn these into one global “Asai logistics multiplier.” Resolve outage duration and freight class.

## Nanjing effect

By summer 1940, Nanjing regime institutions can lower some Japanese static administrative / police / labor / warehousing burden in the lower Yangtze.

This helps North-China emergency response only indirectly, by freeing a little Japanese specialist/vehicle/support reserve.

Do not add a separate Nanjing +X% on top of the existing combined logistics system effect.

## Helicopter boundary

1940 combat helicopter contribution: **0**.

- H1 piston helicopter: development / Army-Navy evaluation only.
- H2 turboshaft helicopter: 1940H2 ground/rig/integration phase; first free flight remains 1941Q1 under current V13 closure.
- No 1940 helicopter resupply, medevac, air assault or mountain pursuit.

Hundred Regiments / North-China mountain warfare may strengthen future H2 mission demand, especially for observer placement, radio/specialist transport and casualty retrieval, but that is a 1941+ development feedback, not 1940 OOB.

## Working exchange-ratio expectation

Do not hard-code absolute historical casualty totals because source traditions diverge. Use relative branch effects:

- initial physical sabotage: ~90–100% of historical-type
- priority-command paralysis time: ~50–70% of historical-type
- bulk rail impact: ~75–90% of historical-type
- Japanese reaction begins ~0.5–2 days earlier in many sectors
- Eighth Route main-force exposure time can increase ~10–25%
- Japanese/collaborationist personnel losses: working ~10–25% lower than historical-type
- Eighth Route personnel losses: working ~20–40% higher than historical-type if it persists at comparable scale

These are planning bands for the next replay gate, not completed results.
