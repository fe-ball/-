## Mandatory guards

### Peace branch

The Japan–Chongqing peace is branch canon as of May 1941. Do not silently restore historical 1941 China campaigns. Reopening requires an explicit treaty-break/rewar event.

### Chinese internal conflict

Do not make KMT–CCP relations peaceful merely because Japan exits. Do not make immediate total civil war inevitable either. Track administration, local force, supply, political legitimacy and specific armed incidents.

### Japan returning forces

A China-withdrawing unit passes states:
`withdrawal ordered -> concentrated -> transported -> theater exit -> rest/refit -> reassigned -> arrived Manchuria/other -> ready`.

Do not collapse these into “freed division available against USSR.”

### Soviet transfer problem

A stronger Japanese threat does not automatically mean “zero Far-East transfers.” Transfer decisions are unit/date/intelligence dependent.

### Neutrality Pact

1941-04-13 treaty remains operative until explicitly broken. Military planning and mobilization can occur under the treaty; cross-border attack requires a new decision.

### Barbarossa historical anchor

Treat 1941-06-22 German invasion as the next historical anchor unless replay before that date creates an explicit causal change. Do not pre-edit German OOB/campaign because of East Asian changes that have not yet propagated.

### Loss propagation

Nomonhan raw casualty deltas are too small to be credited directly as large Barbarossa-force deltas. The major channel is eastern threat perception/retention/transfer timing.

### “Siberian divisions” guard

Never use the phrase as a substitute for unit data. Distinguish Far Eastern, Transbaikal, Siberian-origin, reserve and other formations by actual assignment and transfer.

### US oil branch

Conditional normalization is a working central branch, not immutable US behavior. US choices must respond to Japanese actions and domestic/Allied constraints. High-octane controls and ordinary petroleum trade are separate policy levers.

### Fuel branch

Do not equate avgas restriction with total aviation-fuel restriction. Do not equate kerosene/gas-oil abundance with unlimited total energy or naval fuel.
