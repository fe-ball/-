# NAVY CARRIER TURBOPROP APPLICATION SPLIT — D4Y / B6N — 1941–1942 — V1 D4YAUDIT1

**Status:** CURRENT V10 FUTURE-OUTLOOK. No production/service result is pre-recorded.

The D4Y and B6N TP programs are complementary rather than a single-engine competition.

| aircraft | natural E7 product | principal TP value | outlook |
|---|---|---|---|
| D4Y Suisei | E7-S-T ~1.5 khp | climb/wave-off, cooling simplicity, low vibration, fast recon; only modest max-speed gain over healthy Atsuta | B+ / A- conditional; limited recon/fast-strike series first is rational |
| B6N Tenzan | E7-M-LF ~1.8–2.0 khp | large radial replacement, smaller/lighter nose, loaded takeoff, torpedo acceleration and radius preservation | A- conditional main-production candidate |

Shared bottlenecks are reducer/propeller/governor production, hot-section capacity, carrier turbine-maintenance qualification and kerosene deck logistics. The core gas paths occupy different scale bands.

Planning consequence: do not cancel either program solely to "save E7 engines." Prioritize D4Y as the **small-core carrier qualification / reconnaissance branch** and B6N as the **lower-medium attack branch**. The later B7A decision should be based on measured results from both.
