# V22B — GERMANY/USSR COMBAT REPLAY SUMMARY, 1941–1944

## Method guard

Apply three interacting effects without double counting:

1. German direct improvement from E5/E6 transfer and earlier mature jet operations.
2. German indirect improvement from no US-German war / no Normandy / no US strategic bombing/oil campaign already present in V21.
3. Soviet indirect weakening from additional Far East retention caused by stronger Japanese capability signaling.

The E5 branch does not create all V21 German advantages by itself.

## 1941

- E5-D numbers remain small.
- Main effect: reconnaissance/courier/technical learning.
- Moscow outcome does not flip.
- Additional casualty effect relative to V21 is small.

## 1942

- E5-D is a recurring specialist fleet.
- Case Blue gains somewhat better reconnaissance and command latency, but strategy remains overextended.
- Uranus still succeeds.
- Warning and local defensive preparation improve.
- Winter Storm is stronger; working breakout survivor band from the 6th Army rises toward ~90–120k in this branch.
- This returns disproportionate cadres, technicians and mobile troops to 1943.

## 1943 — Kursk and aftermath

### Kursk
- north still fails;
- south performs better;
- Prokhorovka is taken/held locally for several days;
- German mobile losses are lower;
- Soviet armored and personnel losses are higher;
- Citadel remains a strategic failure.

### Rumyantsev / Bogodukhov
- German aerial reconnaissance + preserved armor + healthier Luftwaffe improves counterattack timing;
- Soviet armored spearheads can be semi-enveloped and forced to abandon more vehicles;
- Kharkov is still evacuated, but later and more deliberately.

### Dnieper / Kiev
- crossings occur;
- Bukrin remains costly and constrained;
- Lyutezh surprise is reduced because high-speed reconnaissance detects disappearance/reconcentration earlier;
- Kiev is held through late 1943 and evacuated around early Jan 1944 in the center branch.

## 1944 Q1/Q2

### Korsun
Historical-scale six-division destruction is avoided. A smaller temporary enclosure/corridor fight may occur, but the German army preserves most formations and heavy equipment.

### Hube / 1st Panzer Army
Earlier withdrawal reduces the historical near-entrapment. Main branch gain is retained armor, artillery, trucks, maintenance and communications assets.

### Crimea
Earlier evacuation saves much more German/Romanian manpower, though the USSR also frees Crimea forces sooner.

## Bagration

Soviet first-wave power remains overwhelming. Belarus is lost.

Branch difference:

- AG Center has real operational reserves;
- Luftwaffe is far stronger;
- E6-D/Ar 234 provide much more persistent reconnaissance;
- withdrawals from Vitebsk/Orsha/Mogilev/Bobruisk begin earlier;
- Minsk is abandoned rather than becoming the center of mass encirclement;
- after early July, Soviet pursuit encounters organized German mobile defense instead of an empty operational depth.

Branch working full-operation German loss band Jun–Aug:

- total: roughly 220–300k;
- irreversible: roughly 100–150k;
- 6–9 divisions destroyed or requiring major rebuild.

The USSR still wins a major territorial victory, but not the historical destruction of Army Group Center.

## Late summer / autumn 1944

### Warsaw / central Poland
- Soviet approach is delayed;
- historical 1 Aug Warsaw Uprising is not automatically triggered;
- Warsaw west bank remains German through year-end center branch;
- Magnuszew/Pulawy bridgeheads exist but remain smaller.

### Lvov / Sandomierz
- Lvov falls;
- Soviet drive to Vistula is slower and costlier;
- Baranow/Sandomierz bridgehead forms smaller;
- German autumn counterattack can retake Sandomierz city and compress the bridgehead without consuming the historical/V21 level of mobile reserve.

### Baltic
- Army Group North's isolation is delayed;
- more mobile/heavy formations escape toward East Prussia;
- Courland pocket still forms but is substantially smaller.

### Romania
- no historical Aug 1944 military/political collapse;
- line contracts through Moldavia toward Focsani–Namoloasa–Galati and Eastern Carpathians;
- Ploiesti remains in Axis economic orbit through 1944 year-end center branch.

## 1944 year-end strategic character

The USSR has gained major territory and still holds the strategic initiative, but Germany has increasingly exchanged terrain for army survival. Unlike history, the German military retains enough manpower, armor, aircraft, fuel and organizational coherence that a 1945 limited offensive option must be reconsidered rather than assuming perpetual retreat.
