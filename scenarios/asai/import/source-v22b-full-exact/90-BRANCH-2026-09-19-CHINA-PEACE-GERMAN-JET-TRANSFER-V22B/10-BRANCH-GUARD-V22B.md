# V22B BRANCH GUARD

## Scope

This folder is a **branch overlay** on the V21 full package.

Inside this branch:

- China peace remains accepted;
- Japan remains non-belligerent against US/UK/NL/USSR through 1944 center branch;
- E5 full transfer to Germany is accepted branch history;
- E5-D/E6-D and the German jet-system consequences are branch-local working canon through 1944;
- 1945 outcomes remain open.

## Precedence

For this branch only:

1. explicit V22B decisions;
2. V21 explicit decisions not contradicted by V22B;
3. V10/V8 technical authority for Japanese hardware;
4. older files as source/history only.

## Non-double-counting

Do not attribute the same effect twice.

Examples:

- V21 already has stronger Germany because the US is not at war with Germany; do not add that again as an E5 transfer effect.
- V21 already pins Soviet Far East forces because Japan is strong; V22B increases the persistence/quality of that retention, not the entire Far East garrison from zero.
- E6-D and Ar 234 overlap in reconnaissance; do not independently maximize both fleets without reducing role overlap.
- stronger German early withdrawal can reduce both German and Soviet losses in a local encirclement battle even if long-run Soviet losses remain higher.

## Technology-transfer guard

Germany receives products, methods, engineers and data. It does not receive Asai's personal reincarnator answer-selection capability. German improvements require German engineering and service feedback.

Likewise, Germany's field feedback can improve Japanese E6/E7 only through actual reports, samples, engineers, tests and redevelopment.

## Modeling style

- distinguish produced / present / serviceable / immediately operational;
- distinguish total Eastern Front assets from one army-group allocation;
- distinguish historical anchors from branch estimates;
- use ranges for uncertain OOB and casualty figures;
- preserve Soviet adaptation rather than treating reconnaissance superiority as omniscience;
- preserve Hitler as a real decision constraint; no assassination/coup shortcut is assumed.
