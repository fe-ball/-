# V22B — GERMAN / AXIS LATE-WAR OOB WORKING STATE — 1944-12-31

**Status:** BRANCH WORKING STATE, NOT ARCHIVAL CENSUS.

## 1. Strategic geography

Year-end center branch approximately:

- small Courland pocket rather than historical full Army Group North entrapment;
- Warsaw west bank remains German; Soviet east-bank pressure and smaller bridgeheads remain;
- Magnuszew/Pulawy bridgeheads exist but are smaller;
- Sandomierz city can be German; Baranow bridgehead remains compressed but not eliminated;
- Romania remains in the Axis camp; defense converges on Eastern Carpathian / Focsani–Namoloasa–Galati system;
- Ploiesti remains available to the Axis economic system;
- Germany has not suffered historical western ground invasion or US strategic-bombing destruction.

## 2. Manpower

Working branch bands:

- German field army: ~4.6–4.9 million;
- German Eastern Front: ~3.1–3.4 million;
- organized Axis east-facing total including Romania/Hungary etc.: ~4.2–4.6 million.

Main late-war constraint increasingly shifts toward manpower/cadre quality rather than equipment scarcity.

## 3. Armor

Eastern operational tanks/assault guns: ~5,000–6,000 class.

Typical high-value composition is not "Tiger II everywhere."

Main combat ecosystem:

- Panther A/G;
- Panzer IV H/J;
- StuG III/IV in large numbers;
- Jagdpanzer IV family growing;
- Tiger I heavy battalions;
- Tiger II limited/growing;
- Hetzer increasing in later 1944;
- extensive Pak 40/Pak 43, mines and Panzerfaust/Panzerschreck support.

The important feature is better filling of existing formations and larger recoverable mobile reserves.

## 4. Airpower

Eastern operational Luftwaffe: ~2,800–3,500 aircraft class.

Jets east: ~400–650 operational class.

Working split:

- Me 262 ~250–350;
- E6-D ~100–170;
- Ar 234 ~50–80;
- residual E5-D/other: balance.

Conventional Bf 109/Fw 190 remain the sortie backbone.

## 5. Ammunition/fuel/transport

Relative to history:

- artillery ammunition availability is materially healthier;
- truck/tractor availability is materially healthier;
- rail system is less damaged;
- Ploiesti remains available through 1944 center branch;
- synthetic fuel plants have not suffered the historical US Oil Campaign;
- jet fuel/kerosene-type supply is therefore much less of a system-killing problem than in historical 1944–45 Germany.

## 6. Operational reserve

Germany can still plausibly concentrate roughly 10–15 mobile-division equivalents into one priority theater if enough warning is available, though not simultaneously everywhere.

The 1945 question is therefore no longer whether Germany can form an operational strike force. It is whether using one offensively produces more strategic value than preserving it for mobile defense.
