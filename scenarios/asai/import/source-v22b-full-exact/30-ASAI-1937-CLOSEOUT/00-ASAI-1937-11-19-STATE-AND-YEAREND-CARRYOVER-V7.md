# ASAI 1937-11-19 STATE / YEAR-END CARRYOVER — V7

## Corporate identity
Asai has not become an aircraft-only company or photography company.

1937 operating character:
**main revenue = special steels/tools/precision machinery; military glamour = aviation GT; biggest volume war-growth = turbos, tools, gauges, maintenance/industrial equipment.**

Founder Asai Iwao is aggressive and market-making: demonstrate first, create demand, sell complete interfaces/ecosystems, exploit Army/Navy rivalry and external clients, but protect core turbine manufacturing IP.

- annual sales center ~¥25.3m;
- year-end assets ~¥23m;

Working sales composition center:

War increases H2 order backlog rather than instantly doubling the company.

## Main production bottleneck
Not demand. The bottleneck is allocation of skilled labor and scarce process capacity:
- grinding,
- heat treatment,
- balancing,
- metrology/inspection,
- test cells,
- experienced supervisors.

## Tool/steel spillover
China-war military allocation rises. General special-steel/tool/gauge output +5–10% class and military allocation +15–25% class remain plausible working deltas.

Asai need not build rifles/tanks itself for national weapon output to improve. It supplies tools/gauges/jigs/metrology/special steels/heat-treatment know-how/machine tools to arsenals and Mitsubishi/Kawasaki/Nakajima etc.

## Exhaust turbos / vehicles
1937 exhaust-turbo factory completions ~1,300/y, military ~325–465.

Military priority after July:
- heavy cargo;
- artillery tractor/high-load use;
- workshop/recovery support;
- Manchuria/North China;
- replacements.

Not all standard trucks receive turbos.

Special vehicle packages use existing chassis where possible. Asai provides turbo/power/cooling/integration, or high-value body/power packages, not a monopoly complete vehicle line.

Advanced workshop/recovery package year-end population is still small: **roughly 10–20 vehicles class** as a safe center. 1938 can add another low-tens batch.

Photo-processing support vehicle is at most 0–2 prototype/package stage in late1937; specialist photo equipment comes from other companies/military branches.

Information/computation remains a minority (~5% sales working), but military work expands:
- inventory,
- repair statistics,
- production progress,
- ballistic/engineering calculations,

Asai's photo role is interface/information processing, not lenses/film/chemistry ownership.

## Economic spillover to other companies
