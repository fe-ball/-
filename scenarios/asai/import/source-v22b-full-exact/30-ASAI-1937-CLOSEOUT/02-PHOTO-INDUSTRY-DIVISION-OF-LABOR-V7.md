# PHOTO INDUSTRY DIVISION OF LABOR — V7

Do not depict Asai as turning into a vertically integrated photography company.

Likely 1937 division:
- lens/camera/optical mechanisms: Nippon Kogaku and other optics specialists;
- film/sensitized material/chemistry/photo supplies: Konishiroku and related photo firms;
- enlarging/copy/processing apparatus: photo/printing/precision suppliers;
- interpretation, map grids, distribution: Army/Navy photo/survey/intelligence organizations;
- radio: military-approved electrical/communications manufacturers;
- Asai: aircraft, engine, camera mount, vibration/isolation, optical window, power, shutter/flight-record interface, instrumentation, integration tests, ground power, vehicle platform/environment package, information-index workflow.

If the military says “photos take too long to process near the front,” Asai's natural product is not photographic emulsion. It is a **mobile support platform**: truck, generator, water, heating/cooling, ventilation, blackout, vibration-safe racks, leveling jacks, work surfaces and power distribution, with specialist photo equipment installed by the appropriate supplier/military unit.
