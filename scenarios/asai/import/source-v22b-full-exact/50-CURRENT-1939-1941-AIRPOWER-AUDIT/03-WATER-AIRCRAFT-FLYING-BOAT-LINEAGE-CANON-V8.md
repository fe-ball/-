# WATER AIRCRAFT / FLYING BOAT LINEAGE CANON — V8

## Why this branch can be retroactively audited
Most of the 1937-40 seaplane/flying-boat engineering changes are technically independent of the China campaign. They may be rebaselined early provided prior combat outcomes are not automatically rewritten.

## Wet-operation materials rule — CLOSED
- **Seal electrical/equipment interfaces; drain and ventilate structure.** Do not simply epoxy-seal every cavity and trap moisture.
- epoxy waterproof adhesive/sealer can materially improve conventional plywood without replacing it.
- hemp/epoxy plate is not automatically superior to plywood; density can make direct like-for-like replacement worse.
- hemp/epoxy + paper-honeycomb sandwich is attractive for stiffness-dominated large secondary panels.
- 1939-40 paper-honeycomb is not the default primary float shell.
- likely early uses: floors, partitions, doors, fairings, dry covers, radio/camera shelves, selected upper/dry sandwich panels; later metal-face sandwich and limited GFRP can expand after wet/fatigue qualification.

## Material lineage already in package
- M0/M1 work includes epoxy adhesion and paper/honeycomb process before 1935.
- Material No.1 already flies larger-area plant-fiber/epoxy + paper-honeycomb structures by 1938H2.
- Material No.2 is the higher-dynamic-pressure / vibration / bonded-joint follow-on.
- Do not retcon 1939 into an all-composite fighter era.

## E13A Zero reconnaissance floatplane
- design already mature by 1938-39, so no full structural revolution.
- after E13K competition resolves, production engineering can accelerate: radio/camera moisture protection, float compartment sealing/drainage, anti-corrosion, bonded secondary panels, photo metadata/calibration.
- target technical selection 1939Q4-1940Q1; formal adoption 1940Q2-Q3 candidate.
- initial V8 performance ~380-385 km/h class; main benefit is availability/information quality, not a large speed scalar.

## F1M Zero observation seaplane
- mostly historical airframe; no fantasy plywood->composite main redesign.
- water-handling/finishing loop may close somewhat faster; sealing, corrosion protection, ignition/radio wet reliability benefit.
- roughly 370-378 km/h class, center ~375.

## E15K Shiun
- 14-Shi 1939 requirement remains.
- mission is speed-based reconnaissance under hostile fighter threat, so laminar-flow care is mission-compatible here even though it is not preferred for Horikoshi's large-wing fighter philosophy.
- Asai contribution: surface waviness control, bonded fairings/panels, precision manufacture, wet surface protection, instrumentation.
- factory-clean and operational-wet laminar benefit must be separated.
- contra-prop remains a research option but likely falls from production baseline after rig/maintenance trade study.
- retractable outrigger floats likely lose to fixed low-drag floats after tank/hydraulic/salt-cycle testing.
- central-float jettison only survives if repeated full-scale airborne jettison tests pass; it is emergency escape, not routine tactic.
- target first flight 1941Q2-Q3, center Jun-Jul; normal max ~480-490 km/h, center ~485. Float-jettison shadow 525-540 km/h only if qualified.

## H6K
- no major design retcon; 1938 service date stays.
- retroactive V8 benefits are sealing, corrosion control, fuel/electrical interfaces, drainage, radio reliability, instrumentation and maintenance-state retention.

## H8K
- 13-Shi requirement in 1938 and active 1939 design window means Asai wet-engineering can be design-origin.
- primary hull stays metal.
- major benefits: tank/model/high-speed-photo water behavior, spray/porpoising diagnosis, corrosion/sealing, electrical/autopilot/navigation, light secondary interior panels.
- historical water-handling problems are not deleted, but correction loop can be shorter.
- first-flight historical late-1940 window broadly remains; maturity can improve by weeks/months rather than giant speed bonuses.

## H9A training flying boat
Useful as a 1940 wet-material/service testbed because it is a mixed-structure trainer rather than a high-risk frontline design.
Potential comparison coupons/panels: conventional plywood + epoxy, epoxy-sealed plywood, hemp/epoxy, paper-honeycomb sandwich, bonded metal/wood joints in noncritical areas.

## E16A Zuiun
- predecessor 14-Shi concept issues exist from 1939; Aichi autonomous AM-22 work ~1940, formal 16-Shi 1941.
- therefore 1940 design can use material/test knowledge by design-origin.
- primary structure remains metal; epoxy/wood/honeycomb/GFRP options are selected by part rather than ideology.
- key benefit is shortening dive-brake vibration, flap, float-bottom strength and stability troubleshooting, not huge max-speed inflation.

## N1K Kyofu -> interim Zero floatfighter — causal chain now authoritative
### Kyofu
- formal 15-Shi request: **Sep 1940 SAME**.
- because Shiun and common prop/water-test work has already exposed expensive options, baseline is likely conventional 3/4-blade constant-speed prop + fixed outrigger floats instead of making contra-prop/retractable outriggers the primary path.
- first-flight target **Nov 1941-Jan 1942, center Dec 1941**.
- service trials 1942Q1; production-ready 1942Q2-Q3 candidate.
- max ~500 km/h class; do not erase float drag.

### Interim A6M floatfighter
It is **not an automatic historical event**. It is generated by the predicted Kyofu service gap.
- 1940Q4: contingency feasibility/paper study is reasonable even if Kyofu is healthy.
- Feb 1941: center branch still issues formal bridge-aircraft order because Kyofu prototype may fly in late 1941 but service-ready/production is still in 1942.
- prior Zero productization + accumulated float hydrodynamics/sealing data shorten the conversion loop.
- target first flight Aug-Oct 1941, center Sep.
- technical/service acceptance Nov-Dec 1941 candidate.
- therefore **"Type 1 Float Fighter" is a serious center candidate**; bureaucratic slippage into 1942 yields the historical "Type 2" designation.
- if a future Kyofu gate moves service-ready into 1941, re-run R6 and the interim floatfighter may shrink or disappear.
- quantity remains OPEN and depends on actual 1942 island/seaplane-base demand; a faster Kyofu tends to reduce bridge-aircraft total, but mix production can still preserve both.

## Next consequence
Earlier Kyofu maturation naturally advances the moment when Kawanishi notices that removing the floats could make a powerful land fighter. This feeds the N1K1-J Shiden line and must be audited next rather than assumed.
