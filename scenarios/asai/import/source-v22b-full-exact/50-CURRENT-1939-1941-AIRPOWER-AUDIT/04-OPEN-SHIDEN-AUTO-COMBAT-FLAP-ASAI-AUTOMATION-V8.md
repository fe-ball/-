## Why N1K1-J still exists — CLOSED INTERPRETATION
N1K1-J is not an accidental mistake that competent engineers would automatically skip.
It provides:
- early landplane service from a largely available Kyofu-derived wing/system;
- Homare/prop/cooling/weapon/control flight data;
- combat/use data while the cleaner low-wing aircraft is being redesigned.

It can therefore be intentionally short-lived.

Its known penalties remain:
- mid-wing layout;
- long, complicated retracting/extending main landing gear;
- gear-door/fuselage drag and maintenance burden;
- rough-field vulnerability.

A good Homare does not remove these reasons for N1K2.

## Control principle — CLOSED INTERPRETATION
The automatic combat-flap system is a period electro-hydromechanical analogue controller, not modern electronics.

Conceptually:
- required lift coefficient varies approximately with n/q for a given aircraft state;
- pneumatic/dynamic-pressure sensing plus inertial/load information generates a state proxy;
- electrical contacts/relays produce flap-down / hold / flap-up commands;
- solenoid/hydraulic valve and actuator move the flap;
- mechanical cable/cam/position feedback encodes the desired flap schedule and prevents runaway/hunting.
