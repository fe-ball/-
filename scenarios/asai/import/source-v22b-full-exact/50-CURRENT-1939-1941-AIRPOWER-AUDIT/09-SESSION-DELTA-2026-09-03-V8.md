11. Asai high-altitude gasoline-engine research model formalized around the existing high-speed rotating-machinery core, with upstream materials/gears and downstream turbo/flight plus parallel production/QC.
12. Asai's cheat is explicitly a **development-mechanism/search-order cheat** as well as knowledge of later solutions. He may pre-test the correct failure modes and avoid dead ends, but does not know engine-specific frequencies/thermal maps before hardware exists.
13. Prewar secret-patent practice accepted as a legitimate controlled domestic military technology-transfer channel. Ordinary patents, secret patents and retained process know-how are separated.
14. High-load multilayer bearing path reclosed: Asai can plausibly independently converge on silver/soft-overlay/indium-treated high-load bearing technology by the mid-1930s, with small-scale indium recovery seeded ahead of demand. This does not eliminate oil supply/geometry failures.
15. Low-octane-sensitivity gasoline-supercharging research formalized. Objective is not "high octane unnecessary" but "standard-fuel degradation is controlled and mapped" using compressor efficiency, charge cooling, combustion/ignition, mixture distribution, ADI and boost limits.
16. Asai need not become a mass gasoline aero-engine maker. Research engines/aircraft, data, critical components, patents/specifications and joint test procedures can expand turbo sales while Nakajima/Mitsubishi remain the mass military engine makers.
17. Homare development reclosed: 1940–41 prototype timing only modestly improves because history was already fast; V8's large gain is fuel/lubricant adaptability, diagnostics, production replication and service-output variance.
18. Fuel and lubricant are treated as separate service gates. Fuel mainly controls knock/boost; lubricant primarily controls duration/life and can force boost restrictions. Use explicit service maps, not blind fixed percentage penalties.
19. Homare turbo branch accepted as historically grounded and plausibly earlier-integrated in V8, but remains mission-selective; full military installation still costs mass, heat management, ducting and aircraft integration time.
20. Fighter powerplant/material integration recalibrated: Homare Reppu center ~615–620 km/h, Ha-43 Reppu ~628 km/h, Kinsei Zero ~590 km/h class, N1K2 ~600 km/h class remain **working**, not final canon.
