# DENSE MATERIALS / SPECIAL AP — 1939-04-20 STATE — V8 AIRPOWER12

> No combat-clock advance. This promotes the 1937 dense-material audits to an Apr20 technical state while blocking later Nomonhan field-validation language.

## 1. Material families

### W-Ni-Cu heavy alloy

By 1936-37 Asai has a small commercial heavy-alloy line. Apr1939 uses include compact counterweights, shielding/vibration masses, dense machine components and military test lots. Working density remains roughly 16-17 g/cc class.

Do not equate ordinary shielding-grade WHA with a fully optimized penetrator alloy without separate firing qualification.

### Uranium-base dense metal

This remains a distinct natural-U dense-metal route:

- not a cemented-WC replacement;
- not a 1937/39 U-0.75Ti mass product;
- not a commodity structural metal.

1939 technical output potential remains around **18-25 t/year**. This is capacity/potential, not a booked fiscal production total.

## 2. Strategic allocation in 1939

The old 1937-38 special-AP window is retained. By 1939 the opportunity cost of natural uranium is consciously high, so new ordnance allocation is deliberately capped around **2-4 t/year material-equivalent working** even though the metallurgy line could make more.

This is not a late realization caused by public fission news alone; the reincarnator already knows the strategic direction. The public/scientific event merely gives an ordinary-world justification for a policy he already has reason to prefer.

W resources remain prioritized for HSS, carbide/cutting tools, wear/dies and other high-value uses. Dense-core ammunition stays a special-use branch.

## 3. 37 mm special AP Apr20 state

The later Nomonhan branch requires a pre-existing technically releasable round. Close Apr20 as follows:

- comparative firing between WC/hard-W, WHA and U-base dense material has been conducted;
- at least one 37 mm special-AP configuration has passed controlled proof/range qualification sufficiently for a **limited contingency / controlled-issue stock**;
- exact core material can differ by lot/test family; do not decree that all dense-core rounds are uranium;
- ordinary AP remains the bulk ammunition;
- exact round count and per-unit allocation remain open until a named mobilization/order gate.

## 4. Critical rollback guard

At Apr20 the correct phrase is:

**PROOF/RANGE QUALIFIED + LIMITED STOCK / CONTROLLED ISSUE POSSIBLE.**

The following phrase is prohibited:

**FIELD VALIDATED.**

That field-validation result belongs to the later Nomonhan branch and must not be back-propagated.

## 5. 20 mm and other calibers

20 mm dense-core work remains a useful evaluation/pre-production branch as the Type 97 20 mm production line grows. Larger ordinary artillery and naval major-caliber AP are not automatically converted to dense-core ammunition.
