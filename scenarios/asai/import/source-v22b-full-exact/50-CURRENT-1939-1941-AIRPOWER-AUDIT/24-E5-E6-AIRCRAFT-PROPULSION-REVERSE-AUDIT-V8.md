## 0. Central verdict

The E5-J / E5-JF product closure does **not** break the accepted E5 aircraft population. The principal corrections are interpretive rather than destructive:

1. E5-J 425-455 kgf normal product bands and ~395 kg dry are compatible with the accepted E5 single/twin performance envelopes.
2. E5-JF adds roughly **70-95 kg per engine, center ~85 kg**, but does **not** add a large new maximum frontal area because the E5 centrifugal compressor/diffuser already dominates engine OD.
3. Therefore the old shorthand `JF = obviously slower because the nacelle becomes much fatter` is superseded. At E5 flight Mach numbers the exact J/JF top-speed ranking is an **aircraft H->A measurement**, not a fixed percentage penalty.
4. Static/takeoff/climb/mission advantages of JF remain robust: roughly +20% static thrust and ~15-18% lower same-condition static TSFC before aircraft-local drag/mass/fuel accounting.
5. The 1937 Q4 E5-JF first-flight chronology is retained; any Sep-Oct 1938 first-flight wording is superseded.

### 1.1 Ki-42 JF derivative — technically attractive, not current hardware

The old V7 sentence that 1938 may create a `ko pure-J / otsu JF` pressure remains a **design/procurement possibility**, not evidence that a JF Ki-42 physically exists.

If one replaced a ~395 kg E5-J by a ~480 kg E5-JF while holding the rest of a representative 2.04 t airframe fixed, gross weight rises to ~2.125 t. Static T/W changes approximately:

- pure J: 452 / 2040 ~= **0.222**;
- JF practical/high point: 530 / 2125 ~= **0.249**.

Thus even after the engine mass tax, static T/W rises by roughly 12% in this simple comparison. Takeoff/initial climb/go-around would therefore be materially attractive.

However a real conversion still owes:

- rear package length and mount change;
- CG correction;
- tailpipe/rear fuselage revision;
- free-spool controls/oil/inspection access;
- measured aircraft high-speed drag and thrust-lapse map.

Because the E5-JF fan does not dominate maximum engine diameter, **a large frontal-area penalty is not an automatic reason to reject the derivative**. Conversely, no automatic top-speed gain is credited. As of 1939-04-20 the six accepted Ki-42-line airframes remain pure-J hardware.

- takeoff acceleration: robustly better;
- initial climb: robustly better;
- go-around: robustly better;
- useful mission/endurance: strongly favored after local fuel/drag accounting;
- maximum speed: **OPEN H->A**, not automatically a fixed -5% or -10%.

### 4.1 Geometry correction

E5-J maximum engine OD is ~0.69-0.76 m, dominated by the centrifugal compressor/diffuser. E5-JF fan local casing is smaller than that maximum envelope. The JF aircraft therefore pays primarily:

- additional rear length;
- additional wetted area;
- propulsion mass;
- CG/mount/rear-structure changes;
- added spool/control/inspection burden.

Any older statement that explains the JF top-speed penalty mainly through a much larger maximum frontal area is superseded.
