# RELATIVE MOBILITY / GEOMETRY / TIMING — CANON — V8 NOMONHAN-A2

## Purpose
A hardware mobility delta is not a top-speed multiplier. When two worldlines use the same unit, attack plan, and named vehicle but one vehicle has different acceleration, grade retention, recovery, or transient power, the individual vehicle trajectory must be recomputed before copying a historical kill, obstacle encounter, or arrival time.

## Core rule
**same unit + same plan != same individual vehicle trajectory** when mobility differs.

Replay sequence:
`power at local conditions -> power/weight -> traction/gearing/thermal gates -> acceleration/grade/soft-ground behavior -> formation spacing -> arrival/turn/reverse timing -> relative geometry -> exposure -> hit/damage/recovery`.

Do not apply a blanket percentage to battlefield speed or survivability.

## Mobility realization bands
These are replay working bands, not measured track-test numbers:
- sustained good-road maximum: **~39-40 km/h center**, only ~+3-5% versus historical practical 38 km/h; 41-42 km/h is at most short/conditional;
- acceleration / post-turn recovery: **~10-20% shorter time** depending speed range;
- firm rough-ground average: **~+8-15%**;
- power-limited grades: **~+12-18%** speed/retention advantage;
- soft ground: **~+3-10%** center because extra mass and traction losses consume power;
- short escape/reverse/re-acceleration transient: **~+15-25%** usable margin;
- once wire/track gear becomes mechanically bound, additional engine power may provide little or no escape benefit and may worsen entanglement.

## Road-wheel / gearing guard
Engine output alone does not authorize a 50 km/h Chi-Ha. Historical gearing and road-wheel rubber heating remain independent gates. Unless a separate road-wheel/rubber/gear thermal qualification is closed, sustained road speed remains near 39-40 km/h.
