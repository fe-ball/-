## 1. Small mobile GT utility sets

Closed anchors:

- 1937 summer: limited military use / low single-digit to low-teens yearly delivery scale;
- 1938-06-30: cumulative operational/evaluation population **~15-25**;
- 1939-06-30 future branch: **~32-45**.

The coherent Apr20 bridge is:

**~28-40 cumulative operational/evaluation sets, center in the low/mid-30s.**

This is a working stock-flow closure, not a serial-number ledger.

## 2. What the set is

The normal set is a road-mobile or towable **small GT/APU-derived power module in the tens to low-hundreds kWe class**, with generator/switchgear/cables and role-specific auxiliaries.

Typical roles already being evaluated/used:

- field/rear workshop power;
- battery charging, lighting and signals/HQ power;
- airfield ground support;
- pumps/water/engineer work;
- medical/maintenance utility.

A vehicle carrying the module need not be an Asai-designed chassis. The module/interface is the Asai product.

## 3. Large mobile E4 branch

E4 ~400-500 kWe mobile/towed power remains exceptional. Preserve roughly **1-3 special trial/engineering sets** as the carryover scale; do not turn them into forward tactical generators.

## 4. Workshop / recovery package

Asai improves existing trucks/tractors with:

- turbocharged heavy-duty engines where appropriate;
- winch/towing equipment;
- mobile power;
- drills/grinders/small machine tools;
- gauges, battery charging and lighting;
- standardized shallow spare sets.

From ~10-20 at end-1937 and ~15-25 by 1938H1, a reasonable Apr20 working population is **~25-40 enhanced packages** across major users/theaters. This remains a small high-leverage support population, not a new armored-recovery fleet.

## 5. Direct GT traction demonstrator

Retain the 1938 ~15 t vehicle/tank demonstrator:

- ~300 hp continuous;
- ~340 hp short;
- technically successful.

General adoption remains rejected because dust, part-load fuel economy and fuel logistics dominate. The branch stays useful for winter/cold-start, transmission and thermal-management research.

## 6. Diesel exhaust-turbo route

For ordinary heavy vehicles, exhaust turbocharging is the mature route. It buys loaded pull, altitude/temperature compensation and towing margin without the GT traction penalties.

Do not convert this into a blanket increase in Japanese truck count or motorization.

- `LIMITED STANDARD` Army/corps issue family;
- `FIELD VALIDATED` support equipment;
- post-Nomonhan procurement expansion.

Those are later events if the combat branch again reaches them.
