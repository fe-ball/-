# Protagonist Cheat / Authority Guard — V18

## Mandatory interpretation

The protagonist must not drift to either extreme:

1. **Ruler drift** — "Iwao knows the answer, therefore the Navy/state/industry obeys and resources appear."
2. **Manager drift** — "Iwao only has foresight and project-management skill; specialists still have to discover the engine/GT answers from scratch."

Both are wrong.

### Core technical domain

Existing parent canon states that his real profession is engines / GT / jet, with strong extension into metallurgy and aviation, and that he knows the answer and route sufficiently to open the idea gate almost for free in-domain.

In practice this means he can directly contribute to:

- compressor/turbine/combustor architecture.
- rotor/shaft/bearing/seal and balancing logic.
- thermal expansion/cooling/lubrication failure logic.
- test instrumentation and fault isolation.
- the difference between useful endurance testing and wasted testing.
- known dead ends and dangerous resonance/temperature regions.
- manufacturing tolerances/material-property questions that dominate real rotating machinery.

### What still costs time/resources

- alloys and raw material supply.
- casting/forging quality.
- machine-tool capability.
- production tolerances/yield.
- endurance and corrosion proof.
- training and maintenance.
- customer requirement/adoption.
- budgets, shipyard/plant slots and political support.

### Distance rule

Farther subjects use Iwao's general technical judgment, measurement/test culture and industrial infrastructure, but retain independent local specialists. A naval architect, hydrodynamicist, gun/fire-control expert, cryptanalyst or physician is not replaced by Iwao.

### Future audit rule

When evaluating an Iwao-core technology, do not automatically use "historical development time minus a few years". Compress the search/idea/failure-discovery loop strongly when the real limiting factor is known knowledge. Do not compress physical factory/material/endurance gates without a separate reason.
