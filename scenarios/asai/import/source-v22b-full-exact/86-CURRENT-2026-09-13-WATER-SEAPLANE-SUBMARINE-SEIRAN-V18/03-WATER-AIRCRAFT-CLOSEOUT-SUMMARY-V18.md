## Cross-system rules

- Water-aircraft gains should preferentially be spent on takeoff margin, thermal margin, reliability and repeatability when mission value exceeds a few km/h of headline speed.
- CRP is mission-dependent. It survives for E15K/Kyofu where torque/power-diameter constraints justify it, but loses for E16A/N1K2 and likely standard Seiran once a normal 4-blade passes its gates.
- Do not erase water takeoff hydrodynamics with horsepower. High wing loading can still increase takeoff speed/distance even when acceleration is better.
- Do not confuse a technical qualification shadow with actual service introduction in the frozen replay.
