# Submarine / I-400 / Seiran Consolidation — V18

## 1. Pre-I-400 inheritance

I-400 must inherit the existing 1938–43 submarine canon rather than resetting to historical-quality machinery/operations:

- standardized dive/trim trajectory acceptance.
- improved lead-battery separator/support/QC and 350–450-cycle target bands.
- hydrogen ventilation / charge restrictions / shock-safety improvements.
- main motor insulation/temperature/brush/commutator rating maps.
- own-noise vs rpm maps, quiet/avoid bands, cavitation maps.
- engine fault-card/EGT/bearing/shaft-vibration culture.
- radio reliability / moisture / EMI / power-conditioning improvements.
- E14Y packaging/catapult/corrosion/work-sequence experience.
- Type 95 body unchanged in headline performance; FCS3 can be integrated on new build.

Do not import the separate 12/15 kt underwater-speed attack-submarine electrical branch into I-400.

## 2. Strategic branch

Restore Yamamoto's concept as the strategic requirement rather than pre-optimizing toward the smaller AM design.

- ultra-long-range strategic attack submarine.
- design evolution from 2 aircraft toward 3 aircraft is reasonable once the huge range/hangar hull tax is already being paid.
- initial future-history planned requirement may remain **18 boats**.
- completion count is not canon here; actual yard/resource release is the next procurement audit.

## 3. I-400 working central specification

- length ~120–122 m; beam ~12 m.
- surface displacement ~5,100–5,300 t; submerged ~6,400–6,600 t.
- 3.5 m x ~30–31 m pressure hangar.
- 3 Seiran.
- mature Type 22 10-cylinder-equivalent diesels, ~7,700–8,000 hp total.
- surface ~18.5–19 kt.
- ordinary large lead battery 120 cells x3 = 360 cells, with V18 inherited quality/safety package.
- historical-style underwater motor architecture ~2,400 hp, ~6.5–7 kt.
- safe depth ~100 m class.
- 8 bow tubes / about 20 torpedoes central first-block branch.
- FCS3 can be integrated for torpedo fire control.
- 14 cm deck gun retained in first central concept; removal is a later-block comparison, not automatic.

## 4. AM branch

AM remains a useful two-Seiran support/common-aircraft branch for distributed special attack/reconnaissance. It is **not** the default replacement for I-400.

## 5. Seiran central engine / packaging

- E7-M lower-flow TP + 3.20–3.25 m 4-blade central if 1942 Q3 naval qualification gate passes.
- Atsuta 32 is the schedule fallback and remains technically respectable.
- E7-S-T is intermediate fallback only under specific maturity conditions.
- E7-M CRP is a comparison branch and normally loses if the 4-blade passes.
- keep 3.5 m storage envelope common to I-400 and AM.

## 6. Attack / accuracy model

Do not use a single fixed hit percentage.

Use:

1. common formation/mission error: target acquisition, weather, navigation, target ID, attack-axis disruption.
2. individual aircraft setup/release error.
3. weapon functioning.
