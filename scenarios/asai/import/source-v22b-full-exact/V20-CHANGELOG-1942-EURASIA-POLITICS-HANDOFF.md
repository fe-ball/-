# V20 CHANGELOG — 1942 EURASIA / POLITICS HANDOFF

V20 advances the replay from V19's 1941-05-31 clock through **1942-08-31 24:00** and records the major engineering/economic/naval audits developed during that replay.

Major additions:
- Barbarossa, Continuation War, Kantokuen, Soviet transfer choices and 1941-42 eastern-front downstream effects replayed through Aug 1942.
- South Indochina occupation and historical oil-embargo cascade removed absent their trigger.
- No Pearl Harbor/Pacific War in Dec 1941; no historical German-US declaration; no Tojo Cabinet formation from the historical Oct 1941 crisis.
- Third Konoe Cabinet continues; Matsuoka is removed in July 1941 after losing the immediate-northward-war dispute.
- Kwantung grows from ~13 divisions in June 1941 to ~19 by late 1941; two additional divisions ordered north on 1942-08-24 with ~21 target during September.
- Soviet transfer ledger becomes unit-specific: selected Primorye/coastal formations and 58th Tank Division remain east; a later 1942 transfer wave is also partly blocked.
- Moscow still holds; Soviet winter counteroffensive still occurs; Stalingrad approaches are weaker in 1942 because some historical Far-East reinforcements remain opposite Japan.
- Japan strategic policy expanded from a narrow northward-opportunity model to parallel gates: N-OPP, N-PREV, N-ACC, S-COM, S-POL, S-CRISIS, S-WAR, HOLD.
- China-peace economic dividend consolidated: shipping/fuel/trade recover first, fiscal/labor/civilian relief later; Kantokuen consumes part of the dividend.
- Carrier conversion gates, 1942 carrier-balance logic, Taiho/No.800/801/802 policy and No.800 medium-standard design band recorded.
- Naval hydrodynamic/propulsion optimization reinterpreted correctly: no arbitrary bonus, but real causally justified efficiency gains are allowed.
- Marine E6/E7 recuperation workbands, exhaust-oxygen/high-pressure-steam combined-cycle research, C0-C7 gates and ideal E7 leader study recorded.
- Carrier catapult CAT0-CAT6 gates recorded; Taiho structural reservation separated from actual launcher qualification.
- Global-war branch recorded: no Japanese Indian-Ocean war, stronger British Middle-East reserve, working Gazala/Tobruk divergence, no automatic Madagascar/Torch.
- Next major topic changed from tactical replay to political perception: post-Soviet Eurasian order, German-Japanese spheres, Soviet neutrality price, US/UK reaction triggers and actor misperception.

V20 does not claim that all working engineering/economic bands are exact observed quantities. It marks them by status and preserves explicit-only supersession.
