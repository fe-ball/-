# V12 FIXED-WING CLOSEOUT HANDOFF — 2026-09-08

## Status
This directory is the highest fixed-wing planning/procurement authority in V12 for aircraft that can exist, be tested, be procured, or be service-released through **1941-12-31**.

It supersedes V11 `78-CURRENT-2026-09-08-AIRCRAFT-DEVELOPMENT-CLOSE/` only where this directory is more specific. V11 remains the development-history and industrial-feasibility base.

**No combat clock advances.** China remains closed at 1939-12-31 24:00, Nomonhan remains revised-closed at 1939-08-31 24:00, and 1940 combat remains unstarted behind the existing weapon-appearance gate.

## Closing rules
1. `factory accepted`, `service released`, `unit assigned`, and `operationally available` are different states.
2. A research or service-evaluation aircraft is not ordinary OOB simply because it can fly from a military field.
3. Service-fit identities inside common E6 Twin production are not additional airframes.
4. Existing production aircraft converted into development articles are not counted twice.
5. Formal designation is applied only after its naming gate; pre-gate X/research work is not back-dated.
6. Old quarantined branches remain provenance-only.

## 1941 serial-production center
V12 closes the **1941 factory-acceptance planning center** at:
- **E6 Twin: 228 aircraft** (`216-240` acceptance band), with 240-aircraft firm-class production reservation plus 72 rolling option capacity;
- **E6 Single: 195 aircraft** (`180-210` acceptance band), with 216-aircraft firm-class reservation plus 48 options.

These are procurement/factory-planning values, not a statement that all 423 aircraft are simultaneously assigned, serviceable, or present in a particular theater on 1941-12-31.

## Service-fit no-double-count rule
Navy E6 reconnaissance and high-speed land-attack fits, Army/Navy high-cover, and other common E6 Twin mission fits draw from the same E6 Twin acceptance pool. `R2As`, `G4As/G4As1` or similar service-fit labels never create extra airframes.

## Powered-lift / extreme-STOL closure
The 1941 practical path is **powered-lift STOL**, not VTOL:
- use E6-M-JF bypass/cold-flow deflection and blown high-lift surfaces first;
- retain aerodynamic-control authority by staying above very-low-speed hover conditions;
- no 1941 production-standard vertical landing, hover, or large-angle hot-core vector system;
- one existing E6 Twin can be converted to a powered-lift demonstrator;
- A8N No.2 is the natural dedicated 1941 carrier-airframe powered-lift article.
