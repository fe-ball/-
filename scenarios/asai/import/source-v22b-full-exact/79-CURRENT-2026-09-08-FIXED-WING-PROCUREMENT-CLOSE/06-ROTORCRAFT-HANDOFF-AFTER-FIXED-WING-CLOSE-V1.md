# ROTORCRAFT HANDOFF AFTER V12 FIXED-WING CLOSE

V12 closes the 1941-or-earlier fixed-wing development/procurement architecture. It deliberately does **not** promote the recent helicopter discussion to current closeout authority.

The rotorcraft audit may use the following already-inherited anchors, but must close them separately:
- Asai helicopter research begins in the early 1930s and reaches free flight before 1939;
- two small piston-helicopter development airframes exist by 1939 in current/support history;
- Kayaba remains an independent rotorcraft/autogyro research actor; research meetings/data exchange do not imply forced joint design;
- a small E5-S-S turboshaft helicopter around the 330/360-shp class and ~1.35-t class is a plausible 1940-41 candidate, but its exact current geometry, mission inventory and procurement remain to be formally closed;
- medium/heavier rotorcraft remain a separate scale/transmission/rotor-structure problem and must not be inferred merely from engine power.

Fixed-wing STOL / powered-lift knowledge may inform rotorcraft mission thinking or test methods, but fixed-wing high-lift performance must not be copied into rotorcraft aerodynamics.
