# POWERED-LIFT / STOL CLOSEOUT THROUGH 1941 — V12

## STOL development ladder
V12 recognizes a continuous technical ladder rather than a sudden Harrier-like jump:
1. aerodynamic STOL — P1/P2 and military light STOL;
2. propulsive STOL — propwash + high-lift devices, especially P2/A7M school;
3. powered-lift STOL — JF cold/bypass flow used for high-lift augmentation and limited vectoring;
4. STOVL/VTOL — future research beyond the 1941 fixed-wing closure.

## P2 field-performance planning authority
For P2 ~2.8 t MTOW / ~27 m2 / E5-S-T ~952-1,000 hp / power-off CLmax ~2.4-2.6:

| condition | takeoff ground roll | 15 m obstacle takeoff | landing ground roll |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| light 2.4-2.5 t | 55-75 m | 120-170 m | 100-140 m |
| normal 2.5-2.6 t | 60-85 m | 130-185 m | 110-160 m |
| MTOW 2.8 t | 75-105 m | 160-220 m | 140-190 m |
| hot/high/soft | normally ~1.3-1.8 x appropriate baseline | same direction | same direction |

The old `118-123 km/h approach` number is retained only as a conservative MTOW planning point. Normal landing-weight planning should separate:
- Vs ~84-91 km/h;
- final ~105-115;
- threshold ~100-108;
- touchdown ~95-105.

## P2 deck demonstrator
One existing P2 may be modified in 1940 as a carrier-landing technology demonstrator:
- temporary hook/local reinforcement;
- arrest-load instrumentation;
- go-around/prop-response/deck-wind/landing-gear testing.
It is not a serial carrier P2 and is never counted as a new aircraft.

## Propeller beta/reverse
- 1939 production P2: constant-speed + feathering is sufficient;
- 1939H2: ground-beta / zero-thrust research may begin;
- 1940: limited beta/low-thrust flight/ground testing;
- full reverse remains experimental, not a 1939 standard feature.

## JF cold-flow powered lift
The practical 1941 path uses the E6-M-JF **bypass/cold stream** before attempting large-angle hot-core vectoring.

Development concept:
- preserve core exhaust predominantly aft;
- use a movable cold-flow cascade;
- direct bypass flow toward large Fowler/high-lift surfaces;
- use mechanical/hydromechanical detents/interlocks rather than a future digital control system;
- preserve enough forward speed for normal aerodynamic pitch/roll/yaw controls to remain effective.

### 1941 targets
- powered CLmax target ~2.8-3.0 from a ~2.4-2.6 power-off baseline where geometry supports it;
- extreme-STOL takeoff/approach reduction is a test target, not a guaranteed production value;
- 0/20/45-degree cold-flow cascade states are an acceptable test architecture;
- no 1941 hover or vertical landing claim;
- no production-standard large-angle hot-core vectoring.

### Demonstrators
- one existing E6 Twin can be converted for 1940Q4-1941 flight research;
- A8N No.2 is the natural dedicated 1941 powered-lift carrier-airframe article;
- baseline A8N remains independently flyable/launchable without powered lift.

## Critical measured opens
The following require actual test data and remain intentional OPEN:
- bypass/vector thrust loss;
- powered CLmax;
- asymmetric-engine roll/yaw/lift transient;
- nozzle/cascade synchronization and load;
- flap hinge/load and structural fatigue;
- deck/ground erosion, crosswind and FOD effects.

These opens do not reopen the 1941 fixed-wing development architecture.
