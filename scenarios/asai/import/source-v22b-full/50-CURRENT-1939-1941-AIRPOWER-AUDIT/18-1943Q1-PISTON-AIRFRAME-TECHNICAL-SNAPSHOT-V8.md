# 1943Q1 PISTON / AIRFRAME TECHNICAL SNAPSHOT — V8

> Technical maturity snapshot only. Not a combat OOB or procurement closure.

## Engines

### Homare
Good 20/21-class qualified engines exist in limited/priority availability. ~1,950–2,000 hp takeoff/emergency class; near 6 km ~1,600–1,680 hp good-engine band depending map/configuration.

### Ha-43
Strong development/qualification object; selected prototype engines available, but broad series allocation is not automatic.

### Older engine families
Remain volume/service workhorses where adequate. Improved process/QC may keep them reliable and cheaper than newest engines.

## Propeller/cooling

High-power four-blade/advanced combinations can be PA-Q/selected PA-S by program if developed since 1940–41. Cooling remains aircraft-specific.

## Exhaust

New high-speed radial aircraft normally begin EX2/EX3 by this date; large collector-to-stack delta is mostly gone for new designs.

## Materials

Advanced composites/materials exist only where local qualification/production ledgers permit. No universal structural replacement.

## Aircraft implication

A 1943 new fighter may plausibly expose aerodynamic/landing/armament/range tradeoffs rather than simply waiting for an engine. But mass production, unit fielding and combat effect remain branch decisions.
