# GT LIFE / RATING SEMANTICS CANON — V8

## Purpose

Prevent confusion between material/core life, scheduled inspection, overhaul/removal interval and emergency/high-rating use.

## Distinguish

- **material/hot-section life coordinate:** engineering endurance under defined condition;
- **inspection interval:** time between checks;
- **scheduled removal/OH target:** product-service planning interval;
- **fleet observed MTBO:** operational statistic;
- **emergency/high-rating minute budget:** cumulative short-duration use requiring special inspection.

One does not replace the others.

## Rating

A higher TIT/thrust point may exist as:

- bench development;
- selected-material short rating;
- engineering capability;
- qualified normal rating;
- emergency rating.

Do not call all of them “engine thrust.”

## E5 example

Current E5 core life coordinates remain:

- Ni @800C ~400–700 h;
- non-Ni @800C ~150–300 h;
- non-Ni ~765C ~350–600 h.

Product scheduled-removal/OH may be shorter than those numbers.

## E6/E7

Use local product files. A capability shadow at higher TIT does not authorize service TBO.

## Field use

Starts, rapid throttle changes, dust, salt, poor fuel/oil, overheating and mission duty can reduce effective life. Fleet statistics require actual field data.
