# GT ADVANCED BRANCH 1939 STATE CANON — V8

## Purpose

Close subsystem-level 1939 readiness for technologies that later appear in E7/E8/E9 so they are neither reset to zero nor promoted to finished engines.

## VSV / variable stator

By 1939:

- variable inlet guide vane experience exists;
- multi-row variable-stator concept is known;
- rigs/actuation/control studies are plausible;
- no broad series multi-row VSV compressor product is automatically closed.

State: **component/rig development**.

## Multi-spool compressor

By 1939:

- architecture known;
- rotordynamic/bearing/control implications understood;
- shaft/bearing/seal test articles possible;
- no integrated qualified E7+ two-spool flight engine.

State: **architecture + subsystem rigs**.

## Reheat / variable nozzle

By 1939:

- afterburn/reheat concept known;
- combustor/nozzle rig work possible;
- thermal/nozzle control burden recognized;
- no service reheat aircraft product.

State: **rig/experimental branch**.

## Front fan / low bypass

Aft-fan/free-turbine experience exists. Front low-bypass fan is conceptually accessible but creates new inlet/compressor/spool integration. No 1939 service front-fan engine.

State: **concept/rig branch**.

## Advanced cooling

Hollow/air-cooled turbine parts and coatings are inherited research areas; exact high-temperature E8/E9 integrated product still owes process yield/endurance.

State: **component/material development**.

## High pressure ratio

Higher-PR compressor component work may precede E7 product. Surge/start/off-design and manufacturing precision remain gates.

## Guard

These states are sufficient to justify continued parallel research and later faster integration. They do not create 1939 aircraft thrust, production or combat capability.
