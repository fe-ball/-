# PISTON ENGINE PRE-BRANCH ROLLUP — V8

## Purpose
Compact bridge for 1939–43 piston-engine state. Specific engine canons override this summary.

## Sakae / existing ~1,000 hp class
By 1939–40 mature production engine families benefit from better QC, supercharger/mixture measurement, exhaust/cooling and propeller integration. Do not assume large headline horsepower inflation.

## Kinsei / larger radial class
Mature robust family remains important for bombers/large aircraft. Later improvements should distinguish engine rating from airframe installation.

## Ha-101 / Army bomber radial
1940 redesign/integration is a natural gate for thrust exhaust and production/QC improvements.

## Homare
- late-1939 concept;
- 1940 design;
- 1941 first-run/endurance development;
- 1942 pilot/series introduction;
- 1943Q1 technically mature good-engine specification under intact supply/process.

## Ha-43
- 1940Q3 pre-study allowed;
- 1941Q1 formal design;
- first-running center early 1942;
- 1942H2 selected development-aircraft engines possible;
- 1943Q1 still development/qualification/priority-allocation object rather than universal mature mass engine.

## Liquid-cooled DB601-derived families
Ha-40/Atsuta type installations begin near good individual-exhaust state; radiator/duct recovery and manufacturing/licensing quality are major issues. Do not apply radial collector-to-stack bonuses.

## Turbo / high-altitude branches
Treat separately from ordinary engine production. Turbo hardware/control/duct/fire/oil installation is its own qualification path.

## Central rule
No engine exists at one scalar “technology level.” Always name model/date/rating, production repeatability and aircraft installation.
