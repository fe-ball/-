## Controlling accounting rule
Maintain three ledgers without silent netting:
A. Japan: named hull/boat operational clocks, fuel, ammunition, damage, transit, next-ready time.
B. Allies gross: hull-days, aircraft slots/sorties, route/convoy delay, dockyard/repair, port/base/engineering burden.
C. Counterfactual tradeoffs: historical commitments absent in Branch, Branch-only commitments, and cross-theater reallocations.

"Full cost" does not imply "full combat effect": a committed but non-flying aircraft slot still costs resources, but does not suppress an IJN contact unless the patrol physically occurs.

## Correction: ILLUSTRIOUS leaves the Indian Ocean in July, not August
Historical INDOMITABLE and the 19th Destroyer Flotilla were withdrawn from the Eastern Fleet on 9 July 1942. INDOMITABLE reached Gibraltar on 9 August and immediately joined PEDESTAL.

Branch canon keeps PEDESTAL GO, with ILLUSTRIOUS replacing the sunk INDOMITABLE. Therefore the Branch working transit clock must mirror the historical one closely enough to make Gibraltar by 9 August. Center timing:
- ILLUSTRIOUS departs Eastern Fleet area approximately 9 July 1942 (working tolerance +/-1 day).
- Eastern Fleet modern-carrier gap therefore opens during WIO-3 itself.
- FORMIDABLE is already sunk in Branch; HERMES survives but is not treated as a fleet-carrier performance equivalent.

Historical STAB is still not inherited because its immediate trigger was the historical US Solomons/Guadalcanal operation. However, cancellation of STAB does NOT free a modern carrier in Branch: ILLUSTRIOUS is already committed westward to PEDESTAL. The avoided STAB credit applies only to the surface ships, destroyers, dummy-convoy/merchant burden, fuel, and associated sorties that are actually avoided and can be explicitly reassigned.

## First true H2 divergence gate: Japanese Operation B
Historical anchors now establish that a second Indian Ocean/Bay of Bengal commerce-raiding operation was materially staged at Mergui in late July/early August 1942:
- DesDiv 2/DesRon 3 moved Kure -> Singapore -> Mergui for a planned commerce-raiding incursion into the Bay of Bengal/Indian Ocean.
- SUZUYA and KUMANO reached Mergui 30 July and were fueled/provisioned there in early August.
- ISUZU was moving into the same Indian Ocean operation chain, reaching Mergui 8 August.
- In history the raiding force was aborted/redeployed because WATCHTOWER/Guadalcanal opened on 7 August; SUZUYA/KUMANO departed Mergui for the South Pacific that same day, and the Burma-area destroyer forces followed.

Branch consequence:
- Historical WATCHTOWER/Guadalcanal emergency is absent.
- Therefore Operation B MUST NOT be auto-cancelled.
- It also MUST NOT be auto-executed at full historical planning strength. Branch limited-FS preparation for Santo (operation window 23 Aug-2 Sep) competes for cruisers, destroyers, oilers, and transit time.

WIO-4A must adjudicate three possibilities from named-hull clocks:
1. FULL/near-full Operation B executes.
2. TRUNCATED Operation B executes, then selected hulls turn east for Santo support.
3. Operation B is cancelled for a new Branch-specific reason tied to limited-FS priorities, not Guadalcanal.

No center choice is certified in v062; exact OOB, sail date, route, search geometry, and Allied traffic are next-step work.

## Madagascar consequence
Historical STREAM-LINE-JANE relied heavily on ILLUSTRIOUS. Historical planning/operations used roughly 45 FAA aircraft from the fleet carrier, plus WARSPITE, cruisers, destroyers, ALBATROSS, minesweepers and land-based air. In Branch:
- ILLUSTRIOUS is in PEDESTAL and is mission-killed there on 12 Aug under existing Branch canon.
- FORMIDABLE is already sunk.
- HERMES survives and 814 NAS/Swordfish manpower-aircraft continuity exists, but HERMES is not a one-for-one substitute for ILLUSTRIOUS, especially for fighter cover and sortie mass.

Therefore historical STREAM-LINE-JANE cannot be copied unchanged. Britain must choose some mixture of:
- HERMES + surviving FAA package,
- greater land-based RAF/SAAF commitment,
- additional cruiser/destroyer surface cover,
- slower/staged amphibious timing,
- or higher accepted operational risk.

This is the main Madagascar tradeoff gate and must be charged gross before later checking whether Britain can pay it alongside PEDESTAL/TORCH/Atlantic-Mediterranean commitments.
