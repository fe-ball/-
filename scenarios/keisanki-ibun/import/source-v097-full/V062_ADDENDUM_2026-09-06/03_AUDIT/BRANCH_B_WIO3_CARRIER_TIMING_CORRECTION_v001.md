# WIO-3 carrier timing correction v001

## Prior v061 wording
v061 left the exact ILLUSTRIOUS east-to-west sailing clock as a timing gate and only stated that she could not be assumed freely available for a late-July Madagascar sweep.

## Correction
The timing gate is now closed.

Historical Eastern Fleet proceedings state that INDOMITABLE and the 19th Destroyer Flotilla were withdrawn on 9 July 1942. INDOMITABLE reached Gibraltar early on 9 August and sailed into PEDESTAL hours later.

Branch canon requires ILLUSTRIOUS to take the lost INDOMITABLE's PEDESTAL position. A materially later departure from the Indian Ocean would not preserve the historical Gibraltar assembly clock. Therefore center working departure from Eastern Fleet is 9 July +/-1 day.

## Consequences
1. The Eastern Fleet's large modern flight-deck shortage begins at the opening of WIO-3, not after it.
2. Historical cancellation of STAB remains a Branch credit, but it does not make ILLUSTRIOUS locally available.
3. HERMES is the surviving local carrier hull but is not assigned ILLUSTRIOUS-like fighter/sortie performance without a separate air-group reconstruction.
4. Historical September Madagascar carrier support by ILLUSTRIOUS is impossible under current Branch PEDESTAL damage canon; STREAM-LINE-JANE requires re-adjudication.
5. WIO-3 direct merchant-sinking result is unchanged: Branch extra 0 ships / 0 GRT in 9 Jul-4 Aug.
