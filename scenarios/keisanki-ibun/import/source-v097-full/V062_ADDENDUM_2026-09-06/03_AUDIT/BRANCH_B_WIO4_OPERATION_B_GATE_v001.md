# WIO-4 — Operation B activation gate v001

Status: OPEN
Window of immediate decision: 1942-08-05..08-15

## What is now established
Operation B was not a paper-only idea. A substantial raiding package had moved to or toward Mergui and was being replenished:
- SUZUYA/KUMANO at Mergui from 30 Jul, fueled 1 Aug and otherwise supported in the first week of Aug.
- DesDiv 2 and other DesRon 3 destroyer elements had moved to Mergui specifically for a commerce-raiding incursion into the Bay of Bengal/Indian Ocean.
- ISUZU was assigned to Indian Ocean operations and reached Mergui 8 Aug.
- Historical 7 Aug WATCHTOWER caused immediate eastward redeployment; the commerce raid was aborted.

## Branch trigger logic
WATCHTOWER as historically executed does not occur. The original abort trigger is therefore absent.

But Branch limited FS is not free:
- Santo assault window begins 23 Aug.
- Any raider retained west of Malacca consumes transit time, destroyer/cruiser availability, fuel/oiler capacity and readiness that might otherwise support Santo or the broader Pacific screen.
- Branch US carrier force is far weaker after MO/MI, so the marginal need to mass every available heavy cruiser/destroyer in the southeast is lower than in history. This is a pro-Operation-B factor, not an automatic result.

## Mandatory adjudication sequence
A. Freeze exact Mergui OOB by named hull as of 5-8 Aug.
B. Determine which hulls are genuinely required for Santo first-echelon cover and which are discretionary.
C. For any Operation B force retained west, calculate earliest sortie, Bay of Bengal ingress, commerce-search days, fuel expenditure, ammunition, and earliest turn-east date.
D. Reconstruct Allied traffic actually present under the already-active British route-control system; do not reuse April traffic density wholesale.
E. Run contact/attack/survival events ship by ship.
F. Charge British countermeasures gross, then test the bill against PEDESTAL, Madagascar, Middle East/Atlantic escort requirements.
G. Carry delayed IJN hulls into Santo/New Caledonia clocks; no free teleport/reassignment.

## Center prior for next run (not a certified result)
A truncated raid is presently more plausible than either extreme:
- full cancellation lacks its historical Guadalcanal trigger;
- a prolonged full-strength raid risks colliding with the 23 Aug Santo clock;
- the Branch's severe US carrier weakness makes it plausible Japan accepts some temporary Indian Ocean surface commitment while retaining enough Pacific cover elsewhere.

This is only a prior. Exact OOB/transit calculation can overturn it.
