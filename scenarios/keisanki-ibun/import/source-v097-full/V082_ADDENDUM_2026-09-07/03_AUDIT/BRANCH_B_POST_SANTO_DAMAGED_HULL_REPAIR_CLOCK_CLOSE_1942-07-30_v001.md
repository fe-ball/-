## 0. Governing distinction

Every damaged hull is tracked through separate gates:
1. **safe navigation / emergency operation**;
2. **mission-usable release**;
3. **full first-line material release**.

Do not collapse these into a single “repaired” date. Branch computation/quality-control improvements may shorten avoidable diagnosis, work sequencing and repeat repair, but do not erase structural steelwork, drydock transit, machinery alignment, flight-deck work or air-group work-up.

## 4. Allied damaged hulls

### Wasp — 26 Jul: 2 x 250-kg-class bomb hits + damaging near miss

Current condition:

A desperate local-emergency branch could retain Wasp after temporary repair, but Ghormley/Nimitz command posture and the carrier's unique value make that non-center.
