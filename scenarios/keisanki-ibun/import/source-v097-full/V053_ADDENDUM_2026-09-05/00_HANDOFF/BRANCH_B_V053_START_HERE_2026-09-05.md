## Performance interpretation
The existing Branch A6M2-N-equivalent remains:
- maximum speed working band: **444~452 km/h**
- front-line median: **438~447 km/h**
- armament class: 2 x 20 mm + 2 x 7.7 mm
- role: genuine local interceptor / convoy-cover fighter, not merely an armed observation floatplane.

Do NOT treat it as an A6M2 Zero with floats and no penalty.
The center float and outrigger floats impose major drag, acceleration, climb, dive/energy-retention and high-speed maneuver penalties.

Do NOT treat it as helpless against combat aircraft either.
It is especially useful against:
- PBY / Hudson / OS2U and other reconnaissance/patrol aircraft;
- bomb-laden or disorganized SBD/TBF formations;
- damaged/straggling attackers;
- low/medium-speed attack aircraft;
- convoy attackers after land/carrier fighters have tied down the escorts.

Against F4F/P-39/P-38-class modern single-seat fighters it is clearly disadvantaged and should avoid a fighter-sweep duel.

F1M2 is a secondary fighter/top-cover/light-bomber asset, not a modern single-seat fighter. It can attack patrol aircraft and bombers and complicate low-altitude strike geometry, but it must not be converted into "extra Zeros" for accounting.

## Operational output
Water fighter operations are a system:
aircraft + pilots + tender/shore crane + fuel/ammunition + sheltered water + towing/recovery + maintenance + communications + sea state.

Working sustained sortie output:
- established sheltered base: **~60~75%** of a comparable land-fighter unit;
- newly established forward lagoon point: **~45~60%** until moorings, fuel, cranes, spares and maintenance settle.

New Caledonia's west-coast lagoon therefore gives a particularly useful forward operating environment once Boulouparis/La Foa shore access is secured, but reef passages, anchorage attack, sea state and recovery accidents remain real constraints.

## Accounting discipline
Never count a water fighter as "one extra Zero" in aggregate fighter strength.
Use mission-fit:
- modern fighter duel: strongly discounted;
- bomber/torpedo/dive-bomber interception after escort separation: substantial;
- patrol/recon interception: high utility;
- convoy local CAP: substantial utility;
- runway-denial resilience: very high operational utility;
- sustained sortie generation: below land fighters due water-base handling.

The main value is not kill ratio. It is that the enemy cannot make "runway cratered = local Japanese air defense zero" and must devote escort, search and strike capacity to a second aviation substrate.
