# BRANCH B v050 — Session Full Resume: Santo → New Caledonia Re-audit (2026-09-05)

## Why this session changed the old 1942H2 settlement
The old Santo/New Caledonia chain under-modeled the naval war. It had too little surface damage, too little day/night interaction, stale cruiser rosters, and insufficient propagation of damage/ammunition/fuel/aircrew state into later operations.

This session rebuilt the chain from late-September Santo using:
- corrected Branch MI hull losses;
- no historical Guadalcanal/Savo losses unless a Branch substitute occurs;
- Branch naval gunnery/torpedo/reload/night-optics changes;
- Branch aviation reliability/accuracy/search changes;
- actual base geometry (Lunga/Tulagi/Rabaul/PM vs Santo/Efate/Noumea);
- fleet-in-being effects of Washington and Japanese torpedo screens;
- explicit shore-fire ↔ ground-war coupling.

## Air-base system correction
Japan:
- Lunga = forward land-attack base, but Lunga→Santo fighter escort is an extreme-range mission, not routine CAP.
- Tulagi = flying-boat/seaplane/search/contact/recovery “eyes”.
- Rabaul = repair/replacement/logistics reservoir and ferry source.
- PM = western constraint/search/retreat-cover, not a Santo direct-strike base.
- support carriers can materially handle convoy CAP/search/secondary fixed-target tasks in 1942; they are not fully integrated modern carrier warfare but are operationally useful.

Allies:
- Santo + Efate must be treated as a two-base aviation system.
- Noumea is rear repair/reinforcement/long-range aviation support.
- Wasp aircraft can recover ashore after hull damage, so carrier-hull mission kill does not equal air-group deletion.

## Aviation-tech correction
Branch 1942 does not receive fantasy future aircraft in mass.
The main difference is improved median quality/reliability/attack repeatability/search/navigation and preserved veteran nuclei.
Relevant relative improvements under comparable firing/attack opportunity include modest-but-real gains in fighter gunnery, dive-bombing and aerial torpedo accuracy.
Japanese Type 91 aerial-torpedo reliability/setting errors improve; U.S. 1942 Mark 13 problems remain.

## Night-war correction
Japanese Type 93 doctrine is modeled as a cycle:
- firing permission;
- concealed/managed post-launch maneuver;
- enemy maneuver shaping;
- staggered second group;
- reload while opening distance;
- possible second firing solution 12–25 minutes later depending class/damage/geometry.

Washington/SG gives high first-contact and first-fire probability, but not a magical fleet-wide shared 1944 radar picture.
The U.S. is strongest as a radar/gunnery cell around Washington/Helena; escort ships have uneven information/tactical integration.

- runways/coral base;
- roads/drainage/revetments;
- incomplete bomber-field work;
- Segond protected anchorage;
- construction matériel and some heavy equipment.

The U.S. preferentially denies/evacuates:

- flyable aircraft;
- codes/intelligence;

By early Nov center Santo can sustain 45–65 operational aircraft and function as forward fleet staging/aviation base, not heavy repair base.
