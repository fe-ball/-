## 1943-05-11
Version: v001 / 2026-09-10
Status: current working snapshot for the local Aleutians authority

## 1. Attu ground weapons — center

Do not create a new 1944-style arsenal. Branch effect is mainly that weapons intended for the garrison actually arrive, remain serviceable, have ammunition/parts, and benefit from better survey/fire-table/maintenance practice.

Working center:

| Weapon | Physical | Combat-ready center | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Type 88 75-mm AA/dual-purpose | 16 | 15–16 | main heavy AA/ground-fire system |
| Type 41 75-mm mountain gun | 6 | 5–6 | all/most of unit allowance arrives |
| Type 92 70-mm infantry gun | 3 | 3 | historical one plus the two guns normally belonging to the 303rd battalion element |
| 20-mm AA cannon | ~10 | 9–10 | no large count inflation |
| Type 94 37-mm AT/rapid-fire | 3–4 center | 3–4 | minor system |
| medium mortar | 0 center | 0 | grenade dischargers remain important; do not invent mortar pool |
| tanks | 0 | 0 | no automatic transfer from Kiska/elsewhere |

Branch artillery improvement is **repeatability**, not magic destructive power:
- better survey;
- ammunition lot control;
- weather/propellant correction;
- barrel/maintenance state;
- pre-registered ridge/pass/beach approaches.

U.S. artillery superiority remains large.

## 2. Attu stocks / fixed base

By D-day, Attu is no longer a historically shallow emergency position.

Current center:
- food is not an immediate combat clock;
- artillery/small-arms ammunition has dispersed reserve;
- dry storage, drainage, workshop, power/communications and handling facilities are materially improved;
- existing construction stock allows faster runway/radar/position repair after shelling.

U.S. intelligence recognizes visible fortification/airfield growth but underestimates this stock depth.

## 3. Attu radar / warning

Center:
- **one early-warning metric radar station operational**, but with imperfect availability (~70–85% working-use band);
- no SG/PPI/CIC equivalent;
- no radar-directed precision AA/fire-control assumption;
- warning feeds a human-loop plot/telephone/radio system with visual posts and weather observation.

Function:
`warning -> alert -> launch/AA preparation`, not `automatic weapon-quality solution`.

Attu RWR: **none center**.

## 4. Kiska radar / EW

Kiska center:
- **two early-warning radar stations** / stronger warning and plotting structure than Attu;
- more resilient power/repair due to better base depth;
- U.S. can detect/characterize the emissions; radar existence is not a surprise weapon.

Metric RWR:
- one trial/selected set at Kiska is permissible as a working edge, but it provides only bearing/warning;
- it does not detect all U.S. bands and does not create an SG counter-system.

## 5. Kiso / surface sensors

- Kiso: Type-21-class early radar fit is accepted for the local snapshot.
- Trial metric receiver may be carried, but cannot see U.S. 10-cm SG as a universal warning device.
- Japanese surface force still lacks U.S.-quality PPI/CIC / weapon-quality radar tracking.

Night battle asymmetry therefore remains:
- Japan: better navigation, formation, torpedo reliability and battle preparation than history;
- U.S.: earlier/more accurate radar fire-control track in poor visibility.

## 6. Attu airfield and local fighter force

Current center, 11 May:
- runway: limited operational, weather/mud/damage sensitive;
- land fighters physically present: **~11–14**;
- immediate serviceable: **~7–9**;
- aircraft family: Branch Zuisei Zero mix, with late-generation machines already significant; do not reduce the force to a pure historical A6M2/A6M3 assumption.

Mission value:
- radar-cued local interception;
- harassment of Nassau/P-38/P-40 support;
- limited short-range escort for an arriving land-based strike;
- not sustained local air superiority against the U.S. theater air system.

## 7. Kyofu gate

Branch authority puts the first Kyofu operational element in **1943 Apr–May**.

D-day center:
- Attu: **0 Kyofu**.
- Kiska: **0 on 11 May**, but the first 6–8 aircraft northern element is already allocated/being moved.
- Kimikawa mission is therefore a real D+0 decision gate. Current center favors continuing to Kiska rather than aborting solely because Attu is invaded.
- resulting Kiska arrival is small and does not decide Attu by itself.

Do not backdate later 1943 mature Kyofu squadron numbers into 11 May.

## 8. Japanese carrier-air personnel rule

Do **not** apply a blanket strike-accuracy penalty to Ryujo/Zuiho/Junyo/Hiyo merely because they are categorized as second-line/training/work-up carriers.

Branch system:
- training pipeline not brutally shortened;
- carrier decks used for initial qualification/cross-deck/work-up;
- experienced cadres intentionally mixed into new groups;
- historic Guadalcanal/Rabaul aircrew bleed largely absent.

For selected 20–30 aircraft strike packages, crew quality can approach first-line average.

Apply real penalties through:
- hull/deck limits;
- weather launch/recovery;
- carrier-specific aircraft compatibility;
- new-airframe spare/tool/maintenance maturity;
- formation scale and repeated-cycle tempo.

## 12. U.S. local air / artillery baseline

U.S. still has the dominant total theater support system:
- Nassau F4F force;
- P-38/P-40 from Aleutian bases;
- heavy/medium bomber support as weather allows;
- 24 105-mm howitzers and 12 75-mm pack howitzers in the broader Attu force structure;
- radar-equipped surface screen.

Branch change is not U.S. weapon downgrade. It is the interaction with stronger Japanese stocks, a real Attu fighter field, and earlier Japanese carrier response.

## 13. Aircraft-fielding guardrails

- Do not insert Shiden-kai, Reppu, or later 1943 mature airframe counts into May.
- Late-Zuisei Zero is already a real quantitative update, but May does not automatically equal November mature performance on every aircraft.
- B6N and D4Y exist in limited operational fielding; concentrate them rather than spreading them evenly over all carriers.
- New-airframe crews need not be less skilled pilots, but mission-ready rates are lower because spare/tool/maintenance maturity is worse.
