# AL Reverse-Invasion Gate / Bengal-Arakan Option
## 1942-11-20

Status: OPERATIONAL OPTIONS OPEN, NOT EXECUTION-AUTHORIZED

## A. Aleutian reverse-invasion spectrum
Do not default to permanent Adak conquest.

### Amchitka preemptive occupation
Possible, but generally poor return because Japan inherits a difficult base and longer supply line.

### Amchitka destructive counterlanding
Conditional and potentially useful:
- attack shortly after U.S. landing/build-up.
- selected 300–800 men class.
- DD/APD-class fast lift.
- target bulldozers, fuel, radio, generators, landing craft, runway-building machinery.
- purpose is delay/destruction, not permanent occupation.
- only launch if weather/contact/escort gates produce a favorable exchange.

### Attu counterlanding
Existing downstream AL concept remains strategically plausible:
- U.S. attack brings valuable shipping/surface forces into Japanese northern reach.
- Japanese reinforcement/counterlanding can exploit this.
- exact 1943 carrier OOB must be rebuilt from current v093 carrier wallets before execution.
- do not blindly reuse older Ryujo/Zuiho/Junyo/Hiyo numbers.

## B. Bengal / Arakan option
Do not interpret as automatic Calcutta conquest.

The useful concept is limited Arakan–Bengal pressure:
- make Akyab expensive to retake.
- pressure Cox's Bazar / Chittagong.
- periodic Calcutta-area air threat if operationally feasible.
- increase Bay of Bengal convoy/escort/air-defense burden.
- reinforce with limited Army artillery/engineer/AA/infantry packages rather than a new giant invasion by default.
- use submarines as the cheap naval layer.
- CA short raid only with strong target intelligence.
- no automatic fleet-carrier commitment.

## C. Shared strategic principle
AL and Bengal can both be prepared because they draw on partly different resource classes.
They become mutually harmful only if both escalate into large permanent occupations.

The preferable 1942-43 posture is reactive:
let the Allies put expensive forces forward, then concentrate the preserved Sargasso pool where the exchange is favorable.
