# Efate Air Suppression / Pilot Attrition Ledger
## 1942-09-20 -> 1942-11-20

Status: WORKING CAMPAIGN MODEL

## 1. Operational purpose
Japan does not need to occupy Efate if Santo-based air can reduce its sortie-generation value.
Do not seek complete air annihilation; seek to make reinforcement, PBY basing, fuel concentration and routine offensive use expensive.

## 2. Geometry
Santo–Efate: ~155 nm class.
Tontouta–Efate: ~280 nm class.
Therefore:
- Santo is the normal attack base.
- Tontouta is a periodic second direction / timing disruption tool, not daily routine.

## 3. Sep20-Oct20 first phase — working center
- major attacks: 7–9.
- armed recon/sweeps: 8–14.
- defender scramble/alert without meaningful air combat: 5–10.
- one highly effective Santo+Tontouta two-direction attack; later attempts lose surprise effect.

Air losses:
Japan:
- Zero 6–9 irreversible.
- G3M/G4M 2–4 irreversible.
- Zero pilots 4–6 irreversible, 2–4 temporary injury/absence.
- bomber crews 12–20 irreversible class.
- ground aviation personnel 10–25 casualties class.

Allies/Efate:
- fighters 20–28 irreversible.
- other aircraft 5–10.
- fighter pilots 7–10 irreversible, 5–8 temporary injury/absence.
- ground aviation personnel 30–60 casualties class.

Because U.S. pilots fight over their own base, aircraft-loss ratio is more favorable to Japan than irreversible pilot-loss ratio.

## 4. Training vs grinder rule
Track:
- flight load
- combat-contact load
- irreversible crew loss
- rescue rate
- ground exposure
- evacuation throughput
- leader cadre

General interpretation:
- low weekly irreversible crew loss + repeated survivable combat = valuable real combat training.
- high loss rate, especially leader-cadre loss, flips the system into attrition even if aircraft replacement is abundant.

Japanese best practice:
- small veteran leader fraction + middle layer + limited newcomers.
- do not send the same veteran leaders on every sweep.
- use false alarms / armed recon to raise Allied flight load without always paying combat-contact load.

## 5. Ground/base recovery
U.S. pilots rescued over Efate are not automatically lost:
- island/shore bailout recovery can be high.
- Fiji evacuation remains possible while the base network is open.
But pilots/maintenance/medical/communications personnel remain exposed to subsequent ground bombing.

Japanese Santo is also not safe:
- U.S. counter-raids can inflict small ground-aircraft and personnel loss.
- replacement aircraft do not replace leader cadre.

## 6. Oct20-Nov20 density reduction
Japan reduces bomber participation and overall tempo:
- attack when reconnaissance shows renewed concentration.
- otherwise rely on fighter sweeps, armed recon, ISR and intermittent pressure.

Expected additional month:
- Zero irreversible ~3–5 class.
- bombers 0–2 class.
This is intended to preserve the early-war Japanese aircrew advantage rather than convert Efate into a new Guadalcanal.

## 7. Strategic result
Efate remains Allied, but oscillates below normal sortie generation.
It becomes a resource sink / forward outpost rather than a free offensive air hub.
Occupation is reopened only if:
- fighter MR stays >30 for a week,
- an RCT-size ground reinforcement is confirmed,
- PBY/medium-bomber offensive tempo against Santo materially rises,
- or regular large-shipping use resumes.
