# Global Resync — AL / Sargasso / WIO / Five-Go
## 1942-09 -> 1942-11-20

Status: WORKING GLOBAL CONTINUITY CLOSE

## 1. South Pacific
- Japan holds Midway, PM, Santo, NC strategic zone.
- Efate is Allied but intermittently suppressed.
- Fiji becomes the major Allied SOPAC defense/build-up node.
- Tonga is a fleet staging/fleet-in-being node.
- New Zealand is a regeneration/staging base for U.S./NZ formations.
- Limited FS ends after NC success; Fiji/Samoa are not automatic.

## 2. Allied force allocation effect
NC loss does not free Fiji/Samoa/Tonga; it makes them harder to strip.
43rd Division-related shipping no longer has historical Santo as destination and is likely staged through NZ/Fiji before any risky Efate forward commitment.
Australia remains a SWPA repair/logistics depth because PM is lost.

## 3. Aleutians
Existing AL close remains:
- Dutch Harbor strikes 6/24–25.
- Kiska 6/27.
- Attu 6/28.
- Ryujo/Junyo released early July after limited AL success.
- Adak U.S. forward build-up follows in Sep.

Late-1942 Japanese policy:
Kiska main base / Attu second base / Semichi-Shemya satellites / Amchitka tripwire.
No automatic Adak occupation.

11–12月 Kiska+Attu working delivery band:
- 13–17kt effective cargo.
- 4–6kt base-forming material.
NC early close makes upper half somewhat easier, not guaranteed.

## 4. Sargasso / Guadalcanal non-attrition
Not a unilateral Japanese buff:
- Japan retains many historically lost carrier-air, CA/DD and trained-aircrew assets.
- U.S. also retains historical Guadalcanal losses that did not occur.
- Branch NC/Efate losses substitute for part, not all, of historical attrition.

By Nov 1942 Japan still has unusually deep:
- carrier hull layer,
- CA/DD layer,
- aircrew cadre,
- Army strategic depth not bled into Guadalcanal/New Guinea.

## 5. Indian Ocean / Madagascar
Operation C losses remain:
- Indomitable and Formidable lost.
- British modern-carrier elasticity is reduced.

Madagascar:
- Japanese rescue/reversal is no longer plausible after Sep.
- useful Japanese response is submarine/recon pressure, convoy-delay pressure, and possibly short CA raids if target intelligence is good.

WIO Branch value:
- direct additional sinking is not the only metric.
- 20–40 large-merchant-ship/month class delay/reroute equivalent and 5–8 escort/ASW hulls of additional binding remain useful working effects.

## 6. China / Five-Go
Do not transfer the Five-Go core divisions to Limited FS.
The 8th Tank Regiment was not consumed as the NC first-response weapon.
Post-NC released shipping reduces winter concentration-risk but does not auto-advance the 1943 spring offensive date.

## 7. Europe / USSR
Keep:
- El Alamein Allied win direction.
- TORCH execution.
- Stalingrad / Uranus broad outcome.
Branch oceanic/logistics effects can alter margin, escort elasticity and lower-priority cargo, not automatically reverse these campaigns.

## 8. Strategic Japanese posture by Nov 20
Best-value strategy is no longer another automatic island conquest.
Use a portfolio:
- South Pacific: suppress/observe.
- AL: fortify and prepare conditional counterlanding.
- WIO: submarines/recon/conditional cruiser raid.
- Burma/Arakan: limited pressure study.
- Five-Go: build logistics.
The common principle is to force Allied defense expenditure while preserving Japanese veteran aircrews and high-value hulls.
