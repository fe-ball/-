# New Caledonia Post-Capture / East Coast / Occupation Ledger
## 1942-09-14 -> 1942-11-20

Status: WORKING CONTINUOUS SETTLEMENT

## 1. East Coast Force
9/14: 12–16k remnants physically withdraw east/southeast.
By 9/16: organized controlled force ~9–11k.
9/30: controlled ~8–9.5k, combat-capable ~6–8k.
Late Oct / Nov working center: controlled ~6–7.5k, combat core ~4.5–6k.

Not a full field division:
- heavy guns/vehicles progressively abandoned.
- small arms, MG, mortars, radio, demolition and reconnaissance dominate.
- malaria is not the main killer; nutrition, foot/skin disease, dysentery-type illness, dengue, medical shortage and attrition matter.

## 2. Allied resupply
Submarine/flying-boat supply is a high-value trickle, not a division-scale logistics system.

9/14–21 center:
- 2 submarine successes / 1 failure.
- 60–80t high-value supplies.
- ~100 persons evacuated by sub/flying boat combined order of magnitude.

9/22–30 center:
- 3 attempts / 1 success.
- 30–40t additional.
- cumulative 90–120t class by month end.
- at least one submarine forced to abort after ASW pressure/damage.

Meaning:
- enough for radio, medicine, batteries, explosives, ammunition, concentrated food and specialist movement.
- not enough for 8–10k men to operate as a normal artillery/vehicle-supported division.

## 3. Japanese blockade
Do not attempt total mountain sweep.
Layers:
- outer: H6K/H8K + submarines watch Australia/Fiji/Efate approaches.
- middle: E13A etc. coast reconnaissance.
- inner: small craft / patrol craft / occasional DD at suspected landing coves.
- ground: passes, roads, ports and western infrastructure secured; 8–12k class may be tied to east-coast containment at times.

## 4. Occupation / civil economy
NC is not only a base.
Japanese administration should seek:
- restoration of water/electricity/hospitals/markets.
- use of French local officials and commercial expertise where possible.
- controlled purchase over uncontrolled requisition.
- preservation/restart of fisheries, coastal trade, agriculture and essential mining workforce.
- avoid blanket punishment that makes local support for U.S. remnants cheaper.

Political model:
Japanese military occupation + retained French local administrative layer + possible Vichy legal cover as a political tool, not as a magical legitimacy solution.

## 5. Nouméa/Tontouta repair
Nouméa:
- immediately after capture: only limited quay/lighter use.
- first week: ~10–15% class working handling capability as an operational abstraction.
- late Sep: ~20–35% class.
- around end Sep/Oct: ~40–50% general cargo class center, fuel system lags and drum/lighter handling remains important.
Exact tons/day remains OPEN pending a dedicated port-damage close.

Tontouta fighters:
- around 9/16: 6–8 MR.
- 9/18–19: 9–12 MR.
- 9/21: 12–16 MR.
- late Sep: 18–22 MR class.
Santo remains active and is not emptied; replacement pipeline fills behind transfers.

## 6. Strategic meaning
The East Coast Force is valuable if it forces Japan to pay occupation/containment cost.
It is not worth unlimited U.S. submarine expenditure.
The U.S. objective shifts from "hold NC" to "deny cheap exploitation and preserve intelligence/harassment".
Japan's objective shifts from "capture every soldier" to "make the remnant strategically harmless at acceptable cost".
