## Non-stacking rule
The v051 class overlay is mostly an engineering/reliability/I-O/DC realization layer. Do not add its percentages as independent hit-rate or damage multipliers on top of global gunfire, torpedo or radar tech.

## Retroactivity rule
v051 does not automatically rewrite prior settled battle outcomes. It applies prospectively from the current NC first-night point and whenever a prior event is explicitly reopened for re-adjudication.
