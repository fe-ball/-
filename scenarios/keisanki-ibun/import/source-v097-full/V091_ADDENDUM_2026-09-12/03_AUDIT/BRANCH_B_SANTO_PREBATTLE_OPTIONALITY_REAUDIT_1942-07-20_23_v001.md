- warm reserve doing another real task;

## 6. Battleship metacognition

Both sides know the opponent can concentrate surface power.

Japan can still create one extra fast-BB option in July by deferring Haruna maintenance. The United States cannot put Washington or South Dakota into the 27-Jul Santo battle on physical transit/work-up clocks.

Therefore at the local D-day horizon:
- Japan: 2 committed fast BB + 1 warm fast-BB reserve;
- U.S.: North Carolina as the only immediate fast-BB center.

This asymmetry strengthens Japan's reason to exploit the July window, while strengthening U.S. incentives to preserve NC/Wasp and buy time for later fast-BB reinforcement.
