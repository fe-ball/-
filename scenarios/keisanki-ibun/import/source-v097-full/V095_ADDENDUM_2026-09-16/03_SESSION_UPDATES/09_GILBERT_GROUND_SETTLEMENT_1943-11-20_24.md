# Gilbert ground settlement — 20 to 24 Nov 1943

## Makin
### 20/21 night
- Japanese R3 counterlanding succeeds: 390–460 personnel + 40–55t.
- Existing first-night ship damage remains valid: Japanese DD sunk, DD heavy, DD light/medium, CL light; US DD sunk + DD heavy; AO heavy mission-kill from separate submarine action.
### 21 Nov
- US combined-arms timetable delayed; Japanese organized strength evening ~700–850.
### 21/22 night
- R2 relief attempt aborted before terminal approach; no Type93 firing. Limited infiltration only.
### 22 Nov
- East Tank Barrier broken ~14:15 center; evening organized strength ~300–430.
### 22/23 night
- R0/R1 terminal delaying action only.
### 23 Nov
- organized C2 ends 13:30–15:00 center. Isolated holdouts remain.

## Tarawa
### 20 Nov
- Worse-than-historical US D-day but landing holds. Historical hard gun count retained; Branch difference mainly survivability/dispersion/C2/repair.
### 21 Nov
- Green Beach captured later than historical; Branch Japanese C2 preserves organized defense. Evening ~2,800–3,100 organized in then-working line.
### 21/22 night
- limited infiltration, no mass terminal attack.
### 22 Nov
- US combined-arms attack expands control to ~55–62% by evening; Japan retains ~1,650–1,950 organized, Shibazaki alive, 2–3 Type95 ready.
### 22/23 night
- distributed 220–300-man limited counteractions; no historical terminal banzai. 23 Nov dawn ~1,450–1,700 organized.
### 23 Nov
- US controls ~75–82% by evening; Japan retains ~750–950 organized. Tarawa becomes highest-value relief target.
### 23/24 night
- R1 air-drop partial success: 0.8–1.4t recovered from 2–3t dropped. I-175 Makin-area attack misses; Makin CVE release delayed by ASW response.
### 24 Nov
- 13:00–14:00: Shibazaki command net ceases effective island-wide control.
- 16:00–17:30: organized resistance ends.
- isolated holdouts 80–150 remain.
- 24 Nov US additional ground loss working band 260–380; final total exact accounting OPEN.

## Strategic meaning
The Branch gain is not permanent island retention. It is added US mission-days and delayed release of CVE/BB/DD/transport/engineering/ASW systems. The effect must be counted as time/mission fragmentation, not multiplied directly into unrelated air/naval combat bonuses.
