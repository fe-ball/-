# Technology audit closeout A1–D5.2

This file is a compact ledger of the 2026-09-16 audit. Detailed reasoning remains in session provenance; current values below supersede earlier open working values.

## A — carrier battle systems
- A1 early warning/fighter direction: Japanese Type21-style warning network is useful for sector cue and CAP vectoring but not US-style CIC. Early warning and plot integration reduce surprise; radar blind fire is not assumed.
- A2 AA fire control: Type94 HA director is a competent tachymetric base. Branch improvements focus calibration, I/O, stable reference, fuse-setting, transfer/reacquisition. Type III HA director remains prototype/evaluation in late 1943, not fleet standard. Akizuki screen materially strengthens but does not equal US radar/VT AA.
- A3 damage control: MI lessons institutionalize fire-main segmentation, foam, avgas isolation/draining, ventilation cut-off, emergency power, hangar ordnance discipline. Damage-state transition risk lower than historical Japanese carriers, not US-equivalent.
- A4 US carrier air wallet: healthy six fast carriers retain large air pool; Mk13 remains poor in Nov 1943. Counterstrike on 21 Nov fails for geometry/contact, not lack of aircraft.
- A5 secondary carriers: Junyo/Hiyo can operate D4Y/B6N with deck-cycle penalty; Zuiho/Shoho/Ryuho limited modern-attack operations; Ryujo fighter-heavy, B6N normal combat ops not credited.
- A6 air-ground separation: hull loss, aircraft loss, flying-personnel loss, direct-maintenance loss and ship-air-department loss are separate ledgers. Unit capability regenerates over days/weeks.

## B — air technology
- B1 Kinsei Zero: 12–15 embarked on main four carriers at sortie, ~11–14 ready for CAP after strike; first long-range strike uses Suisei-powered late Zero because of range margin. Early combat Kinsei Zero ~588–592 km/h center, not F6F-superior.
- B2 Ki-84: first limited operational sentai exists by Nov 1943; ~26–30 ready center. Homare practical output/reliability improved but still maintenance-heavy.
- B3 Ha43-Ki84: comparison prototypes only; production HOLD. Ki-100-equivalent is insurance prototype branch with quantified trigger gates, not production main line.
- B4 Shiden-Kai / Reppu: prototypes flying by late 1943 in Branch, no GALVANIC combat credit. Shiden-Kai Homare land fighter; Reppu Ha43 future carrier fighter.
- B5 Kyofu: ~81 existing / ~53 ready center; Gilbert-Marshall ~17 / ~12 ready. Value is runway-independent air layer; support boats/ramps/fuel/maintenance remain targetable.

## C — escort/logistics/ASW
- C1 kaibokan: physical 19, routine-ready 15–16. Old 29 / 23–25 withdrawn. Mutsure historical loss avoided by Branch ASW doctrine; Manju/Miyake early physical completion but work-up.
- C2 fleet DD: physical 85, routine-ready 75–78 center76; Akizuki-class physical6, four concentrated with main carrier screen. Solomons-loss avoidance, not magical construction, is main Branch surplus.
- C3 Tokai/MAD: assigned48–50, ready39–42, radar33–36, MAD fitted16–18. MAD reliable lateral band ~100–120m; air-to-escort handoff is primary gain. Ka-go remains non-MAD in 1943.
- C4 tankers/oilers: Japanese trunk tanker capacity moderately above historical working line; forward fleet oilers remain scarce. US AO loss disrupts local geometry, not theater fuel supply.

## D — weapons/base/shipbuilding
- D1 Type91 aerial torpedo: Mod3 Improved standard; ~850kg, ~240kg Type97 explosive, 42kt/~2000m, structural high-speed drop margin; normal-run working 94–97% under proper envelope.
- D2 D4Y 500kg anti-ship bomb: Type2 No.50 Model1, heavy forged penetration body / ~65–67kg explosive, short delay. Damage resolved by penetration and detonation location, not flat damage points.
- D3 Type93 surface torpedo: Japanese fire-control base already strong; Branch gains calibration/I-O/reload consistency. SG/CIC US first-track advantage retained. Do not fire without weapon-quality solution.
- D4 island-base system: runway suppression != base neutralization. Warning/C2, land-air, water-air, logistics, engineering, personnel are separate functions. Branch raises repair/dispersion/redundancy, not invulnerability.
- D5 shipbuilding: Taiho ~Feb 1944 physical completion, Unryu/Amagi Jul 1944, Katsuragi Sep 1944 working; Ibuki completes as CA rather than carrier conversion; Yahagi historical Dec 1943 class timing retained.
- D5.1 Shinano/No.6 dock: Shinano float Dec 1942 center, dock release Jan 1943 center; 5002/5005 Unryu-family hulls laid spring 1943.
- D5.2 industrial response: do not model fixed historical resource box. Added demand triggers production response. Nickel is priority war-spec floor; Cu/Mo/large gear/turbine/electrical/director capacity are harder elastic limits. 5002/5005 use Type89 12.7cm AA, not scarce Type98 10cm by default.
