# Branch B — Southeast forward-node network audit start, July 1942 v001

Date: 2026-09-07
Status: **AUDIT START / NOT YET A CLOSED FACILITY LEDGER**
Scope: Japanese rear-area robustness for Limited-FS / Santo, with emphasis on small air/seaplane/logistics/ISR nodes.

## Why reopened

Prior Santo/NC rebuilds under-modeled the Japanese practice of using small anchorages, seaplane bases, tender-supported temporary sites and intermediate fuel/communications nodes as a distributed ISR/recovery network.

Expected effects include shorter search legs, re-acquisition after contact loss, damaged/low-fuel aircraft recovery, local convoy/ASW support, extra raid targets and redundancy. These are not free: fuel, moorings, cranes, spares, radio/weather, tenders, AA, boats and mechanics remain limiting inputs, while heavy repair and strategic fuel stay concentrated.

## Facility classes

1. **Anchorage / temporary point** — communications, fuel boat/drums, moorings or tender support; short-duration floatplane use/emergency recovery; little heavy maintenance.
2. **Forward seaplane base** — fuel, ammunition, radio/weather, rescue craft, basic workshop, limited AA; rotational detachment possible.
3. **Regional seaplane/air logistics hub** — tender/fuel/workshop/spares concentration and multi-detachment rotation; Shortland/Faisi is the prime July-Solomons candidate subject to named audit.

Major land-air/logistics nodes remain separate from these three classes.

## Branch effect allowed

Branch aviation/maintenance improvements may raise reproducibility of small-node operations through documentation, radio/weather procedure, navigation, search-report formatting, recovery/repair cycles and modest availability. They do not grant magic radar coverage, unlimited flying-boat basing, exact fixes from one sighting, zero fuel/spare cost or raid immunity.

Primary effect: denser **acquisition → re-acquisition → handoff**, not direct weapon accuracy.

- PBY/Hudson/B-17/SBD days used on suppression or ASW;

## Working structure

**Distributed ISR/recovery, concentrated heavy logistics:**

`Rabaul / PM / major roots`
→ `Shortland/Buka intermediate layer`
→ `Tulagi/Rekata/Lunga and temporary forward points`
→ `air / submarine / surface-force handoff`

A single raid is less likely to blind the entire southeast network, but strategic fuel, heavy repair, replacement engines, major ammunition and long-term maintenance remain concentrated and targetable.
