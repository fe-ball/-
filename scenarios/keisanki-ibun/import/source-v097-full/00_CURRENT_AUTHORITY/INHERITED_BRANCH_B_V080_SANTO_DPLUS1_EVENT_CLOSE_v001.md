## 8. 10:30–15:30 — Pallikulo defense breaks

The key advantage is combined-arms timing: 75-mm guns, heavy mortars, communications and trucks are already integrated, while the Allied newly arrived regiment still lacks its principal field artillery and has paid the previous night's bombardment/disruption.

Around ~12:45–13:20 Japanese infantry obtains a penetration on the northeastern/eastern side of the Pallikulo belt. Arthur judges that holding the forward base line to destruction risks encirclement without preserving an operational airfield or meaningful heavy fire support.

At ~13:30–14:15 he orders a staged withdrawal toward the Luganville/Segond core.

Edson's surviving mobile nucleus conducts ambush/rear-guard actions at road junctions rather than a second full-scale frontal counterattack.

By **~15:00–15:40 center**:
