# World / Branch delta snapshot by late 1943

## Largest current divergences
1. Japanese maritime capital stock preserved:
   - combat carriers 10 alive vs old Sargasso/weaker branches.
   - battleships 12 incl. Mutsu.
   - heavy cruisers 18.
   - destroyer attrition much lower than historical Guadalcanal line.
2. Merchant/tanker/logistics system healthier:
   - merchant 500GRT+ still ~5.9m GRT late Oct.
   - tanker composition +8–13% usable vs historical working line.
   - dedicated escort/ASW system increasingly replaces DD convoy duty.
3. Geography:
   - Midway, PM, Santo, strategic NC Japanese.
   - Hanzhong Japanese.
4. Allied cost shifts from simple sunk tonnage to carrier-days, suppression-days, escort/oiler cycles and wider defensive geography.

## Europe / global
By late 1943 the European war's main campaign calendar is not yet automatically overturned.
Branch impact is primarily:
- British reserve elasticity and carrier/escort rotation
- Indian Ocean/CBI burden
- Allied shipping/escort day allocation
rather than automatic reversal of Sicily/Italy/Soviet campaigns.

## China economy
New conquests do not deliver an oil bonanza.
Main gains:
- Hanzhong/local food/fodder reduces long-haul ration burden.
- Baoji/Xi'an light industry/repair/textile/alcohol capacity can partly recover.
- more important: stability raises extraction/transport/tax efficiency in already occupied North/Central China.
- black/gray economy remains bilateral; some strategic materials and consumer goods continue to cross fronts.
