# CHINA WEST AIR / HUMP / ICHI-GO SETTLEMENT — through 1944-04-30

## 1. West-front purpose

Hanzhong–Guangyuan is not a Chengdu springboard in the current line. It is an **active pressure front** designed to:
- pin Chinese effective divisions in northern Sichuan;
- force 14AF/CACW fighters, fuel and spares to remain west;
- destroy imported high-value items at the far end of the Hump relay;
- reduce the growth of free stockpiles available for Hunan/Ichi-Go response.

## 2. Air doctrine

Targeting is opportunistic with a high-value bias, not fetishistic target purity.

Priority logic:
1. aircraft / repair-aircraft concentration;
2. aviation fuel / refuel trucks / pumps;
3. spare engines / workshops / radio / radar / specialty tools;
4. munitions / tires / lubricants / power;
5. bridges / runways / roads when they synchronize later high-value attacks or force costly dispersal.

The operational idea is not merely to shoot down 14AF aircraft. It is to make scarce imported assets consume more Hump/China distribution capacity and prevent stockpile growth.

## 4. Hump economics

Key insight: loss at Chengdu/Sichuan is late-chain destruction.

Assam/India -> Hump -> Kunming/China distribution -> Sichuan air base -> operational use.

Destroying fuel/engines/vehicles/aircraft after they pass the Hump consumes not just the item but replacement cargo slots, ferry fuel, maintenance effort, dispersal cost and future stockpile growth.

Mature spring work band:
- aviation fuel/CAP/dispersal: 260–420 t/month;
- parts/tires/ammo/radio-radar etc.: 90–150 t/month;
- repair/AA/comms/other: 70–120 t/month;
- total high-priority extra burden: **450–700 t/month**.

This is not a claim that Hump total tonnage collapses. Hump grows; the free marginal stockpile grows less than headline tonnage.

## 5. Ichi-Go

- Jan24: policy/preparation gate formally moves from study to preparation.
- Apr end: Hunan-centered 10–12 new divisions is the main package.
- West front keeps 2.3–2.8 division-equivalent / 45–58k, pinning 5–7 effective Chinese divisions.
- Combined active pressure is therefore roughly 12–15 division-equivalent.
- Sichuan deep pursuit remains NO-GO unless separately reopened.
- Guangxi continuation is not automatic.
