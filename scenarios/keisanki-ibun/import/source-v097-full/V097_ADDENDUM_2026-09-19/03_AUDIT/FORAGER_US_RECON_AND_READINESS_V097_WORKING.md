# FORAGER — U.S. Reconnaissance / Readiness Working Assessment

Status: FUTURE-PLANNING SENSITIVITY. No event beyond 1944-04-30 is canonized.

## U.S. knowledge model
The U.S. can identify much of:
- troop-convoy increase;
- new/heavier AA and coast guns;
- airfield expansion / dispersal;
- exposed field works and some cave entrances;
- increased operational importance of the Marianas.

The U.S. should NOT pre-understand the full system:
- distributed underground logistics;
- multiple firing positions / reappearance;
- reception redundancy after port suppression;
- small-craft / Tinian local transfer system;
- improved Army–Navy I/O;
- actual aircraft repair/replacement regeneration.

Thus the U.S. sees “a very heavily reinforced island” rather than possessing a mature conceptual model of a Peleliu-type system.

## Schedule
Current planning center:
- June advance remains more likely than a July postponement if no new major May carrier/amphibious loss occurs.
- June 15–22 is a planning sensitivity range only.
- Waiting has diminishing value because Japanese fortification, reinforcement, heavy-gun installation and air concentration also improve with time.

## U.S. force sufficiency
Current interpretation:
- historical-scale Saipan assault package can still be assembled.
- Branch damage reduces margin more than minimum capability.
- fast-carrier / replacement-air / amphibious reserve margins are thinner than desired.
- heavy surface / DD depth is not simply historical-worse because much Guadalcanal/Santa-Cruz cruiser/DD attrition did not occur.

## Ground formation quality
Headcount can be regenerated by June, but veteran/NCO density and replacement share must be tracked separately for Marine and Army assault divisions.
Do not assume a full-strength TO&E equals historical-quality cohesion.
