## 3. Europe / Soviet spillover — current interpretation

Do NOT model as automatic Soviet collapse or delayed Overlord.

Model channels:
- Persian/Arctic/Pacific Lend-Lease timing and ship-month friction;
- RN carrier shortage and Home Fleet / Arctic / Mediterranean / Indian Ocean rotation choices;
- escort/patrol aviation retained in Indian Ocean;
- US/RN strategic reserve erosion;
- same European pressure maintained at cost of shorter rest, older units, thinner reserve, more maintenance/accident/casualty exposure.

No deep U-Go reduces British-Indian battle attrition, BUT this is partly offset by:
- healthy Japanese Indian Ocean fleet/submarine/air system;
- Bengal/Ceylon/Assam/Persian-route defense needs;
- broader Oceania perimeter defense.

Thus “U-Go absent = Allied ground reserve fully freed” is rejected.
