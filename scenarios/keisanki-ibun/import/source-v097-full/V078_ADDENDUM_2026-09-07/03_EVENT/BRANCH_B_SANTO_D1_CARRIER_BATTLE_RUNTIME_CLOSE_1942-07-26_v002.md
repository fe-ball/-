## 0. Why v001 is superseded

v001 correctly preserved three-deck Japanese physical availability at D-1 opening and correctly used Ryujo for the first bounded transport strike. It then treated the Wasp counterstrike defense too locally: the effective CAP was resolved mainly as Shokaku/Zuikaku CAP plus escort AA, while Ryujo's fighter-heavy deck was not explicitly re-integrated into fleet defense after the U.S. carrier was physically found.

That is inconsistent with both the accepted Branch role split and the post-Midway Japanese reorganization logic. The v071 D-4 close gives Ryujo **20 A6M / 10 B5N, 30 MR** as a mixed support/CAP deck. Historical post-Midway CARDIV 1 doctrine likewise assigned Ryujo a fighter-heavy role intended to support fleet defense while Shokaku/Zuikaku concentrated on defeating U.S. carriers.

Therefore the carrier engagement is rerun from the 08:43 Wasp contact forward. Events before that contact are retained unless directly affected.
