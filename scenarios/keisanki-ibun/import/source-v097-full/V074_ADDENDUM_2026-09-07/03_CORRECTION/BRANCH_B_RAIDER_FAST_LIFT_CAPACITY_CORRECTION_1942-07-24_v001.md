# Branch B — 1st Raider fast-lift capacity correction, 1942-07-24 v001

Status: **CLOSED CORRECTION; SUPERSEDES V072/V073 RAIDER-LIFT EFFECT ONLY**

This correction does not reopen the v072 global allocation logic, the location of 1st Raider Battalion in New Caledonia, or the existence/readiness of Little, Colhoun, Gregory and McKean. It corrects only how much of the battalion four original APDs can physically carry to Santo in one fast lift.

## 1. Conflict found

v072/v073 simultaneously carried:
- 1st Raider Battalion: roughly 820–900 physical in New Caledonia;
- 3–4 TransDiv-12 APD-equivalents as fast lift;
- a near-complete battalion contribution of roughly 750–850 effective at Santo if the Raider gate fired.

Those three statements cannot all remain true at once.

Historical Marine Raider material states that the original APDs had troop compartments for about **130 embarked Marines per ship**. Four such APDs therefore provide about **520 normal troop spaces**, not 800–900. The same official Raider history later describes about 200 effectives as roughly one quarter of the 1st Raiders' original strength, consistent with an approximately 800-man original battalion rather than a four-APD lift capacity.

## 2. Corrected physical lift

Named APDs retained:
- Little (APD-4)
- Colhoun (APD-2)
- Gregory (APD-3)
- McKean (APD-5)

One-lift troop capacity for event use:
- nominal: **~520 physical** total;
- working band with minor loading variation: **500–540 physical**;
- do not create a fifth/sixth APD or a free conventional transport solely to move the remainder.

The Santo fast-lift package is therefore a **Raider forward combat detachment**, not the whole battalion.

## 3. Composition rule

Default center for the four-APD lift:
- forward battalion command/communications/medical element;
- three reinforced rifle-company equivalents;
- selected machine-gun, 60-mm mortar, demolition and pioneer elements;
- light ammunition and rations appropriate to the short emergency movement.

Default left in New Caledonia:
- approximately 300–380 physical Raiders;
- remaining rifle/rear-echelon personnel;
- bulky stores and the 81-mm mortar element where APD loading is the limiting factor.

Vehicles remain **0** in the APD lift.

## 4. Santo H-hour correction

If the four APDs arrive and unload cleanly before H-hour, Raider contribution becomes:
- physical ashore: **500–540**;
- combat-effective: **460–510**, center ~490.

Therefore the prior v072 H2/H3 bands are superseded as follows before new event losses:
- **H2, timely 2d Marines + Raider fast detachment:** approximately **4,000–4,900 effective**, center ~4,450;
- **H3, H2 + separately successful Efate light battalion:** approximately **4,400–5,550 effective**.

The resident-only, late-2d-Marines and 2d-Marines-only H0/H0.5/H1 bands remain unchanged.

## 5. Historical provenance guard

Use the Marine Corps official Raider history only as a hardware/organization capacity anchor. Historical Watchtower orders, sailing decisions and outcomes after Branch divergence remain provenance only.
