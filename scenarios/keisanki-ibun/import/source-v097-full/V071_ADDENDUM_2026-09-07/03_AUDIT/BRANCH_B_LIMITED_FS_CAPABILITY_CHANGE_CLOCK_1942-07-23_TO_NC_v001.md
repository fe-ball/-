# Branch B — Limited-FS capability change clock, 1942-07-23 → New Caledonia v001

Status: **CLOSED GATES / EVENT OUTCOMES OPEN**

A future capability contributes only after its gate passes **and after** combat loss, maintenance, fuel, aircrew and shipping costs are carried forward. This is not a list of free reinforcements.

## 1. D-4 to Santo D-day

- **RO-33/RO-34:** useful submarine geometry can rise from 4–5 at D-4 to 6–7 at D-day if arrival/repositioning succeeds.
- **Shokaku:** final work-up can naturally recover roughly 0–4 additional MR aircraft above the 52 D-4 center, but the whole three-deck package is still bounded by the 135–150 MR working band unless an explicit reserve transfer is posted.
- **Allied clock rises at the same time:** B-17 elements enter New Caledonia; after Japanese movement is reacquired, 2d Marines can divert; Turtle Bay construction continues unless suppressed. Waiting is not a free Japanese repair bonus.

## 2. Surface repair releases

- **Hiei — late July:** becomes a fast-BB physical reserve after repair/trials. It is not retroactively in the Santo core; NC employment needs an explicit order, fuel and screen.
- **Arashi — July:** can return as one DD reserve after repair/trials. v070 did not need it to pass the Santo gate.
- **Chokai — late August:** returns from MI heavy damage; too late for immediate Santo/early-NC center use.
- **Kongo and Hagikaze:** their MI repair costs are already embedded in D-4 availability. Do not add another readiness bonus.

## 3. Carrier release / substitution clock

- **Hiyo:** near-term physical completion/work-up path. Zero Santo credit. Even after physical completion it needs acceptance, trained air group and machinery/deck-cycle work-up; treat as a later support reserve, not an instant first-line carrier.
- **Hiryu:** hull/flight-deck repair runs roughly late July–August, then air-group work-up. No Santo credit and normally zero immediate early-NC credit. Yamaguchi/personnel survival improves the later regeneration ceiling, not the repair clock itself.
- **Soryu:** heavier August–September repair, then work-up; later than Hiryu for practical combat return.
- **Replacement policy:** if Zuikaku/Shokaku is made unavailable before NC, Junyo is the first fleet-capacity substitute. If Ryujo/support capacity is lost, Zuiho is the first support-deck substitute. Shoho remains the last concentration choice while Southeast-area/base/convoy obligations persist. These are substitutions, not automatic additions.

## 4. Forward-base maturation

### Lunga

If construction is not heavily suppressed and materials/fuel remain available:
- roughly **30 Jul–3 Aug:** emergency recovery/ferry-strip threshold can be crossed;
- roughly **5–10 Aug:** a small 8–16-aircraft fighter/recon operational detachment becomes possible.

These are conditional thresholds, not a mature fighter base suddenly appearing on a calendar date.

### If Santo is captured

- **C+1 to C+2:** 4–8 water-aircraft can stage south if anchorage/tender/fuel/sea-state gates pass. They must be moved from an existing node or reserve, so northern search density falls accordingly.
- **C+2 to C+4:** emergency fighter/recon recovery becomes possible if a captured runway sector is usable or quickly repairable.
- **C+4 to C+7:** a small 8–16-aircraft local fighter/recon node can operate if fuel, ordnance, maintenance and C2 pass.
- **C+7 to C+14:** a 20–30-aircraft mixed local pool becomes possible if suppression and logistics permit.

Even then Santo is **not** a mini-Rabaul. Strategic fuel, heavy engine repair and large naval repair remain concentrated farther north.

## 5. Mandatory Santo → NC reset

Before deciding the NC start date, rebuild these ledgers from the actual Santo result:

- **Carrier air:** actual MR count and crew quality after sorties/losses; never reset to 143. Reserve decks replace losses unless a new concentration order is explicitly made.
- **Submarines:** the same 6–7 boats cannot remain indefinitely at full forward effectiveness. Immediate post-Santo center is **4–6 useful boats**; after replenishment/rotation, **5–7** can support NC if no losses. Any NC line above seven requires a newly named-hull allocation audit.
- **Surface:** fuel, torpedoes, bombardment ammunition, maintenance days and floatplane loss carry forward. Hiei/Arashi can offset attrition only after their gates.
- **Vehicles:** carry physical survivors and damage classes. Branch recovery can improve repairable returns in 24–72 h but cannot recreate destroyed/drowned/abandoned vehicles.
- **Engineers:** the 15th Independent Engineer elements building Santo cannot simultaneously appear in the NC first wave. They can be released only after Santo minimum runway/port/garrison tasks are handed over.
- **Ground:** Kawaguchi main body stays the main NC follow-on reserve, but its own vehicle/artillery/shipping wallet must be closed at the actual NC decision date. Do not clone the Santo hardware package.
- **Allied state:** re-audit New Caledonia at the actual post-Santo date. Allied air and ground preparation also improves with time.

## 6. Main nonlinear feedbacks during FS

MI left Japan with unusually thick veteran carrier-air and staff nuclei. FS can spend that advantage much faster than it spends airframes: a small number of lost strike leaders, navigators and maintenance/deck chiefs can reduce effective quality even when replacement aircraft arrive.

Conversely, if Shortland/Rekata/Tulagi are suppressed, carrier/shipboard aircraft have to absorb the missing search/recontact burden. If Santo is captured and its water/land aviation gates pass, some of that ISR and fixed-target burden can move ashore and free carrier sorties. These are shifts in mission allocation, not free force multipliers.
