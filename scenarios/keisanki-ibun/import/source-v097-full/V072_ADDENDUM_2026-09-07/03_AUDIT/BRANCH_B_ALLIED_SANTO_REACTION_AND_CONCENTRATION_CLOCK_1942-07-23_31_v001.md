### Wasp

1. obtain reliable target/route information;
2. divert the already combat-loaded 2d Marines;

4. concentrate PBY/B-17 search/strike while retaining a Central-Pacific heavy-bomber reserve;
5. use Wasp to preserve the convoy and exploit favorable contacts, not trade it automatically against multiple Japanese decks;
6. keep NC/Fiji/Samoa/Tonga/Efate/Hawaii fixed garrisons in place;
7. prepare the 1st Marine Division as the post-D-day second wave.
