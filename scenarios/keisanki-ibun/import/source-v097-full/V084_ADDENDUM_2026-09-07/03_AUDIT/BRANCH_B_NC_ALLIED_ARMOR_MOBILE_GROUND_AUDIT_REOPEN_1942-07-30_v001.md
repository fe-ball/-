## 3. Terrain implication

New Caledonia is not Guadalcanal.

Road corridors around Boulouparis–La Foa–Tontouta–Noumea permit more useful tank movement and counterattack, but:
- bridges/culverts and demolitions create chokepoints;
- plantations/jungle and narrow roads constrain mass;
- Japanese air/artillery can punish concentrated tank packets;
- coral/road conditions and fuel recovery still matter.

The 754th therefore has real operational value as a **mobile counterattack / road-blocking reserve**, not as an armored-division breakthrough force.
