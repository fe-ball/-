## 1. WORLD-LINE CONSTANTS — DO NOT DROP

### Modified MI and naval-personnel lineage
- Modified MI main carrier battle: 1942-06-10; Midway Japanese-held by 1942-06-15.
- Japan: Akagi/Kaga sunk; Soryu/Hiryu heavily damaged but survive; Zuikaku survives. United States: Saratoga/Enterprise/Hornet sunk. Wasp could not participate in MI.
- Yamamoto, Nagumo and Yamaguchi survive. Yamaguchi remains an important carrier-war command/training asset because Hiryu survives.
- Akagi/Kaga loss does **not** equal annihilation of their entire air personnel: a large attack force was airborne and survivors were diverted/recovered. Soryu/Hiryu and Shokaku/Zuikaku survival also preserves more carrier staffs and technical cadres than historical post-Midway Japan.
- Persistent Japanese advantage is therefore **institutional continuity / command bandwidth**, especially carrier-division staffs, flight officers, deck chiefs, maintenance leadership, strike leaders, navigators/observers and multi-seat crews. It is not a generic leadership multiplier.
- Experienced carrier personnel are deliberately concentrated in first-line organizations rather than spread evenly. Airframe replacement, carrier hull availability and veteran-aircrew regeneration are three separate ledgers.
- The United States suffered catastrophic 1942 carrier-hull and carrier-aviation cadre losses at MI, but Fletcher, Spruance, Mitscher, Fitch and Kinkaid survive center. U.S. industrial/training depth can regenerate middle layers faster than Japan; do not model the U.S. as command-decapitated.

### Japanese Army systemic capability
Use historical weapon lethality/armor unless a separate Branch technical file changes it. The systemic edge is mainly in making the same equipment available and synchronized more often:
- vehicles/AFV: better reliability, failure tracking, spare allocation, recovery and workshop return; no free gun/armor upgrade;
- artillery: better firing tables, meteorological/charge correction, survey/registration, barrel-wear and ammunition-lot control; less waste and more repeatable fire, but no perfect first-round/counterbattery effect;
- engineers/logistics: better road/bridge repair sequencing, rail scheduling, depot accounting, truck/tractor uptime, workshop throughput, load planning and recovery priority;
- command/planning: assumptions, transport capacity, fuel/ammunition, schedules and failure rates are compared more explicitly; engineers/logistics can document infeasible plans. This does not abolish political pressure, service rivalry or bad decisions.
- Accepted amphibious-process layer: same physical force can land first-day personnel/heavy equipment roughly 15–25% more efficiently, with fewer load/beach and machinery failures, often bringing usable combined-arms power ashore roughly 2–8 h earlier. This is throughput/timing, not extra lethality or cloned equipment.

### China / Five-Go strategic carry-forward
- Branch China effects are category-specific, not a blanket Japanese multiplier: Japanese equipment/maintenance/planning median is modestly better; earlier local advances worsen some Chinese industrial/technical evacuation; Nanjing-linked static security/logistics substitution improves gradually; Chongqing repair/heavy-equipment/transport/fiscal resilience is weaker, while manpower and geographic retreat depth remain large.
- Five-Go remains a 1943-spring preparation project. China/Manchuria Five-Go divisions are protected from NC diversion.
- Branch avoids the historical Guadalcanal/New-Guinea emergency-transfer/attrition chain. Relative to history, roughly 3–4 division-equivalents of continental/Korea strategic depth (not idle troops) remain available for China/Five-Go/contingency duties instead of being consumed southeast.
- If 8th Tank Regiment is sent to NC, it is physically subtracted from Five-Go/continental armor; no double counting.
