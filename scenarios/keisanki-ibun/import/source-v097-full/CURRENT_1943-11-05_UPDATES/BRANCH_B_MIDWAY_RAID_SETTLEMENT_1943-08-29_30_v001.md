# Branch B — Midway Fast-Carrier Raid Settlement, 1943-08-29/30 v001

U.S. force:
- Essex CV-9
- Yorktown CV-10
- Independence CVL-22
- no amphibious convoy

Japanese RED-alert defense:
- Zusei late Zero 24–30 physical class
- Raiden 10–14
- Kyofu 6–8
- recon/floatplanes and rotational H8K
- mature D2-ish base with strong W2+ water-air layer
- limited I-30-derived metric warning / calibration / base-C2 advantages

Battle result center:
- U.S. aircraft irreversible: 15–20.
- U.S. repairable damaged: ~20–30.
- Japan aircraft irreversible: 27–34.
- Japan repairable: ~10–16 class.
- no major U.S. hull damage.

Aircrew / crew effect:
- U.S. irreversible personnel roughly 14–20.
- Japan roughly 10–15.
- short-term flight-personnel disruption becomes roughly comparable when wounded / temporarily unavailable are included.

Base effect:
- land-air sortie generation after peak suppression ~15–25%.
- limited flying resumes ~24–36 h.
- coherent fighter operation ~48 h.
- broad long-range search mostly restored ~3–4 d.
- water-air/search never fully extinguished.

Strategic interpretation:
- U.S. tactical victory: new three-carrier/F6F system can suppress Midway.
- But Midway is not cheaply shut down; one-day raid buys only a short suppression window.
- Japan does not sortie the four-carrier core merely to rescue an outer node without amphibious fixation / fresh carrier track.
- Midway invasion becomes less attractive; Central-Pacific amphibious axis shifts toward Gilberts.
