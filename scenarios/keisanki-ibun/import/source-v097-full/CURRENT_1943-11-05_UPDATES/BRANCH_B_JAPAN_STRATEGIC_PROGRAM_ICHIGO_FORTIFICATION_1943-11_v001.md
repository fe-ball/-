# Branch B — 1943年11月 日本戦略二本立て
## 一号相当準備 / 太平洋内郭要塞化加速 v001

Status: CURRENT WORKING STRATEGIC PROGRAM
Clock: 1943-11-05 center

## 1. 現在の前提
- Five-Goは広元主要地区確保後、1943-11-03前後に攻勢作戦として終了。
- 漢中–広元突出部は2.3–2.8個師団級の能動圧力正面へ移行する。
- Midway / Gilberts / Wake raidにより、米高速空母の回転能力は推測ではなく実証済み。
- 太平洋内郭整備は1943年春から開始、8月には外郭/内郭二層化。したがって11月の措置は「絶対国防圏の開始」ではなく「既存要塞化の加速」。

## 2. 日本側の11月判断

### A. 中国
新しい大陸作戦のstaff studyを即開始。
中心はHunanであり、史実一号作戦のHonan phaseをそのまま再演しない。

目的候補:
1. Yueyang -> Changsha / Xiangtan
2. 14AF飛行場群の破壊/退去
3. rail/river corridor復旧
4. Hengyangは条件付き第二段階
5. Lingling / Guilin / Liuzhouは別承認
6. 成都/重慶への地上追撃なし

1943-11/12:
- staff study / route survey / stock plan / Nanjing rear substitution planning.

1944-01:
- formal policy approval中心。

1944-02/03:
- 10個師団級Hunan packageの移動・車両整備・砲工兵集中・Wuhan–Yueyang stock buildup.

1944-04:
- terminal launch gate.

現時点での発起見込み:
- broad: late Apr–first half May 1944.
- exact D-dayはまだ閉じない。

### B. 太平洋
Marianas / Palau / Truk / Boninsを高価値内郭として引き続き優先。
Wake / Marshalls / Midway / Santo / NC等の外郭は、警戒・航空・水上航空・潜水艦・遅滞機能を安く粘らせる。

11月加速項目:
- secondary revetment / dispersed aircraft parking
- fuel/ammunition subdivision
- runway repair stocks and plant
- backup radio/power/C2
- AA ammunition/fire-control readiness
- radar calibration / selected passive warning
- water-air ramps / fuel boats / cranes / service-point dispersal
- mines/demolition/wire/obstacles
- engineer plant and maintenance shops
- water/storage/sanitation
- alternate local routes and reserve positions

Do not pre-import post-GALVANIC lagoon-side all-around-defense hindsight or 1944 cave doctrine.

## 3. Resource separation

### Mostly-China resources
- infantry divisions
- horses / pack animals
- inland rail/wagon capacity
- Yangtze and internal river lift
- local/Nanjing labor
- existing China depots
- most divisional field artillery

### Mostly-Pacific resources
- ocean shipping
- naval base/airfield units
- marine/construction battalions
- seaplane support
- naval radar/communications
- island water/fuel systems
- mines/coastal defense stores

### Direct competition — strategic red zone
Both programs want:
- high-grade engineers
- bridge/airfield machinery
- AA guns and AA ammunition
- signal/radar specialists
- trucks
- aviation fuel
- precision machine tools/spares
- high-quality cement/steel
- merchant shipping for specialist cargo
- trained aviation-support personnel

Policy:
- China may use already-positioned Five-Go assets and inland infrastructure.
- New high-grade allocations after Nov are capped so the Pacific inner belt is not stripped.
- Pacific does not receive wholesale China divisions merely because U.S. raids occurred.

## 4. 一号相当 — mature target scale if preparation succeeds

Hunan Phase A:
- 10 infantry divisions + independent brigades.
- one named theater reserve division outside D-day assault count.
- direct field/operational personnel: 235k–260k.
- total operation-related Central-China burden incl. rail/depot/engineer/AA/signal: 320k–370k.
- vehicles: 6,800–8,200 physical/rotational, roughly 5,400–6,500 normally serviceable at launch.
- horses/pack/draught: 38k–46k.
- extra army artillery: 10–14 battalion-equivalents.
- extra engineer/bridging: 8–11 battalion-equivalents.
- railway repair: 2–3 regimental groups.
- working campaign stock by terminal gate: 175k–225k t in Wuhan–Yueyang system, with 55k–75k t rapidly drawable.
- eastern/Hunan aviation: 130–165 normally ready; short 48–72h surge 155–190 by borrowing limited western aircraft.
- western Hanzhong–Guangyuan aviation remains active and cannot be stripped for a long campaign.

Nanjing rear substitution:
- by spring 1944 target workload release about 3.0–3.8 Japanese division-equivalents theater-wide.
- this releases Japanese regulars from static/security work; it does not create equivalent assault divisions.

## 5. Pacific fortification outlook

Already in place:
- outer-base steady supply bill remains roughly 75–95 kt/month network-wide.
- inner-defense construction/stocking had risen into roughly 25–40 kt/month class by late summer/autumn if shipping gates permit.

Nov–Jan acceleration center:
- prioritize roughly 30–45 kt/month class of delivered specialist construction/defense cargo to Marianas/Palau/Truk/Bonins/western-Caroline inner nodes, with short convoy peaks above this possible.
- this is a working allocation envelope, not new national merchant tonnage.
- emphasis is plant, AA, repair, communications, fuel/ammo dispersion and aviation support before wholesale division movement.

Expected maturity trajectory:
- Nov 1943: inner nodes are prepared bases under construction, not 1944 cave fortresses.
- Jan 1944: materially improved dispersed parking, repair redundancy, stores, C2 and local obstacles.
- Feb–Mar 1944: enough time for selected terrain-linked defense positions and deeper secondary systems where geology/material permit.
- physical depth remains highly terrain-dependent: Saipan/Guam/Palau can deepen more than flat Tinian/atolls.

## 6. Interaction / ceiling

The two programs coexist because they draw on different large resource pools:
- 一号 consumes continental formations, horses, inland transport and accumulated China stocks.
- Pacific fortification consumes shipping, island construction and naval/base-service assets.

They collide in the red-zone specialties.

Therefore center result:
- No.1/Ichigo-equivalent remains plausible as a 10-division staged operation in spring 1944.
- Pacific inner-defense acceleration continues without waiting for No.1.
- Neither receives the unrestricted upper-bound resource package.
- Chengdu ground offensive is rejected partly because it would consume exactly the engineer/air/transport categories now needed for both No.1 and the Pacific inner belt.
- outer Pacific islands are not massively reinforced with divisions merely to respond to carrier raids.

## 7. Japanese strategic thesis, Nov 1943

China:
- Five-Go converts western China into a permanent pressure front.
- use Hanzhong/Guangyuan to fix Chinese divisions, fighters and Hump cargo.
- next decisive continental investment goes to Hunan/rail/airfield nodes, not Chengdu.

Pacific:
- accept that U.S. carriers can suppress outer nodes.
- make suppression expensive and temporary.
- move decisive construction depth inward before U.S. amphibious power reaches the Marianas/Palau line.
- preserve mobile fleet and carrier-air strength for a battle in which U.S. forces are fixed by an invasion.

This is not a choice between China and Pacific. It is a deliberate two-program strategy with shared scarce-category caps.
