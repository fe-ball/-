# MARSHALL 2 FEB SUBMARINE RECONTACT + SECOND SUPPRESSION AUDIT — 2026-08-29

## Scope
Advances only the WORKING Central-Pacific clock from 1944-02-02 09:30 to 16:30 Marshall local. CURRENT authority remains 1944-01-01.

## Submarine recontact
The 08:05-08:25 three-carrier visual is propagated as a moving corridor, not a datalink. One inner SenTaka gets a high-confidence hydrophone/periscope recontact before noon. Geometry is not good enough for a responsible carrier torpedo shot, so the boat reports instead. US destroyer/air ASW then breaks contact. Selected acoustic-decoy/high-underwater-mobility effects contribute to survival but do not grant immunity. A Zuiun/fast float-recon confirmation sortie refreshes the same track into early afternoon without penetrating CAP for exact hull identification.

## US second suppression
TF58 launches a second, staggered 184-204 strike/sweep cycle; Gilbert land-air contributes 42-58 relevant sorties. TG58.2 is locally delayed 15-25 minutes by the submarine alarm but the operation continues. Targets remain Taroa/Roi while Ebeye/Wotje/Mili/Jaluit water/air infrastructure and the previously under-attacked Eniwetok land-attack reservoir receive additional pressure.

## Carrier decision
Shokaku/Zuikaku/Hiryu do not launch in the afternoon. The repeated TG track is valuable enough to justify conditional eastward closure and dawn-strike preparation, but other TG positions and safe dusk recovery remain insufficient. Status becomes CONDITIONAL_GO_PREPARED_NO_LAUNCH. A fresh overnight/pre-dawn track is still mandatory.

## Historical anchors
Historical FLINTLOCK used repeated carrier attacks through the day and land-based TF57 support; fields were then kept unusable by continued air/surface pressure. Historical Japanese submarines in the area suffered severe US ASW attrition. These anchors constrain the Branch result but are not copied one-for-one because the Branch has a larger and more coherent submarine information network, water-air depth, warning, dispersal and different TF58 composition.
