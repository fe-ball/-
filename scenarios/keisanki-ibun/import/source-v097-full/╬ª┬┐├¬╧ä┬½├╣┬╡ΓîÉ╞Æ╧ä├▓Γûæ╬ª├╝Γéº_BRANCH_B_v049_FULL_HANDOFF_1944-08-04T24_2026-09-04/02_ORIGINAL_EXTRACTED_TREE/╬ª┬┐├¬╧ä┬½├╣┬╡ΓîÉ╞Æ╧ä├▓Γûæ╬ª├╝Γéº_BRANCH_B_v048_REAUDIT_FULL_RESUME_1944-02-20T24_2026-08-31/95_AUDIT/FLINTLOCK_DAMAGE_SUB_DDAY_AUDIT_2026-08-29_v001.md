- Cabot is an Independence-class CVL. Historical Independence's 20-Nov-1943 single aerial-torpedo hit flooded the after engine room and required months of repair, returning to Pearl only in July 1944. Branch Cabot's centered hit is deliberately farther aft/shaft-alley dominant and therefore lighter, but it still requires drydock/shaft alignment and is not a days-scale repair. Full fast-carrier return is bounded late-April to mid-May, center early May.

## Amphibious timing

It is not delayed beyond 6-Feb because ten fast decks remain, Intrepid returns, the assault force is already exposed at sea, and delay increases submarine/night-air risk.
