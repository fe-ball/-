This package applies the newly reopened pre-GALVANIC construction/hardening layer without importing post-GALVANIC defensive hindsight.

## What changed

1. The prior base-by-base air OOB contained a small arithmetic inconsistency. It is repaired in `...OOB...v002.json` without adding combat power.
2. Pass A holds the US suppression plan fixed and measures how much more of the Branch-B base system survives.
3. The old Independence torpedo hit remains centered: hardening increases launch persistence, not torpedo accuracy.
4. Pass B lets the US react to the surviving Marshall network and the Independence hit by allocating repeat suppression/CAP work.
5. Tarawa H-hour is then recalculated with better completed revetments, shelters, ammunition dispersion, communications and tank hide/recovery positions, while retaining the pre-GALVANIC south/west defensive bias and the severe Betio geometry ceiling.

## Main result

The largest immediate delta is not an unsuppressible Japanese air force. The US can still suppress the network, but it pays extra cycles. At Tarawa the net D-day opening fire system is more coherent: initial 87-LVT destroyed/mission-killed moves from v004's 12-16 to a working 14-19, while the 11/24 tide/lane advantage still prevents replication of historical 20-Nov mass reef wading.

This is large enough to reopen the 25-26 Nov cross-domain mission state. The 11/26 carrier battle result is therefore *not* carried forward automatically.
