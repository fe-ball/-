# CURRENT MODELING CONTRACT — Branch B re-audit 2026-08-31

This file is a **runtime guard**, not flavor text.

## 1. What the branch is trying to model

Branch B is allowed to accumulate large advantages where they arise naturally from its premises.  Do **not** artificially flatten compounded gains merely to keep the historical timeline visually close.  The principal genuinely anomalous starting technology is the early computing line; most later advantages should emerge from ordinary causal accumulation in design, quality control, statistics, logistics, maintenance, training, communications, operations research, recovery, and doctrine.

Compounding may create threshold effects.  It must also saturate at real bottlenecks: shipping, skilled crews, range, weather, geometry, sortie cycles, beach capacity, repair plant, communications bandwidth, ammunition, fuel, and command decision time.

There is **no future-knower**.  No participant may infer later historical lessons before they are observed in-branch.

## 2. Adversary behavior

The United States is an intelligent, adaptive adversary, not a fixed historical script and not a blind monster.  It adapts only to evidence it can actually observe.  Model separately:

observation -> recognition -> explanation -> decision -> order -> execution.

Each stage may be delayed or wrong.  U.S. commanders may overclaim suppression, misread causes, suffer communications friction, or respond by adding resources that create congestion.  The diagnostic GALVANIC Pass A fixed-US run is retained only as sensitivity evidence; it is **not an active alternate scenario**.

Japan is governed by the same rule.  Better sensor/C2, logistics, computing, or doctrine cannot produce omniscience.

## 3. Mandatory accounting separations

Always distinguish:

- physical count / serviceable / mission-ready / actually committed;
- existence / production / delivery / unit conversion / frontline-ready / local allocation;
- platform hardware / weapons / sensors / software-doctrine / crew quality / maintenance;
- hull location / air-group location / deck readiness / mission assignment;
- carrier deck loss or damage / fate of airborne aircraft;
- repair/recovery / new production;
- cue / contact / track / weapon-quality fix;
- reconnaissance layer / strike layer / CAP layer;
- runway aviation / independent water aviation;
- direct combat loss / campaign-out / short repair / recoverable damage.

Never duplicate a resource across theaters.  Every redeployment must subtract from a wallet somewhere.

## 4. Fortification means a base-defense system

Fortification is not only concrete.  Audit at least four layers:

1. physical defense: revetments, shelters, firepoints, obstacles, ammunition positions;
2. base persistence: runway repair, fuel, workshops, power, water, construction plant;
3. sensor/C2: radar, DF/RI, passive warning where traced, plot, visual posts, telephones/radio, backup power and spares;
4. mobile sensors: flying boats, floatplanes, Zuiun-type fast confirmation aircraft, submarines and other reconnaissance.

Open-top revetments reduce correlated fragment/blast losses; they do not make aircraft immune to direct hits.  Radar antennas are fragile; separated power/plot/comms/spares can nevertheless preserve warning or accelerate restoration.

## 5. Water aviation

Water aviation is an independent operational layer.  It is not erased merely because a runway is cratered, and it is not automatically double-counted inside fighter/attack totals.  Ramps, cranes, fuel, tenders, radio, lagoons, anchorages and maintenance are separate target systems.

## 6. Carrier/deck separation

Aircraft and decks are separable.  A damaged carrier does not automatically destroy aircraft already airborne.  Other decks may recover orphaned aircraft if range, fuel, deck state and procedures permit.  Secondary carriers may provide CAP rotation, search, ferry, recovery and limited secondary strike without being added mechanically to the first-line strike package.

## 7. Land vehicles and tanks

Reliability/QC/maintenance improvements chiefly change daily usable fractions, recovery, and repair reappearance unless a new weapon/platform is independently traced.  Do not create untraced tanks.  Better tank doctrine does not make a 47-mm gun magically defeat an M4 frontally.

## 8. Computing timeline guard

Do not back-project mature parametron computing into 1942-43.  Branch timing:

- 1932: EM-1 relay + magnetic drum and commissioned computing service begins;
- 1934-38: practical relay systems spread;
- 1939-41: sealed-reed/Type-2 era and broader military-industrial/statistical use;
- 1942: parametron concept begins;
- 1943: Type 2P hybrid / arithmetic-unit experimentation;
- 1944: Type 3 full-logic parametron.

Pre-1944 logistics/process gains come from a decade of commissioned computing, card/statistical workflow, standardization and operations-research-like practice—not mature parametron machines.

## 9. Historical reference rule

Historical OOB, doctrine and commander behavior are reference anchors.  Historical named losses, dates and ambushes do not survive automatically if upstream geometry, COMSEC, availability or force composition changed.  In particular, do not resurrect the rolled-back “Second Battle of Midway / U.S. retaking Midway in Sep 1943” in current Branch B.

Whenever an upstream threshold changes, reopen downstream events that materially depend on it.
