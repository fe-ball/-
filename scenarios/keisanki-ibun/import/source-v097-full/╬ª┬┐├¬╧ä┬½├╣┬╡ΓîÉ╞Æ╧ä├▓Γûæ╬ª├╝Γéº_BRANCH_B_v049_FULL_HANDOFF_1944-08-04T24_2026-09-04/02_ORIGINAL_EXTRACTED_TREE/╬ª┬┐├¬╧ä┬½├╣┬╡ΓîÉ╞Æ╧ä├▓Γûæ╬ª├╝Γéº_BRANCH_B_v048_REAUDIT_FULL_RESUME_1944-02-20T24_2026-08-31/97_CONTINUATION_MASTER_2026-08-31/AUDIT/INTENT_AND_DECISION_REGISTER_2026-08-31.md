# INTENT / DECISION REGISTER — why these repairs exist

## Branch intent

The objective is not to keep the alternate history close to real history.  The objective is to let Branch B's premises compound honestly while enforcing real bottlenecks and information limits.  Strong advantages are acceptable when they are earned by the branch.  Artificial nerfs are not.

## Decisions that must survive session resets

- **U.S. adaptation is always active.**  Historical behavior is a reference for likely decisions under observed conditions, not a fixed script.
- **No future knowledge.**  Tarawa lessons cannot be used before Tarawa; Kwajalein lessons cannot be used before Kwajalein.
- **Sensor/C2 is fortification.**  Radar, DF/RI, visual warning, plot, communications, backup power, spares and water-air reconnaissance are part of a base-defense system.
- **Water aviation is independent.**  Runway suppression does not erase lagoon reconnaissance; water-air assets must be targeted separately and counted without duplication.
- **Secondary carrier decks matter without becoming free first-strike aircraft.**  CAP rotation, search, recovery, ferry and cross-deck absorption can change thresholds.
- **Vehicle reliability affects availability/recovery, not unexplained inventory.**
- **Named historical losses are not inherited automatically.**  If COMSEC, OOB, warning, geometry or hull survival changed, reopen the event.
- **U.S. mass has conversion bottlenecks.**  More aircraft, LVT, DD or ammunition can create deck, beach, lane, fuel, ASW and command congestion.
- **Repair/recovery and production are separate wallets.**
- **Downstream reopening is mandatory after a material upstream threshold change.**

## Why the frontier moved backward

The package's original external frontier was 1944-06-15T24.  During continuation to 21 June, omitted water aviation, secondary-carrier/deck separation, tank recovery and especially pre-GALVANIC fortification/logistics/sensor effects were identified.  Correcting only June would have left the causal chain inconsistent.  The simulation was therefore deliberately rolled back to GALVANIC and replayed forward.  The new authoritative working frontier is 1944-02-20T24.

This rollback is a **quality improvement**, not lost work: the quarantined later states remain valuable sensitivity tests and OOB/procedure references.
