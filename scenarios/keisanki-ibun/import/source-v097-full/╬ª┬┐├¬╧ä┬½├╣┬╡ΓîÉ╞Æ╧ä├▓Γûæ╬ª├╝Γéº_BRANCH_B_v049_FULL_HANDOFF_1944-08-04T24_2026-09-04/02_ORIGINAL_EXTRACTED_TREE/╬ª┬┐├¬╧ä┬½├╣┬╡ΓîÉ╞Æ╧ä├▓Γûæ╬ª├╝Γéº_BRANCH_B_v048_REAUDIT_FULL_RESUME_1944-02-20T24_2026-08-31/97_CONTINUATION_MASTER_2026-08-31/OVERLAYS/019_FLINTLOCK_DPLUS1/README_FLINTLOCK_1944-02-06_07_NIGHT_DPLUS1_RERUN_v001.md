This package advances the current Branch-B FLINTLOCK frontier from 1944-02-06T24 through 1944-02-07T24. ## Key causal points

1. The Japanese do not return to an automatic all-in banzai. Tarawa learning favors bounded attacks against support nodes, but bombardment-fragmented C2 limits synchronization.
2. US forces now expect remanning and infiltration. Registered defensive fires, illumination, tanks held behind seams, and traffic metering prevent local penetrations from becoming operational breaks.
3. Added US material still creates queueing and radio/fire-request competition. D+1 improvements come mainly from better beach exits and strongpoint-reduction sequencing, not infinite simultaneous fire.
4. Namur ends later than the historical behavior reference because more defenders survive and local C2 is better; it does not become a multi-day fortress because terrain/depth and US combined-arms superiority still dominate.
5. Kwajalein remains the principal unresolved ground battle at 7-Feb T24.
