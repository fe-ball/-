## Centered corrections

1. **Soryu repair is much faster.** The new 26-Nov hit state is one bomb plus damaging near miss, propulsion/waterline intact. Centered line: limited deck work on 27 Nov, Truk/Akashi repair into early Dec, usable first-line hull/deck again during roughly 8-15 Dec. No home-yard absence through Feb is centered. Fleetwide strike-cadre losses remain.
2. **Marshall defense becomes a network problem.** Taroa/Wotje/Mili/Jaluit/Kwajalein-Eniwetok are joined by rear staging at Kusaie/Ponape, with Wake/Nauru as long-range side nodes and Truk as strategic regeneration. Staging reach does not equal simultaneous fighter concentration.
3. **Japanese GALVANIC learning is real but delayed and imperfect.** Simple observable lessons propagate in days; procedural lessons in weeks; physical changes require shipping/material/terrain. No post-Marianas cave network is retrojected.
4. **US learning is also real and finite.** Historical post-Tarawa recommendations already called for more recon, obstacle work, destroyers, landing craft and roughly 3x bombardment/ammunition. Branch US will act similarly, but extra bombardment, CAP, ASW, LVT, engineers and screens compete for ship-days, deck cycles, fuel, ammunition, landing lanes and command bandwidth.
5. **More force can create congestion.** On small atolls, airspace/spotting, reef lanes, beach exits, radio nets, logistics areas and destroyer roles become bottlenecks. Added material remains useful but has diminishing and sometimes negative marginal effect if tasking/deconfliction is not improved.
