# Electronic-warfare / radar operational derivatives 1942-44 v001

status: CURRENT_BRANCH_AUDIT_OVERLAY

## Principle
Do not model improved radar/RWR as upgraded boxes on unchanged bases and formations. Once tools become reliable enough, they change organization, readiness states, reconnaissance tasking, EMCON, battle preparation and maintenance. These are distinct causal stages and may compound when not double-counted.

## Contact-quality ladder
Adjudicate separately:
1. **EM WARNING** — an emitter exists/bearing sector; often no range or identity.
2. **BEARING HISTORY / BATTLE PREPARATION** — multiple bearings/time permit sector narrowing and stations/torpedoes/CAP readiness.
3. **SENSOR CONTACT** — own radar/visual/sonar echo/contact.
4. **TRACK** — stable range/bearing/motion estimate.
5. **WEAPON-QUALITY** — CAP/fire-control/torpedo/strike solution.

The side receiving first EM warning need not be the side forming first weapon-quality track. This replaces vague recurrent language such as 'US finds first.'

## 1943 Branch Japanese derivatives
### Passive emitter warning / emitter library
I-30's late-1942 return with Metox-class equipment accelerates existing Japanese work. By 1943H2 selected ships, submarines and major bases can use metric-band passive warning. It does not reliably cover all US microwave systems. A single bearing cannot identify a carrier.

### ESM-cued reconnaissance
Multiple passive reports plus RI/DF/traffic and Type2P correlation cue D4Y/seaplane/flying-boat/submarine confirmation. Value is narrower search sector / better recontact probability, not magical passive target fixes.

### Base air-defense cells
A-grade bases increasingly link radar, visual posts, DF, weather, plotting, fighter readiness states and AA alert. This permits layered readiness instead of continuous maximum CAP and can lower fuel/engine-hour/fatigue/noncombat-loss burdens. Do not add this again on top of already-accounted lower noncombat-loss assumptions without attribution review.

### Electronics field service
Priority bases/formations form radar/radio service detachments with common tubes/spares, test gear, calibration sheets, fault logs, backup power and exchange modules. Main effect is availability/recovery time, not peak range.

### RCM / Giman-shi
Historical Japanese chaff existed by 1943; Branch better emitter-band knowledge can improve timing/cut/raid geometry against compatible metric radar. Treat as minutes-scale friction/track splitting, not task-force radar blindness and not a defense against incompatible microwave sets.

### EMCON / radar survivability
Selected attacking formations can use passive-heavy approach and short active-search windows. Fixed bases instead emphasize redundant sets/power, physical separation, decoys and rapid repair. Do not force a single 'always radiate' or 'always silent' doctrine.

### Island sensor line
In archipelagos, islands can act as cheap pickets: visual posts, RI/DF, passive ESM and selected warning radar feeding a network. Do not automatically create a US-style destroyer radar-picket doctrine in 1943.

### Night surface battle
For selected trained cruiser/destroyer forces, passive bearing warning can buy battle-station time, tube orientation, lookout concentration and short own-radar range checks before visual/own-radar target-quality contact. US SG/CIC can still form the first weapon-quality track. This changes Japanese confidence in a **one-pass torpedo ambush + withdrawal**, not confidence in prolonged radar gun duels.

## Timing guard
- late 1942: I-30 artifacts arrive; research/comparison begins.
- 1943Q1: bench/prototype RWR/precision-tracking/decoy work.
- 1943Q2: limited selected operational ESM; A-base control cells begin; fixed precision-tracking trials.
- 1943Q3: ESM-cued search, field service, EMCON/RCM and priority-base integration become materially usable.
- GALVANIC 1943-11: these tools may affect warning/tasking/preparation, but no fleetwide centimetric RWR and no US-style CIC equivalence.
- I-8 returns 1943-12-21: tacit acoustic/radar test knowledge starts a new absorption cycle; large hardware changes do not appear by 1944-01-01.

## Mandatory battle-use rule
For any 1943H2+ event, ask whether the affected causal stage was EM warning, own detection, track, fire-control, interception, recovery or repair. Do not apply one radar percentage to the whole event.
