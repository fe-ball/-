## 作戦前提

5/27 一号相当開始、overall ~14 divisions。目的は四川征服ではなく、Huazhong→Hunan→Hengyang、条件付きGuilin/Liuzhou、rail/airfield/population-tax-control corridor。Pacific priorityにより既集積China resourcesは剥がさないが、新規可動high-quality naval air/fuel/engineers/comms/fleet fuelはPacificへ約2:1 marginal lean。Chinaにunlimited fresh top-grade resourceを与えない。

中国側は1943五号作戦から学習し、都市決戦へ主力を固定せず、bridge/river/hill exitで遅滞し、heavy guns/vehicles/commsを先に後退させる。日本側も撤退を「崩壊」と誤認し続けず、low POW / low abandoned guns / reconからplanned retreatを認識する。

## Changsha/Hengyang logistics geography

Changsha capture does not instantly create through railway. Closest old established Japanese forward air base is Pailiuchi/Yueyang area, with Wuhan as heavy rear air/logistics base. Changsha/Xiangtan are newly repaired forward nodes.

Hengyang is a major junction of Yuehan and Xianggui axes, plus Xiang River and roads. Capture means a large railhead/river-port/airfield/front logistics node, not immediate continental through traffic.

8/6 forward local transport state working bands:
- Wuhan→Changsha B～B+
- Changsha→Zhuzhou B
- Zhuzhou→Hengyang railway C+ (temporary bridge/slow/transfer)
- Xiang River bulk transport→Hengyang B-～B
- roads C+
- Hengyang city logistics B-, rapidly improving
- Hengyang→Lingling Xianggui line C or less/front axis
- Hengyang→Guangzhou Yuehan through line impossible while enemy holds intervening sector

Transport uses rail usable sections + river + trucks + repeated transshipment; improved Japanese logistics management reduces breakdown/confusion but cannot remove handling/bridge/air interdiction costs.

Value thresholds:
- Hengyang alone: major forward node; opponent network redundancy reduced
- +Lingling: short southern corridor, Hengyang becomes one step rearward
- +Guilin: first qualitative shift; Hunan→north Guangxi becomes one operational transport zone, Hengyang becomes true intermediate logistics center
- +Liuzhou: line becomes network with south/Nanning and northwest/Guizhou branches
- +Nanning / Guangdong-side connection and repair: tenuous continental military corridor toward Indochina; still low-capacity/high-security/air-interdiction vulnerable, not sea-lane replacement

Effect on Chinese network: capture of Hengyang turns a more redundant operational “mesh” into roughly two major western/southwestern corridors. Japan gains route-cutting leverage but also inherits more fronts and a longer line to guard.

## Hengyang approach / air-base logistics

Japan does not race two infantry divisions ahead of artillery/logistics. 6/19～24 forward elements advance while Changsha-Xiangtan-Zhuzhou logistics/air nodes are rebuilt. Japanese improvement is fewer breakdowns, intact package arrival, better staging—not magical march speed.

6/19～22 Japan conducts Hengyang/transport/eastern-airfield pressure. Allied irreversible8～12 vs Japan6～9 center; primary effect is forced evacuation/dispersal/repair/CAP, not aircraft massacre。

6/27 Japan physically holds most Hengyang airfield after previous Allied demolition. City remains contested. Airfield is already being repaired for ~40 days before final city surrender; 8/6 surrender primarily removes ground-fire/infiltration and preserves some urban infrastructure rather than handing over an intact pristine runway.

## Hengyang battle 6/28～8/6 forward adjudication

China city force ~18.5～20k center19.2k, somewhat above historical due orderly Changsha retreat and support personnel; does not pack all preserved Ninth War Zone strength into city. Exterior relief armies remain outside.

Japan separates inner siege and outer anti-relief forces. 68/116 divisions lead inner assault, additional division-level elements cover exterior. This reduces city assault mass but avoids relief breakthrough.

7/1 first major attack after artillery/engineer preparation; Japan makes limited gains, no breakthrough. 7/3 pauses not because artillery never arrived but because repeated infantry assault is poor cost-effectiveness.

7/4～18 deliberate siege: observation, approach trenches, selective artillery, air windows, supply interdiction. Fourteenth Air Force remains effective but eastern fuel clock becomes visible. Japan uses Guangdong/Guangxi demonstrations and continued Sichuan pressure as allocation taxes rather than assuming enemy fuel state omnisciently.

7/22 Chinese relief surge with 70～90 sortie-class air concentration and thunderstorm creates temporary gap; 250～400 men + 8～14t high-value supplies reach city, but full relief fails. Japan shifts outer ring to elastic blocking around roads/high ground rather than holding every kilometer.

7/24 onward Japanese 23rd Army demonstration becomes limited real movement from Guangdong, fixing 20～30k Chinese forces broadly, of which perhaps15～25k could otherwise plausibly reinforce Hengyang. It also fixes 15～25 aircraft-equivalent and fuel around Guangxi. This is a real operation with its own logistics cost, not free feint.

7/26～31 another Chinese relief attempt; some 100～180 men + 3～6t supply infiltrate, but breakthrough fails. Fourteenth Air Force hits Changsha-south transshipment 7/30, destroys/immobilizes ~100～160t fuel/ammo center and delays Japanese final bombardment ~1 day.

7/31 city effective 10～11k, food 3～4 days under severe ration + air drop, mortar/artillery ammunition critical, ground supply essentially cut. Fourteenth Air Force eastern sustained output 50～65%, one-day surge75～85% possible but next-day cost.

8/1～2 final Chinese relief effort with 80～95 sortie-class surge reaches5～7km but cannot link. 40～80 men +2～4t supplies infiltrate.

8/3 Japan final attack begins after replenishment. 8/3 night Chinese command debates breakout; Fang Xianjue refuses full command escape abandoning wounded but permits local units to break out if communications collapse.

8/4 defense I/O decomposes from one army system into isolated resistance nodes. 4/5 night 1,500～2,200 attempt local breakout; 700～1,100 reach friendly forces, another200～400 later join local forces/guerrillas, rest killed/wounded/captured/returned. Preservation has geographic cost: west defense thins.

8/5 city core breaks. Fang seeks ceasefire with treatment/safety conditions. Japan/Nanjing practical interest favors organized surrender and prisoner registration over continued costly annihilation, while retaining disarmament and command detention. Medical shortage means heavy-wounded mortality remains high.

**1944-08-06 06:00 Hengyang organized defense ends** in this local forward adjudication.

Final working center bands:

## 中国/日本/南京 8/6 local snapshot

Japan: confidence + caution. “Enemy will fight, but key points can still be taken; Guilin/Liuzhou can complete value of Changsha/Hengyang, Sichuan pursuit would overextend.” Learned to wait for guns, separate siege/outer relief, attack allocation/logistics, avoid one-day prestige assaults.

Chongqing: crisis + resistance confidence. Changsha proved force preservation, Hengyang proved willingness to fight and ability to impose cost, but also that fighting does not guarantee holding transport nodes. Priority shifts from city symbolism to preserving remaining transport network / Guangxi / Guizhou approaches / 14AF bases.

Nanjing: political high and institutional self-confidence. More officials see themselves as administering a real Chinese state, not merely a temporary occupation office. This strengthens Japanese rear administration but also creates longer-term demands for autonomy from Japanese military advisers.
