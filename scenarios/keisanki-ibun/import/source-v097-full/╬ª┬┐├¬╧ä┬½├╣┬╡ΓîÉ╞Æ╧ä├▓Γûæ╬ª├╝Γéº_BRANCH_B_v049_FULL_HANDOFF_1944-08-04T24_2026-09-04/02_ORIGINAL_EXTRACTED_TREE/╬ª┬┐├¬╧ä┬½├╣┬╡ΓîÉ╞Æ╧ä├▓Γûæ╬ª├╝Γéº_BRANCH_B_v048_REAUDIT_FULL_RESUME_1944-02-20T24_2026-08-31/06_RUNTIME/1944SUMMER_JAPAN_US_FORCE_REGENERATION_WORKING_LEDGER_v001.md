## Carrier-air rebuild constraint

Surviving core carrier aviation avoids historical 6/19 annihilation, but 6/16～18 loses roughly110～140 aircraft plus crews. Dive/torpedo aircraft losses have multi-seat crew implications. Machine replacement is easier than experienced flight-leader/navigation/strike-coordination replacement.

Branch aviation personnel system avoids historical extreme novice concentration and wasteful ferry use, allowing roughly30～50 usable carrier cells/month to rebuild in summer sensitivity. However accelerating Unryu/Amagi combat readiness requires experienced flight leaders, landing instructors, deck/maintenance cadres drawn from existing carriers. Rapidly bringing two new carriers to B-class can slow existing-five rebuild by ~20～30 aircraft/cells over Aug-Sep. Do not multiply hull count by constant air-wing quality.

## Ground vehicle preliminary direction

Historical Japanese ordinary truck output collapses late-war partly through material/shipping strain. Branch conditions support a provisional 1944 national military/ordinary truck production investigation band ~26k～32k/year, but NOT YET CANON. Upper values above this require engine/bearing/rubber/tooling audit.

For AFV, do not advance Chi-He/Chi-Nu/Chi-To merely because steel survives. Effects first appear in availability of trucks, artillery tractors, recovery/repair vehicles, spare parts and return-to-service of existing Type97/Type95/Chi-He. Canon ground vehicle reliability improvement under good maintenance can produce 72h effective reappearance cycle 1.3～1.6 historical for relevant formations.

## Three windows currently visible

1. **late Jul～early Aug: surface window**
   Japanese heavy/surface force repairs return faster than carrier-air integration; US fast-BB layer still recovering. Good only for conditional night/surface attack against divided landing/fire-support forces, not a free fleet victory.

2. **late Aug～mid Sep: best combined relative window**
   existing five-carrier air groups materially rebuilt; Unryu/Amagi approach limited combat utility; Yamato/Musashi/Kongo/Ibuki/DD stronger. US still superior, but this may be the best time for one coherent operation while retaining a later fleet.

3. **Oct onward: relative window begins closing**
   US damaged units return, new major hulls approach theater, Marianas base network matures, B-29 clock advances. Japan also adds Katsuragi/repairs/aircraft, but US and Marianas growth become steeper.
