史実1944年3月のBurma Area Armyは8個師団＋独立混成旅団級。史実のTwenty-Eighth Armyは54th/55th/2d Divisions、Fifteenth Armyは15th/31st/33d Divisions。North/East Burmaには18th/56thが存在する。

本枝では第2師団主力はNew Caledonia攻略・守備に投入され、1942H2 settlementで「中国へ自動返還しない」固定資産に指定。v015までにBurma再配置の記録はない。よって史実Burma OOBの第2師団を同時に数えることは禁止。

working interpretation:
- west/southwest Burma: historical OOBより1個師団軽い。
- central Burma: 15th/31st/33dはU-Go deep advanceを実施せず、史実Imphal型の消耗・崩壊を受けない。
- north/east Burma: 18th/56thを基礎にMyitkyina/Burma Road axisを維持。Myitkyina immediate forceは現行settlementの4,000～5,000を採用し、増援・航空警戒を別建て。
- 史実March 1944 Burma total strength約252,000相当から第2師団不在を差し引くと、現行枝のBurma Area Army総人員の初期working bandは概ね230,000～240,000。ただしこれはrear/air/LOCを含む総人員で、前線戦闘員数ではない。division-by-division ledgerで再確認する。

Existing scenario anchors:

For a China land campaign, sea lift is mainly the input gate into ports/coastal/river/rail nodes. Once stock is in China, the primary operational bottlenecks are rail wagons/locomotives, bridge repair, Yangtze/river lift, trucks, horses, engineers, aviation fuel and ammunition. Therefore “more merchant GRT” does not linearly permit a larger Hunan front.

## 7. Nanjing — rear substitution and conversion function

Historical 1943 US estimate: ~366,000 Nanking Government puppet troops in China, ~209,000 in Central China. US planners judged that by end-1944 such forces might relieve up to four Japanese divisions in North China and two in Central China, but only with Japanese nuclei remaining in strategic centers.

Current branch political doctrine already increases practical Nanjing authority for rail/warehouse/police/local administration/anti-guerrilla functions while restricting independent heavy-weapon field-army autonomy.

Therefore the useful metric is not total collaborationist headcount but Japanese static-duty release:
- working spring-1944 hypothesis: 2–4 Japanese division-equivalents of rear/security workload can plausibly be released compared with a thinner collaboration regime, subject to rail-node Japanese cadres and rapid-response reserves remaining.
- this does NOT create 2–4 extra Japanese assault divisions out of nothing; it changes how much existing Japanese regular manpower can leave rail/city/warehouse/anti-guerrilla duty.
- collaboration field forces may hold secondary sectors, protect lines and pursue guerrillas, but should not substitute for Japanese assault artillery, motorized logistics, high-risk breakthrough infantry or operational reserve.

Old retained forward branch later used a broad Nanjing/associated armed-apparatus band of 750,000–900,000 and independently credible field force 120,000–180,000. These later numbers are reference only and must not be back-copied wholesale to 1944-04-25; a spring snapshot must be derived separately.

## 8. Consequence for No.1 / Ichigo-equivalent geometry

Historical Ichi-Go combined northern Honan/rail objectives with Hunan–Kwangsi airfield/rail objectives and ultimately used 15 divisions. Current branch has already spent a major 1943 campaign on western-Honan/Guanzhong–Hanzhong–Guangyuan geography. This reduces the reason to replay the historical northern phase in the same form, but does not automatically mean the Peiping–Hankow through-line is fully secure; rail status must be audited separately.

Provisional scale gates, NOT YET SETTLED:
- 10–12 Japanese divisions: Changsha–Hengyang / airfield-denial and transport-node operation, with Guangxi follow-on deferred.
- 12–14 divisions: Changsha–Hengyang plus condition-based Lingling/Kweilin-Liuchow continuation, while preserving a real reserve and rail-repair echelon.
- 15+ divisions: requires explicit external transfer and extra rail/vehicle/engineer/air-fuel allocation; not justified merely because the historical operation used 15 divisions.

Current preference before detailed OOB is to use any Five-Go/Nanjing-created surplus first as sustainment margin, second echelon, rail/bridge repair, and rear-security substitution rather than automatically widening the frontage.
