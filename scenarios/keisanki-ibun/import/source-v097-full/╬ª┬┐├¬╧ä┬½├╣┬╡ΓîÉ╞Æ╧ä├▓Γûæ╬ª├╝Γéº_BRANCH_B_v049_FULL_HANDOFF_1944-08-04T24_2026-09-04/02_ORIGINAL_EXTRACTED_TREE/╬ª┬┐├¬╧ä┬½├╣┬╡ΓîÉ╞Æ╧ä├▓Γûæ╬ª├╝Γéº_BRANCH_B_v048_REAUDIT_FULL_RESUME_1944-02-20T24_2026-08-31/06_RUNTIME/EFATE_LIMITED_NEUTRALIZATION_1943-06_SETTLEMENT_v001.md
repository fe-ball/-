# Efate limited neutralization 1943-06 settlement v001

status: AUTHORITATIVE_CURRENT_BRANCH_SETTLEMENT
operation_window: 1943-06-08 to 1943-06-09

## 1. Strategic objective
Japan holds Port Moresby, Santo and New Caledonia. Efate is an exposed but important US air/naval node. Objective is **temporary neutralization**, not occupation and not a compulsory carrier duel: suppress air/naval output, damage fuel/comms/repair/PBY facilities, push Wasp away from the base-defense problem, and delay US tempo before new US carrier mass arrives.

Yamamoto remains Combined Fleet commander. The staff's improved calculation culture affects target selection and repetition discipline; Yamamoto's personality is not rewritten.

## 2. Japanese force
South Pacific first-line carrier force central:
- Shokaku, Zuikaku, Hiryu as active three-carrier force;
- Soryu hidden strategic reserve;
- Hiei, Kirishima;
- CA 4–5, CL 1–2, DD 14–16.

Kongo/Haruna are not assumed immediately available south after the Aleutian rotation.

Carrier air group central total 174–178:
- fighters 70–72: 54–60 branch A6M5-ish `瑞星後期零戦` + 12–18 older Zero;
- dive/recon 51–53: D4Y1-C recon 6, D4Y bomber trial 8–12, remainder D3A late;
- torpedo 51–53: B6N 18–24, remainder B5N late.

Do not back-port Kinsei Zero/A7M. D4Y bomber remains limited combat trial; D4Y1-C recon is safer.

## 3. US state
Fast carrier central: Wasp only. No F6F yet in this South Pacific June battle. Wasp ready air group roughly 62–65 F4F/SBD/TBF.

Efate land aviation ready ~175 central:
- fighters ~102: F4U 28, P-38 22, F4F 28, P-40/P-39 24;
- SBD 20, TBF 16, B-25/B-26 14, B-17/B-24 11, PBY 12.

Approximately two Sangamon-class CVEs operate 300–450nm to the southeast/rear as replacement/CAP/ASW/landing refuge, not as duel carriers; a third can be rear reserve. Efate has two SCR-270-ish early warning sites plus competent but imperfect plotting/fighter direction.

## 4. Geometry
Japan creates actual three-axis pressure:
- Santo northwest of Efate;
- Tontouta/New Caledonia southwest;
- carrier group east/ENE ~310–340nm.

Santo+New Caledonia alone do not literally flank Efate from both sides; the eastern carrier group completes the geometry. Carrier aircraft are held mainly against Wasp/mobile targets rather than wasted on fixed-base attack.

## 5. 8 June morning
05:25 Santo wave working central: 58 aircraft = fighters 34, G4M 16, D3A 8. Targets radar/comms/PBY ramp/fuel/parking/Quoin runway intersections.

05:40 Tontouta wave: 56 aircraft = fighters 30, G4M 16, D3A 10. Targets Vila/Quoin maintenance/fuel/power/comms.

Standing CAP ~14–16. First-wave practical warning 18–24 minutes; roughly 65–72 defenders can meet it after scramble/plotting. Morning aggregate:
- Japan land-based all-loss ~21–28 + 10–15 damaged;
- US air combat loss 13–19;
- US ground all-loss/major 18–27 + 15–25 repairable.

Efate retains many aircraft but operational output falls to roughly 55–60% after the morning.

US identifies ~3 Japanese carriers, fourth uncertain. Wasp remains 350–450nm southeast rather than entering the carrier trap. Japanese carrier strike GO conditions require fresh contact inside roughly 250–300nm; central Wasp contact is stale/outside threshold, so **no carrier strike**.

## 6. 8/9 June night bombardment
Two bombardment groups central:
- north: Hiei + 2 CA + CL + 7 DD toward Havannah/Quoin;
- south: Kirishima + 2–3 CA + CL + 7–8 DD toward Vila/Quoin.

Japanese objective: complete 30–40 minutes of bombardment, not sink US fast BBs. US objective: radar standoff denial without accepting a close Long Lance fight.

Central surface result:
- US DD 1 sunk/disabled, CL/CA 1 medium/large damage, DD 1–2 damaged;
- Japan CA 0–1 medium, DD 1 medium;
- no BB major damage;
- bombardment completion 65–80%.

9 June dawn Efate operational output 22–30%.

## 7. Withdrawal and restoration
9 June Japan uses only small repair-suppression attacks; no second full bombardment. US fast BBs block repetition rather than chase under Santo/carrier/sub threat. Yamamoto declares limited objective achieved and withdraws by afternoon.

Aggregate working central:
- Japanese air all-loss ~28–40 (central ~34), mostly base aviation; carrier force near-fresh;
- US all-loss/irrepairable ~36–55 (central ~45), but CVEs can ferry ~18–25 replacements quickly.

Efate restoration central:
- 9 Jun 22–30%;
- 10 Jun 30–40%;
- 11–12 Jun 45–55%;
- 14–15 Jun 60–70%;
- 18–20 Jun 75–85%;
- late June ~90%.

Thus no 4–6 week shutdown: severe inhibition about 4–6 days, below-full output about two weeks.

## 8. Strategic result
Japanese tactical/limited operational success; US strategic hold. Wasp survives. No compulsory carrier duel. Japan proves that Santo + New Caledonia + an eastern carrier group can force multi-axis defense choices, but also observes rapid US repair/replacement adaptation and avoids repeating the same pattern next day.
