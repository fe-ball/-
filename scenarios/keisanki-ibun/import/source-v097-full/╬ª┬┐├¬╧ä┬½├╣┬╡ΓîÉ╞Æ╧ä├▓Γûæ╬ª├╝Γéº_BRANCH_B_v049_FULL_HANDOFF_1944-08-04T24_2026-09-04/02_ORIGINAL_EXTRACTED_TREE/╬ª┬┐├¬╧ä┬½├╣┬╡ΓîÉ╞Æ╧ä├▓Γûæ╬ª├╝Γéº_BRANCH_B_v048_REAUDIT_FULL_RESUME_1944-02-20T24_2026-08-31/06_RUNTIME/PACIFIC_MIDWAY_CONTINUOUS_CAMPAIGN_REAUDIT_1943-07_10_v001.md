## Why this re-audit exists
The rollback removed the old Sep-1943 US Midway recapture. Treating Midway as a forgotten Japanese island then understated the information/logistics consequences for the entire Central Pacific campaign. Midway does not physically block the Pearl-Marshalls route, but it changes observability, submarine basing, patrol geometry and the cost of US operational concealment.

- garrison/base personnel: 5,800–6,800

Japan pays for this. Approximate opportunity cost during stockpiling: 3–5 merchant-hull equivalents of ship-days, ~1 tanker-equivalent rotation, 4–6 escort mission slots; some lower-priority South Pacific secondary construction is deferred.

## US response and its cost
Midway is bypassed rather than invaded in 1943 center branch because an assault would create a favorable Japanese carrier-battle gate. Johnston is expanded as an existing—not magically created—southern routing/patrol node. Incremental working cost: 4–7 kt base material, 150–250 specialist/Seabee personnel, 8–12 patrol aircraft reassigned, 4–6 submarines used for Midway-line surveillance/containment, 3–5 escort mission slots. The first draw comes from Pacific survivor/building buffers; Atlantic/OVERLORD is not charged until those buffers are exhausted.

## Aug 31-Sep 1 carrier raid
The historical Marcus raid geometry is no longer plausible as an unobserved direct Pearl-Marcus dash because it passes too close to Japanese Midway. Working replacement: a US 3-carrier Midway suppression raid, not an invasion.
- US aircraft irreversible: 9–14; damaged 12–20
- Japan aircraft irreversible: 18–26; damaged 8–14
- Japanese ground casualties: 150–250
- runway: limited flight activity restored in 36–60 h
- long-range warning/search substantially restored in 3–5 d
Operational lesson for both sides: Midway can be suppressed but not cheaply silenced for weeks; suppression itself is a warning signature.

Japanese intelligence still obtains high probability of a large carrier movement but sees no corresponding amphibious transport signature; Combined Fleet therefore avoids the historical-style major fleet sortie/fuel waste.
- Wake still suffers a successful heavy carrier raid; fixed-base function loss ~60–75% immediate band
- US aircraft irreversible/accident 24–32
- Japan aircraft irreversible 12–20
- no center fast-carrier hit; minor/medium escort damage remains sensitivity
- major Japanese gain: saved fleet fuel/ship-days and improved raid-vs-invasion classification

## Anti-error rules
1. Midway does not make all US movements visible; it raises detection probability and forces concealment cost.
2. US southward dogleg has small pure distance cost but nonzero infrastructure, patrol, escort, ASW, AO, deception and scheduling cost.
3. Japan cannot operate Midway at RED search rate continuously without burning stocks and readiness.
4. Suppression raids may warn Japan; US can create noise with periodic attacks, but those sorties and munitions are real costs.
5. Recalculate US submarine forward-basing density before Marianas; historical Midway basing cannot be copied.
