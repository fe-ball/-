# China Tank Regiment No.3 retro-audit — Hunan/Hengyang 1944 v001

status: PROVISIONAL FORWARD CANON PENDING GLOBAL SYNC

The Hunan force was previously under-modeled by omitting the Tank Regiment No.3-class tactical armored component. The 3rd Tank Division itself remains outside the Hunan D-Day package. Working launch model: regiment-class physical 55–70 tank/armored-vehicle class, center near a standard 68-tank regiment; launch ready roughly mid-50s.

Branch improvements do not turn Type 97/95 tanks into Sherman equivalents. They improve maintenance planning, recovery, spares, dispatchability, scheduled availability and coordination.

Operational effects:
- strongest on flat/road-axis rear-guard fights, road-junction seizure and short outer relief-force counterstrokes
- moderate around Changsha outskirts/airfields/rail areas
- weak at river crossings before heavy bridging, Yuelu terrain and dense/mined Hengyang city fighting
- city use in 3–5 tank packets with infantry/engineers/direct fire
- outer relief crises may use 8–12 tank rapid-response packets; the key effect can be qualitative by closing a time window before Chinese anti-tank/artillery synchronization

Retro loss effect is not uniform. Where tanks actually participate, Japanese infantry casualties may be roughly 3–8% lower. Whole 5/10–7/21 campaign correction center is roughly 700–1,100 fewer Japanese ground casualties than the earlier no-explicit-tank adjudication.

By 7/21 working center: irreversible tank loss 9–13; physical survivors low/mid-50s; immediate ready around 40; explicit army rapid-response reserve 8–12. High-priority logistics tax normal ~8–15 t/day, heavy movement/combat ~15–25 t/day, inside the existing theater flow but explicitly tracked.
