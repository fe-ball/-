## 1. Historical baseline and branch divergence

Historical January 1944 China Expeditionary Army baseline:
- 24 infantry divisions + 1 armored division + 11 independent mixed brigades + cavalry brigade.
- 11th Army at Wuhan: 3d, 13th, 34th, 39th, 40th, 58th, 68th, 116th Divisions + 17th IMB.
- 13th Army lower Yangtze: 22d, 60th, 61st, 64th, 65th, 70th.
- North China contained 37th, 62d, 69th, 32d, 35th, 59th, 63d, 110th etc.; Mongolia Garrison had 26th + 3d Armored.

Historical Ichi-Go preparation then:
- sent 32d and 35th Divisions out to the Pacific;
- returned 27th Division from Kwantung Army to China;
- shifted 64th and other formations internally;
- mid-May Changsha-Hengyang 11th Army field OOB centered on 9 divisions: 3d, 13th, 27th, 34th, 40th, 58th, 64th, 68th, 116th plus independent brigades.
- historical full operation used roughly 400,000 men, 70,000 horses and 12,000 vehicles over the long campaign.

### Branch-specific Five-Go retention

Legacy/current scenario evidence explicitly used the **36th, 37th, 41st, 27th and 17th Divisions** as the five-division Phase-II Five-Go group in June-August 1943.

In actual history:
- 41st Division was transferred from China to the Southeast Area/New Guinea before this period.
- 36th Division later transferred from China to the Southwest/New Guinea area in late 1943.
- 17th Division assembled for and moved from China to the Southeast Area/New Britain in late 1943.
- 27th moved from North China to Manchuria in mid-1943 and was returned for Ichi-Go in 1944.

The current branch cannot both use 36/41/17 through Five-Go and also automatically place them in their historical Pacific destinations. No current-branch record transfers them out after the limited Guangyuan Phase III-A.

**Anti-double-count rule:** at 1944-04-25 the 36th, 41st and 17th Divisions remain China-theater assets unless a later explicit transfer is adjudicated.

Working China-area branch count after retaining the historical 32d/35th Pacific transfer and adding the three Five-Go-retained divisions:
- **26 infantry divisions + 1 armored division-equivalent headquarters** in the China-area order of battle.
- This is a scenario OOB count, not a claim of 27 full-strength assault divisions.

## 8. Forward logistics gate

### Before launch

Wuhan–Hankou:
- heavy depot, repair, air and command base.
- holds the deep reserve rather than pushing everything to Yueyang.

Yueyang/Pailiuchi/Xinqiang system:
- forward stock and air-support zone.
- pre-launch stock is deliberately distributed, not one giant depot.

Working accumulated campaign stock available inside Wuhan→Yueyang system by early May:
- **175,000–225,000 t** of food/fodder, ammunition, POL, engineer/rail, medical and general operational stores.
- immediately forward/rapidly drawable portion: **55,000–75,000 t**.

This is not all consumed in the first month.

### Throughput behavior

Wuhan→Yueyang combined rear-feed:
- sufficient for a 10-division staged operation while the route is intact and traffic is dispersed.
- 14AF interdiction forces night movement, repair and convoy dispersion but does not sever the system before launch.

Beyond the start line:
- stockpiles can carry the first 7–10 days of high-tempo movement.
- as distance grows, the assault must rely on opening river/road nodes and repairing rail.
- at Changsha/Xiangtan the key metric becomes not "how many divisions exist" but how many can be supplied simultaneously southward.

### Hengyang continuation gate

Do not auto-continue with all ten divisions.

A deliberate Changsha→Hengyang continuation requires:
- Changsha/Xiangtan secured enough to function as logistics nodes;
- rail/bridge repair giving at least a limited continuous flow;
- Xiang River night/bulk movement remaining usable;
- forward delivered combat-supply flow center **~650–850 t/day** for the designated leading force, with short higher bursts from stocks;
- at least 70% of planned truck serviceability after the Changsha phase;
- army-level artillery ammunition reserve above the emergency floor;
- Fourteenth Air Force interdiction not imposing a multi-day transport collapse.

If this gate fails, pause/rebuild rather than turn numerical superiority into an underfed Hengyang assault.
