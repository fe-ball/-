## 1. Historical comparison that matters

史実1944年4月のFourteenth Air Force / CACWは、日本の北方攻勢に対してLiang-shan、En-shih、Nan-cheng (Hanchung/Hanzhong)、An-kang、Sianを前進基地網として使用・準備した。現行枝ではFive-GoによってSian/Xi'anとHanzhong/Nanchengそのものが日本側であり、Baojiも日本側。したがって史実なら米中側が使えた北方forward-airfield ladderの主要二段以上が逆向きに日本側の成都・重慶圧迫へ使われる。

この差分を無視して、Fourteenth Air ForceがHunan/Yunnan/Sichuan間を史実同等以上に自由転用できるとはしない。

## 2. Geography / distance implications

straight-line working distances:
- Guangyuan -> Chengdu ~267 km
- Hanzhong -> Chengdu ~394 km
- Hanzhong -> Ankang ~192 km
- Xi'an -> Ankang ~184 km
- Hanzhong -> Liangshan/Liangping ~276 km
- Hanzhong -> Enshi ~389 km

意味:
- Guangyuanは成都圏への常時戦闘機掃討基地には整備余力が細いが、recon / nuisance raid / light-bomber / staged fighter sweepには十分近い。
- Hanzhongは重整備・燃料・作図を持つ主節点として、Guangyuanで使った航空機を再生できる。
- Ankangは米中側の安全なforward baseではなく、Xi'an/Hanzhongの双方から圧迫されるstaging stripとなる。
- Liangshan/Enshiは残るが、北方作戦へのtransit distanceが増え、Hunan/Yunnanとの兼務費用が上がる。

## 3. Japanese-held western airfield network — Apr 25 working grade

### Xi'an
- grade: A rear/staging/plotting node
- permanent combat parking: 25–40 class
- short surge/staging: 70–90 class
- role: heavy repair handoff, reserve fighters, route warning, Ankang/Hanzhong reinforcement

### Baoji
- grade: B+ intermediate logistics/staging
- permanent: 20–30
- surge: 40–50
- role: Hanzhong feeder, emergency diversion, fuel/ammunition buffering

### Hanzhong / Nancheng
- grade: A- western combat-support node
- permanent: 45–60
- surge for 48–72 h: 70–85
- role: fighter/recon command, medium repair, warning/plotting, western stockpile
- inbound road: 600–750 t/day normal, favorable near 800 retained anchor

### Guangyuan
- grade: B forward aviation/recon node
- permanent: 20–30
- surge: 35–45
- role: recon, fighter staging, light-bomber/direct-cooperation, night/nuisance attack
- heavy engine/airframe repair returns to Hanzhong

### Hanzhong -> Guangyuan route refresh
After ~half a year of consolidation, working normal delivered flow is 430–520 t/day, dry-weather short surge 550–600; early monsoon degradation can return to ~300–400. This is enough for a 2–3 division-scale defensive/pressure salient plus a bounded aviation package, but not enough to support a fresh deep Chengdu offensive simultaneously with a major Hunan campaign.

## 4. Japanese western air package

Apr-25 physical aircraft assigned/regularly available to the Xi'an–Baoji–Hanzhong–Guangyuan western system:
- physical 90–115
- normal mission-ready 68–86

mission-ready composition working band:
- fighters/interceptors Ki-44/Ki-61/Ki-84: 38–48
- Ki-45 heavy/night fighters: 6–8
- Ki-48 / Ki-51 light bomber-direct cooperation: 14–18
- Ki-46 and other reconnaissance: 6–9
- other liaison/utility: 4–6
- Navy long-range fighter cooperation: episodic 6–10 aircraft when explicitly allocated; not permanent local strength

Do not read the Ki-84 line as all-new elite units. A minority of western fighters can be Ki-84; Ki-44/Ki-61 remain major working types and pilot quality is mixed.

Sustainable active-weather output:
- routine western combat/recon: 35–50 sorties/day
- 1-day surge: 60–80 sorties
- repeated 60+ sortie days require drawing fuel, spares and pilots from Hunan/Hankou or accepting falling readiness.

Typical pattern:
- daily/near-daily Ki-46 reconnaissance when weather permits
- 4–10 aircraft nuisance/night/early-morning attacks on exposed fields and fuel dumps
- 8–16 fighter sweep/intercept packages
- every ~7–10 days, 20–35 aircraft concentrated strike/sweep if intelligence and weather justify it
- main targets: Chengdu B-29 satellite construction/stockpile, Liangshan/Enshi staging, Ankang, road/bridge nodes, exposed aviation fuel and repair concentrations

Strategic bombing of Sichuan cities is not the main purpose. The purpose is to force dispersal, CAP, runway repair, warning, fuel movement and replacement flow.

## 5. Ground pressure from the salient

The retained 2–3 division-scale force is not a passive garrison.

working distribution:
- Hanzhong / route / rear security: ~0.7–0.9 division-equivalent plus logistics
- Guangyuan / Jianmen approaches forward hold: ~1.1–1.4 division-equivalent
- mobile reserve / patrol / counter-infiltration: ~0.5–0.7 division-equivalent

Total active salient commitment: ~2.3–2.8 division-equivalents, roughly 45,000–58,000 all-arms personnel including attached support and route/security elements. Exact divisional identities remain for the China OOB ledger.

Operational behavior:
- no automatic advance on Chengdu
- battalion-scale probes / ambush-clearance / local ridge seizure every 5–10 days where useful
- regiment-scale limited action at roughly monthly frequency only when weather/logistics/intelligence justify it
- artillery and air-ground cooperation used to threaten the Jiange/Jianmen–Mianyang approaches and force Chinese reserves to stay north

Chinese consequence:
- 5–7 effective division-equivalents, often 6–9 nominal Chinese divisions plus corps/rear elements, must remain tied to north-Sichuan defense/route protection instead of being freely moved to Hunan or Yunnan.
- the exact Chinese OOB is not settled here; this is a fixation requirement, not a claim that every pinned division is first-line effective.

## 6. Allied/Fourteenth Air Force allocation consequence

Historical Fourteenth Air Force already operated under a Hump/fuel constraint. In this branch, losing Sian and Hanchung as Allied forward fields worsens the geometry while Japanese Hanzhong/Guangyuan pressure creates a permanent western task.

Apr-25 working fighter allocation consequence:
- 70–95 fighter aircraft physically committed/held in Sichuan-western defense and nearby rotation
- 50–70 normally mission-ready
- additional 35–50 replacement/allocation aircraft can be tied in the India→China ferry/replacement pipeline or kept from moving east as rapidly as a no-pressure case
- 10–16 medium/heavy/recon aircraft-equivalents are normally tasked to western reconnaissance, counter-air, supply/ground support, or contingency work

This does not imply the Fourteenth Air Force is crippled. It retains the ability to surge aircraft east. The constraint is duration.

Eastward diversion from the western defense:
- 1 day: +25–35 fighters can be surged east with acceptable risk
- 3–5 days: +15–25 fighters can be sustained only by drawing down western CAP/maintenance reserve
- >5 days: maintaining the same eastward reinforcement means accepting a materially higher probability of Japanese strikes on Chengdu-area fields, Ankang/Liangshan staging, fuel dumps and B-29 support construction

Therefore a Hunan battle can receive a strong one-day Fourteenth Air Force concentration, but not a cost-free continuous maximum while Yunnan/Burma and Sichuan remain active.

## 7. Hump / logistics tax

Historical April 1944 Hump lift was only about 11,000 tons/month and the first half of 1944 was already dominated by Fourteenth Air Force and Matterhorn demands. Current branch western pressure adds a high-priority allocation tax rather than claiming that US national production is exhausted.

Working additional monthly China-bound high-priority burden caused by Hanzhong/Guangyuan pressure:
- aviation fuel / defensive flying / dispersal: 260–420 t
- fighter/bomber spares, tires, filters, guns/ammunition, radio/radar, specialist equipment: 90–150 t
- runway/repair/AA/communications emergency material attributable to raids and dispersal: 70–120 t equivalent
- total additional Hump/high-priority burden: 450–700 t/month, center ~560

Some construction materials are local and not all fuel is literally carried in the same form over the Hump; the ledger is an opportunity-cost equivalent for high-priority China delivery.

At April lift this is ~4–6% of gross monthly Hump tonnage, but a much larger fraction of discretionary high-value aviation cargo. It competes directly with:
- Fourteenth Air Force eastern fuel/ammunition buildup
- Y-Force equipment and air-support stock
- Matterhorn stockpile growth
- replacement aircraft parts / special tools / engines

Matterhorn remains operable. The no-pressure potential stockpile growth is reduced by roughly 10–15% center, consistent with the retained CBI working settlement.

## 8. Direct attrition is secondary to allocation

Working monthly western air-combat loss sensitivity, not yet a locked event ledger:
- Japan irreversible aircraft: ~10–15/month under active pressure
- US/Chinese irreversible aircraft: ~6–10/month
- additional Allied long-repair/unavailable: ~8–15/month

Direct aircraft/personnel exchange remains generally Allied-favorable. Japanese advantage is the local allocation/clock tax produced by geography: each surviving Allied fighter in Sichuan still consumes fuel, spares, alert hours and airfield capacity and is not simultaneously over Hunan.

## 9. Reciprocal Japanese constraint

The western front also prevents the Japanese Fifth Air Army from becoming artificially free.

To keep the pressure credible, Japan must leave roughly 60–80 mission-ready aircraft-equivalents in the western system during the preparatory period. It can temporarily lend 20–30 fighters and 8–12 strike/recon aircraft toward Hankou/Yueyang for a 48–72 h surge, but a longer withdrawal makes the western threat visibly hollow and allows Allied fighters to move east.

Therefore the No.1/Ichigo-equivalent operation cannot simultaneously assume:
1. full-strength persistent Hanzhong/Guangyuan pressure,
2. maximum historical-style Fifth Air Army concentration over Hunan,
3. no additional fuel/maintenance/logistics cost.

At least one must be relaxed.

## 10. Consequence for No.1-equivalent scale accounting

This changes how division counts should be read.

The 2–3 division-scale Hanzhong/Guangyuan force is already part of the active strategic operation against China even if it does not march on Changsha. Its air/ground pressure fixes Chinese troops and Allied aircraft.

Thus a plausible future count is:
- NEW Hunan assault package: ~10–12 Japanese divisions if the goal is Changsha–Hengyang with a genuine reserve
- PLUS existing western active pressure: ~2–3 division-equivalents
- theater-wide active pressure therefore ~12–15 division-equivalents before Burma and coastal security are counted

A new 12–14 division Hunan assault package would mean ~14–17 active division-equivalents once Hanzhong/Guangyuan is counted and would require explicit proof in the China OOB, engineer, rail, vehicle and aviation-fuel ledgers. It is no longer the default merely because a later retained branch used ~14 divisions.

This audit does not yet set the operation at 10–12 divisions. It narrows the question: count the western active front first, then determine how many genuinely mobile China Expeditionary Army divisions remain for Hunan.
