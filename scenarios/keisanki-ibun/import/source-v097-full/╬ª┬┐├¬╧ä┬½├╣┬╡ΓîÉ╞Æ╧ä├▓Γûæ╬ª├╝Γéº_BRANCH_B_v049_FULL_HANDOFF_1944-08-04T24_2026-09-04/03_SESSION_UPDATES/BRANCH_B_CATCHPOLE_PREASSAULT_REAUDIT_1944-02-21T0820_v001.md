## 1. Governing interpretation

- The archived `2.5–4.0 h passage delay` is treated as **delay versus a clean/no-problem plan**, not as an additional 2.5–4.0 h on top of the historical 1944 execution.
- Historical execution already incurred about two hours because discovery of the Wide Passage minefield caused Deep Entrance to be swept before the Northern Group entered.
- Therefore Branch-B's incremental delay versus historical execution is centered at roughly **+0.5 to +2.0 h**, with about +1.25 h as the working center.
- The 20 Feb date is closer to the 24 Feb new moon than the historical 17 Feb preliminary day. Stronger spring-tide tendency and Deep Entrance currents can increase handling friction, but no exact 1944 harmonic tide table has been closed; do not manufacture a precise tide height/time.

## 10. Defense-model classification

**GREEN:** Branch Engebi is a more complete, better dispersed, better maintained version of the historical February-1944 Eniwetok defense system.

**Explicitly NOT:** Peleliu-style defense in depth. No reverse-slope main battle system, deep cave network, multi-layer ridge geometry or July-1944 doctrine is imported.
