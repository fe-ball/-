# Current Master Timeline — 1943-06-30 to 1943-11-21T10:20

- Attu organized defense ended by planned Japanese withdrawal rather than annihilation; 5,050–5,450 Japanese personnel recovered in June.
- Kiska evacuation completed by ~20 Jun; US has not immediately confirmed it.
- China Five-Go Phase II continues toward Hanzhong.
- Background-war audit restores South Pacific air/base contest, Indian Ocean submarine/recon pressure, US submarine commerce war, NC east remnant closeout, Central Pacific buildup.
