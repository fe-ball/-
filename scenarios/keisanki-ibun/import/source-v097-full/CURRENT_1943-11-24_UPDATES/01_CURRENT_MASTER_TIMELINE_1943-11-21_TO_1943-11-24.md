# Current master timeline — 1943-11-21 to 1943-11-24

## 21 Nov 1943
- 05:55: Japanese four-carrier strike launches 140 aircraft against separated US carrier group.
- Strike results retained: Essex bomb2+torpedo1 campaign mission-kill; Belleau Wood bomb1+torpedo1 heavy mission-kill; Monterey bomb1 temporary limitation.
- Japan: 38 aircraft irreversible, 17–23 damaged return, 55–70 flying personnel irreversible/MIA; four carrier hulls intact.
- 10:20: Japanese force completes recovery and turns W/NW.
- US countersearch launches 18–24 aircraft. Because remaining healthy carrier groups are ~330–400 nmi from opening Japanese force and range increases as Japan withdraws, no weapon-quality carrier contact by 13:00. Full counterstrike NO-GO.
- Tarawa daylight: US bridgehead stabilizes and expands but Branch Japanese C2 survives; evening Japan retains ~2,800–3,100 organized personnel in then-working line.
- Makin daylight: R3 reinforcement from prior night delays US combined-arms timetable; evening Japan retains ~700–850 organized personnel.
- 21/22 night: second Makin R2 relief force is prepared and advances conditionally, but ISR finds no verified corridor through strengthened US SG screen. Abort before terminal approach. No Type93 salvo. Tarawa R1 not yet delivered.

## 22 Nov
- Morning ISR: Japan confirms US assault fleet persists; estimates 4–7 combat-capable fast carrier decks, center 5–6; fast BB presence remains. US collapse not supported; schedule disruption supported.
- Tarawa daylight: US combined-arms attack takes Green/airfield sectors gradually. Evening Japanese organized strength ~1,650–1,950; Shibazaki C2 intact; 2–3 Type95 tanks ready.
- Makin daylight: East Tank Barrier broken ~14:15 center; evening Japanese organized strength ~300–430.
- 22/23 night: no strategic relief. Tarawa conducts limited 220–300-man distributed counteractions/infiltration, not historical terminal banzai; Makin only delaying infiltration. Tarawa R1 cargo prepared but delivery window not found.

## 23 Nov
- Makin: organized C2 ends 13:30–15:00 center; isolated groups remain. Makin support forces begin release clock but remain through evening for sweep/ASW/reorg.
- Tarawa: full day combined-arms battle continues. Evening US controls ~75–82% stable area; Japan retains ~750–950 organized personnel under Shibazaki. R1 high-value resupply now has highest marginal utility but delivery window nearly expires.
- 23/24 night: Tarawa R1 conducted by long-range night aircraft. 2–3t dropped, 0.8–1.4t recovered in Japanese-held eastern sector. Cargo emphasizes radio batteries, medical stores, mortar/MG ammunition, explosives.
- I-175 operates Makin area. Historical Liscome Bay success is not inherited: adapted US ASW/screen/zigzag reduces firing geometry. I-175 launches but scores no hit and escapes. Makin CVE group remains on ASW alert into morning.

## 24 Nov
- 07:00 onward: Tarawa final organized defense compressed in eastern Betio.
- R1 supplies delay communications/ammunition collapse by several hours but cannot solve geography/firepower deficit.
- 13:00–14:00: Shibazaki command net ceases effective island-wide control.
- 16:00–17:30: organized Japanese resistance on Betio ends. 80–150 isolated holdouts remain.
- 18:00: Gilbert major organized ground campaign considered closed; operational focus shifts to fleet regrouping, CVE/BB release, carrier-air regeneration and Marshall defense.
