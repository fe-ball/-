## 1. Governing accounting rules

1. British countermeasures are charged at **gross cost first**. Existing dual-use, poor machinery state, or hypothetical alternative use is not discounted at booking time.
2. **Full cost does not imply full tactical effect.** A requested patrol, carrier slot, or escort commitment is charged even if serviceability, detection geometry, weather, or absence of contact prevents it from producing a kill.
3. An unaffordable bill is not erased. It must reappear as a delay, uncovered route, reduced sortie rate, other-theater withdrawal, equipment wear, or blood.
4. Same hull on the same day is never booked twice.
5. No named counterfactual merchant loss is created without a defensible traffic/contact roster.
6. Historical outcomes already generated under historical countermeasures are not re-rolled merely because the same countermeasure exists in Branch B.
