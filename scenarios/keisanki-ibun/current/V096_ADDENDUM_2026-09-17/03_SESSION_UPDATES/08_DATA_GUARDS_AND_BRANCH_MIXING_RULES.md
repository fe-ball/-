# DATA GUARDS / BRANCH MIXING RULES — v096

1. v095 and all earlier authority remains inherited unless v096 explicitly supersedes it.
2. Old embedded 1944 working files are not automatically authority. They can supply structure, historical anchors, or sensitivity only.
3. A value becomes current for post-v095 chronology only if it appears in v096 authority/update files or is clearly inherited from clean canonical references.
4. Physical / serviceable / mission-ready / crewed must remain separate.
5. Permanent loss / long mission-kill / temporary mission limitation / host-unavailable must remain separate.
6. Ground personnel: support/construction/aviation-base personnel are not silently converted into first-line infantry.
7. Fortification: warning x labor x delivered material x plant x terrain x enemy suppression; no instant caves.
8. Armor: no unit appears without a traced allocation and transport path.
9. Aircraft: existence != local allocation != serviceable != sortie-ready.
10. Radar: Branch gains are mostly reliability/calibration/I-O; do not grant US-CIC-equivalent simultaneous multi-raid management.
11. Submarines: Penang gains reduce friction; do not grant guaranteed cm-wave warning, snorkel, or universal German equipment.
12. Hump: logistics tax means extra burden / delayed free stockpile growth, not automatic total Hump collapse.
13. Europe: reserve erosion / flow delay must be counted before extra casualty claims.
14. US Guadalcanal-series historical losses avoided in Branch are a real offset when computing net historical Allied loss.
15. Next session must close ground deployment before advancing major Marianas/Ichi-Go outcome clocks.
