# TECH INTEGRATION AUDIT — 1944-02-25 baseline, propagated through Apr

## 1. Governing principle

The main Branch difference by 1944 is not universal future weapons. It is faster integration of existing technology into repeatable mission systems.

Use five maturity dimensions:
1. existence/design;
2. production/reliability;
3. unit adoption;
4. I/O integration;
5. operational institutionalization.

## 2. Aircraft

- Late Zui-sei Zero: A, quantitative carrier-fighter backbone.
- Kinsei Zero Kai: A-/B+, expanding; high-speed CAP/escort, not wholesale replacement.
- Raiden: A land interceptor.
- Kyofu: A-specialized water-air local defense.
- Shiden-Kai: initial operational cells by Apr, still limited.
- Retsufu: prototype/trials, combat0.
- D4Y/B6N: A primary carrier strike types.
- Zuiun: B+/A-specialized fast recon/contact/spotting.
- Ki-44/Ki-61: mature Army fighters.
- Ki-84: first operational sentai Nov43, rapid 1944 expansion; front-line ready250–290 by late Apr work line.
- Ki-67/Ki-100: no normal combat credit by Apr.
- B7A: limited Taiho high-value cell only, 8–14 ready Apr25.

## 3. Radar / fighter direction

Physical radar improvements are modest:
- practical range +5–15%;
- unit-to-unit variance -30–40%;
- initial uptime relative +15–25%;
- scale/calibration error -30–50%.

The larger difference is process:
- timestamped reports;
- track numbering;
- smoothing/range-rate;
- anomaly rejection;
- radar-range + optical-bearing fusion;
- sector/altitude vectoring;
- re-vector after first interception.

US SG/CIC/fighter-direction/VT/5in38 systemic advantage remains, especially in simultaneous multi-raid management.

## 4. Shipboard electronics

No universal electronic replacement of mechanical directors.

Large ships:
- mechanical fire-control solves relative motion;
- electronic auxiliaries handle residual corrections (met, MV, barrel wear, propellant temp, lot/individual-gun differences);
- second generation extends into radar track and tactical display.

By mission:
- Yamato/Musashi: full large auxiliary computing + optical/radar I/O;
- Shokaku/Zuikaku: retrofitted air-warning/plot/CAP direction;
- Hiryu/Soryu: similar but less electrical/space margin;
- Taiho: first carrier built to integrate 1943–44 power/wiring/ventilation/plot philosophy from design stage;
- Akizuki: target assignment/director handoff emphasis;
- normal DD: radar + simple plot + voice/electrical transmission;
- kaibokan: ASW handoff/navigation priority.

## 5. Communications / ISR

- Stronger cipher/COMSEC delays content recovery but does not erase traffic analysis/HFDF.
- Better units reduce transmission count, use short formatted reports and thicker preplanned orders.
- Photo ISR standardizes interval/overlap/scale/mosaicking/coordinates/previous-image comparison.
- Intelligence confidence uses CONFIRMED / CORRELATED / SINGLE-SOURCE / INFERENCE and freshness decay.

## 6. Penang/Sabang submarine technical system

By Feb44:
- joint IJN/German plot/deconfliction is operational;
- trim/load recalculation and crash-dive test for special-cargo missions becomes standard after I-34 lessons;
- self-noise mapping and slow/relisten procedures mature;
- German radar-warning/hydrophone/electric-torpedo hardware is comparison material;
- meter-wave warning is useful, cm-wave remains a major hole;
- snorkel not standard;
- aircraft/jet technology from later I-29 return is NOT backdated.

Penang dividend is not a double-kill-rate multiplier. It is lower friction in patrol allocation, communications, maintenance and technical learning.

## 7. ASW

- MAD is localizer, not wide-area finder.
- layered cycle: broad fixed-wing search -> datum -> MAD local confirmation -> air/surface attack -> shore handoff.
- Apr MAD aircraft25–40 / 3–5 detachments work band.

## 8. Island defense

Mature A-level by Feb/Mar:
- dispersal;
- alternate C2;
- ammo/water/telephone redundancy;
- all-round/LVT-aware fire plans;
- water-air treated separately from runways;
- small-unit night infiltration/local counterattack.

New B-level after Kwajalein:
- deep tunnels;
- reverse slope;
- underground fuel/C2/ammo;
- hiding from naval gunfire rather than merely surviving it at the beach.

Construction time is mandatory. Marianas/Palau only begin to realize this in Mar–Apr.
