## Japanese understanding of U.S. methods
HIGH:
- F6F is fast, robust, well-armed and dangerous in high-speed/vertical combat.
- U.S. fighters use mutual support / boom-and-zoom.
- mass fighter sweep precedes attack waves.
- radar fighter direction is highly effective.
- U.S. carrier deck cycle supports repeated waves.
- U.S. fleet AA and damage control are strong.

MEDIUM:
- exact CIC/task-group information-fusion depth;
- exact F6F altitude/envelope numbers.

Japan knows the effect of unusually effective AA better than the mechanism.

## Anti-radar / approach doctrine
Branch does not gain radar invisibility.

Permitted/established:
- low-altitude radar-horizon approach;
- sea-clutter exploitation where geometry/weather permits;
- late climb only for dive-attack elements;
- multi-axis groups converging within a narrow time window;
- dusk/night attack;
- cloud/squall routing;
- radio silence / preplanned EMCON;
- limited chaff/Window based on prior combat experience;
- high-altitude high-speed one-pass reconnaissance for suitable aircraft.

Purpose:
reduce warning/processing time and overload CIC choices, not erase U.S. radar/CIC advantage.
